## Question and decision

Object: joint generalized zero-difference constraints under exchange/shift relations.

Prediction: joint counts depart from both the matched PRP and products of marginal counts.

If this prediction survives the specified independent controls, extend the exact constraint model for this mechanism. If its falsification condition holds, revise the observable before attempting larger AES variants. Retain per-key replication and complete costs separately from any claim of scientific novelty. Acceptance: independent predicate implementation, exact toy counts and complete query/memory accounting.

## Proposed discriminating experiment

retrieve complete algorithms, then full 16-bit toy domain on <=8 keys and <=2^18 full-byte structures/key.

Controls:

- all marginals and intersections
- lazy PRP
- random S-box with same mixing
- one relation broken at a time
- degenerate quartets excluded

Falsification: joint excess vanishes with disjoint, nondegenerate structures or matches nulls.

Lossy-projection obligation: find equal retained relations with distinct post-layer relations before assuming deterministic closure.
