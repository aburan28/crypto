Part E: why a_corn/n was falsified (2026-06-29) but nu_hat is not
==============================================================================
  a_corn is the Cornacchia/Eisenstein 'a' -- i.e. |u|, the RADIAL
  part of pi.  But |pi| = sqrt(n) always, so u alone is a lossy proxy
  for theta: u = sqrt(n)*cos(theta) + (v/2), and a_corn/n ~ n^-1/2 for
  every curve.  The information lives in the ANGLE, and it is only
  visible after the anisotropic weighting diag(tau,1) -- an isotropic
  invariant of the ideal cannot see it, because ALL Eisenstein ideals
  are similar as lattices (class number 1, hexagonal shape).

  Demonstration: 8 curves, same n, different roots/angles (tau=20.0)
            n      u      v   a_corn/n  theta(deg)    nu_hat
       524341   -405   -836   0.000772     31.0287   0.41550
       524347    593   -214   0.000408     45.1708   0.86814
       524353    121   -656   0.000231      8.3206   0.77426
       524389   -313   -828   0.000597     38.0174   0.91629
       524413    601    804   0.000387     14.0499   0.80650
       524497   -343   -832   0.000654     35.7851   0.97182
       524509    823    540   0.000540     40.2201   0.61684
       524521   -189   -800   0.000360     46.9382   0.84426

  a_corn/n is pinned to a ~n^-1/2 sliver for every curve; theta and
  hence nu_hat range over the whole interval.  Same underlying datum,
  different coordinate -- which is exactly why one separates and the
  other does not.
