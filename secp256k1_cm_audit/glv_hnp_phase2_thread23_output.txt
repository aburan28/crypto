EXP U1: S_D sweep -- is lambda_1 = n*S_D*e_m really unavoidable?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

linearly in S_D'.  But ||n*S_D*e_m|| = n*S_D scales fully, while only

   ||pv||^2 = (d*S_D)^2 + C^2,   C^2 = sum of the k1/k2/kannan blocks.
