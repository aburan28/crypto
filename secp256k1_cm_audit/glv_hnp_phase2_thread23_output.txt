EXP E4: control — sweep S_D in the OLD lattice
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 2026-07-29 diagnosis was that the trivial vector n*S_D*e_m (norm
n*S_D) crowds out the planted vector (norm ~ n*sqrt(2m/3+4/3)).  Both
scale linearly in S_D *only if d ~ n*S_D dominates the planted norm*; at
moderate S_D the planted norm grows more slowly, so if that vector were
the binding constraint, S_D in 2..8 should help.  It does not (see below),
which is the same conclusion E3 reaches by a different route.
