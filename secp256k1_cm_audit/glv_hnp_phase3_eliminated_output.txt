EXP E5: what kind of wall is it?  info-theoretic vs unique-SVP gap
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 2026-07-29 falsifier offered a dichotomy: either the reformulation
moves the wall, or 'the wall is information-theoretic'.  E3 shows the wall
does not move -- but the second horn is testable too, and is WRONG.
  m_thresh = ceil(log n / log(1/eff))  is the information-theoretic bound
             (unknown entropy < constraint entropy).
  ||w||/GH  is the unique-SVP gap; recovery needs it below ~1.
Planted norms use E[x^2] = n^2/3 per bounded coordinate.

12-bit/2677:  n=2647, K2=52, m=10  (m_thresh << m throughout)

Reading: at K1=6 and K1=8 the information-theoretic bound is m_thresh = 4 and 5
while m = 10 -- twice the data needed -- yet recovery is 0/5.  So the wall is
NOT information-theoretic.  The unique-SVP gap crosses 1.0 between K1=3 (0.94)
and K1=4 (1.08), bracketing the observed 5/5 -> 0/5 transition at K1=4 -> 6.
Phase 3 improves the gap by only 3.5-5% (gap3/gap2 in [0.950, 1.003]), because
it trades one dimension for a factor n in the determinant and these nearly
cancel:  gap3/gap2 = (n*eff^m)^(-1/((2m+1)(2m+2))) -> ~0.97 at m=10.
A 3-5% gap gain cannot move a K1 grid whose steps are 33-50% apart, which is
exactly why E3/E4 are identical in 46 of 46 cells.

EXP E6: is the eff wall structural, or just a shortage of signatures?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E5 says the wall tracks the uniqueSVP gap, and the gap SHRINKS with m:
    gap(m) ~ (n * eff^m)^(1/(2m+1)) * C   ->   sqrt(eff)*C  as m -> inf.
So for any fixed eff there should be an m past which recovery works.
2026-07-26 called the eff=0.157 failure 'structural'; 2026-07-29 T4b
probed only to m=32 -- but on n=2647, where K1*K2 = 416 distinct k values
means m=32 is already deep into birthday collisions AND the model puts the
crossing near m~78.  Re-run on a 20-bit curve where neither problem bites.
