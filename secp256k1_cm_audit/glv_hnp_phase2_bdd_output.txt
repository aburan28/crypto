EXP E5: Gaussian-heuristic crossing -- is the K1 wall a geometry wall?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For the E2 lattice (dim D = 2m+1) compute
    GH(L) = sqrt(D/(2*pi*e)) * det(L)^(1/D)
and the ratio  gamma = ||v_planted|| / GH(L).  gamma < 1 means the
planted vector is (heuristically) unique-shortest-ish and a good
reduction should find it; gamma > 1 means exponentially many lattice
vectors are shorter than the target and NO amount of reduction helps.
