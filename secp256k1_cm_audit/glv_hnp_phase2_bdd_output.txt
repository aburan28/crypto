T23-A: does S_D scaling remove the trivial vector n*S_D*e_m?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2026-07-29 T5 claimed: 'no choice of S_D removes it - both vectors scale
linearly in S_D.'  Only the trivial vector scales fully:
  ||n*S_D*e_m||^2 = n^2 * S_D^2
  ||v_planted||^2 ~ n^2 * (2m/3 + S_D^2/3 + 1)   (only ONE coord has S_D)
  => trivial is longer once S_D^2/1 > 2m/3 + S_D^2/3 + 1, i.e. S_D > sqrt(m+3/2)
The cost is only det^(1/dim): S_D^(1/(2m+2)).
