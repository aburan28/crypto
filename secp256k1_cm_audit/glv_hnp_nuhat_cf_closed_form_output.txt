     Contrast with E5: a_scale -- the quotient at the index

     though E4 shows that index is within +-1 of j* about 97% of
     the time.  Adjacent partial quotients are independent, so an
     off-by-one index reads an unrelated number.

     Corrected explanation for the June failures: the obstruction
     is not scale-freeness per se and not arity -- it is that j*
     cannot be identified without performing the minimisation.
     The O(log n) CF minimisation IS the cheapest sufficient
     statistic; no closed-form CF summary short-circuits it.
