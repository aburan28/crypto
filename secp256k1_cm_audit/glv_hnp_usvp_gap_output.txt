Closed-form check: nu_cf is computed from (n, m, K1, K2) alone; nu_gh uses the
exact integer det and the exact planted norm of seed 42.
nu_proj is the gap in the projection orthogonal to the rank-(m+1)
nuisance sublattice T = <n*S_D*e_m> + <rival block shortest vectors>.
T is primitive in L (index [sat(T):T] = 1, verified exactly by HNF on
the 8-bit and 12-bit/2557 anchors), so det(pi(L)) = det(L)/det(T) holds
with equality, not as a heuristic.
