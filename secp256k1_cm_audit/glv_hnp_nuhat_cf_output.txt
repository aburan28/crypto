reading: the 2026-07-29 guess q_j* ~ sqrt(n*K2/K1) is the right SCALE but
is biased.  q* is systematically about 2.4x SMALLER than that balance
point (median log2 ratio ~ -1.25, stable across 32..256 bits), and only
~40% of instances land within a factor 2.  So sqrt(n*K2/K1) locates the
winning convergent to within a couple of CF indices but does NOT pin it:
lambda_1 must still be taken as the min over all q_j.  That min is the
closed form -- it is exact and costs O(log n).

Note the entries are ~ (n*S_K1)^2 ~ n^4/K1^2; the f64 conversion inside
gauss_reduce_2d overflows once that exceeds ~1.8e308.  This is the same
class of failure as the original Thread 1 P-521 LLL NaN.  The CF form
never converts a large integer to f64: the minimisation is pure integer
arithmetic and the final ratio is taken with math.isqrt at 1e-20.
