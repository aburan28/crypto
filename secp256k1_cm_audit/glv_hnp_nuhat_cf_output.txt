PART C -- nu_hat^2 >= 2c and the role of the critical partial quotient
==============================================================================
nu_hat^2 = x^2 + y^2  with  x*y = c = q*eta/n,  so nu_hat >= sqrt(2c).
c = 1/(q_{j+1}/q_j + eta_{j+1}/eta_j), so c is small exactly when the
NEXT partial quotient a_{j*+1} is large.  Hence: small nu_hat <=> a large
partial quotient at the critical scale.

  sample: 200 20-bit j=0 CM curves, K1=72, K2=isqrt(n)+1
  relative gap between nu_hat and its bound sqrt(2c):
      median=0.1048   q90=0.3644   max=0.8733
