EXP H -- consequence of CLAIM 1: the eff-optimised nu_hat, and why max_a
         *nearly* worked in June
==============================================================================
For a FIXED winning convergent (q_j, delta_j), CLAIM 1 gives
    nu_hat^2 = (Q*delta_j/n)^2 + (q_j/Q)^2,
a function of Q alone.  Minimising over Q:
    Q_opt^2 = n*q_j/delta_j,   nu_hat_min^2 = 2*q_j*delta_j/n.
With K2 = sqrt(n) and K1 = eff*sqrt(n) we have Q^2 = n/eff, so
    eff_opt = delta_j / q_j     (feasible iff delta_j <= q_j),
    nu_hat_min = sqrt(2 * min_j q_j*delta_j / n).
Since q_j*delta_j/n ~ q_j/q_{j+1} ~ 1/a_{j+1},
    nu_hat_min ~ sqrt(2 / max_a).
So max_a DOES govern nu_hat -- but only after optimising eff.  At the
FIXED eff of every June experiment, the relevant quotient is a_straddle,
not max_a.  That is the precise sense in which the June invariant was
the right quantity measured at the wrong scale.
