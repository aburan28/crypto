================================================================
CONCLUSION
================================================================
All 15 product classes E_i x E_j of secp256k1's sextic twists contain a
genus-2 Jacobian, whereas only 5/15 are Howe (2,2)-glueable (Thread 18).
So counting Howe-glueable pairs UNDERCOUNTS the available genus-2 covers
by a factor of 3 for this family.

This does NOT weaken the paper's block-B5 conclusion. B5 bounds the COST of
the resulting genus-2 DLP, not the NUMBER of covers: for a Jacobian J with
#J ~ p^2 ~ n^2 whose group order is divisible by n, Pollard rho on J costs
~sqrt(#J) >= sqrt(n) and index calculus on a genus-2 Jacobian over a PRIME
field has no sub-rho algorithm (Gaudry-Thome-Theriault-Diem applies to
g >= 3 or to extension fields). Tripling the number of covers multiplies the
attacker's options by a constant, not the asymptotic cost.

What WOULD matter, and is not settled here: whether any of the 10 newly
admissible classes is realized by a Jacobian that is EXPLICITLY CONSTRUCTIBLE
over F_p. Thread 2 showed the F_p Rosenhain route is blocked by the cubic
residue obstruction; realization is an existence statement and gives no
construction.
