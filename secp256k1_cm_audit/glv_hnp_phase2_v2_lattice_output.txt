EXP H: is r = ||planted|| / lambda_1(L2) the recovery criterion?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
L2 is the ONLY competing short family left in V2, and it encodes a real
ambiguity: (k1,k2) -> (k1 - lam*t mod n, k2 + t) leaves k_full fixed and
is excluded only by the bounds.  So r < 1 should mean 'planted is
lambda_1' and r > 1 should mean 'a bounded-looking decoy exists'.

  n instances = 240   success = 109   fail = 131
  r | success : min 0.552  med 1.201  max 3.042
  r | failure : min 0.776  med 1.509  max 3.132
  AUC (lower r = success) = 0.612
  best threshold r <= 0.857: accuracy 71.2% (majority baseline 54.6%)

  Stratified by eff (eff is the known dominant variable — an honest
  test of r must hold eff fixed):
      eff  succ  fail  AUC(r)  AUC(1/r)  AUC(nu)
     0.05    60     0     nan       nan      nan
     0.10    32    28   0.243     0.757    0.812
     0.15    13    47   0.026     0.974    0.979
     0.25     4    56   0.246     0.754    0.857

  AUC(r) < 0.5 means the naive reading is BACKWARDS: within fixed eff,
  a LARGER ||planted||/lambda_1(L2) predicts SUCCESS.  Since ||planted||
  is ~constant at fixed eff, r varies through 1/lambda_1(L2), so this is
  the same signal as nu_hat = lambda_1(L2)/sqrt(det L2) with the sign
  reported in commit e845207 (low nu_hat -> high success).  AUC(nu) is
  printed for the direct comparison.
