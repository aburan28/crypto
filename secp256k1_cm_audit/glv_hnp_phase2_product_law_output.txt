EXP Y6: what PI < 1 actually says
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PI = NU*nu_hat < 1  <=>  NU < 1/nu_hat.  Nearest-plane is
guaranteed only for NU < 1 (a theorem).  So 1/nu_hat is the factor
by which Kannan-LLL empirically RELAXES the nearest-plane bound,
and it is the skewness sqrt(det L2)/lambda_1(L2) of the lambda-block.

nu_hat over all 3000: min 0.2244  median 0.7369  max 1.0665
Hermite bound for rank 2, (4/3)^(1/4) = 1.0746  -> violations: 0
relaxation factor 1/nu_hat: min 0.9377  median 1.3585  90th pct 2.0700  max 4.4560

The cases that matter: NU > 1 (no nearest-plane guarantee) but PI < 1.
