CEILING for secp256k1 (n = order of secp256k1, K2 = sqrt(n) = 2^128):
  tau < 1 requires eff = K1*K2/n < 0.17565,
  i.e. K1 < 0.17565 * 2^128 = 2^125.49.
  Unbiased GLV gives K1 = K2 = 2^128, eff = 1.  The attack therefore needs
  log2(1/0.17565) = 2.51 bits of combined bias in (k1,k2)
  before the planted vector is even expected to be lambda_1 -- and LLL/BKZ
  need more than that.  This is Phase 2's ceiling, in bits.
