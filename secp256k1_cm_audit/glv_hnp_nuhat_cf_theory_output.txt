PART G (corollary): why max_a is a one-sided CONSTRAINT, not a predictor
==============================================================================
Each term of the T23-B min obeys y^2/x^2 + x^2 >= 2y (AM-GM, equality at
x = sqrt(y)), so nu_hat^2 >= 2*min_j y_j.  Classically
y_j = q_j*eta_j/n >= 1/(a_{j+1}+2), hence

      nu_hat >= sqrt( 2 / (max_a + 2) ) .

So a small nu_hat REQUIRES a large partial quotient somewhere in the CF
of lam/n -- necessary, not sufficient, because the large a_j must ALSO
land near the scale.  That is exactly the shape of a variable that
correlates weakly (~ -0.2, Part D) and fails as a classifier (June).
