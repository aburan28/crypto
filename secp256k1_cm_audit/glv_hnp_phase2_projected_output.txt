removing the trivial vector exposed a DIFFERENT parasitic vector.

so the governing quantity is not lambda_1 of the full lattice but
        gamma = ||pv_0|| / (lambda_1(L_0)/2)
where L_0 is the Kannan-0 sublattice (= the R2 lattice L2, generated by
the modular rows, the d-row and the k2-rows) and pv_0 = (k1_i*S_K1,
k2_i*S_K2) is the exact distance from the BDD target to L2.
Unique decoding is possible iff gamma < 1.
