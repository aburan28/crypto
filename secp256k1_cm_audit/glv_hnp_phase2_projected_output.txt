EXP U5: closed form for the wall, and where it actually sits
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Solving ||pi(v_planted)|| = GH(piL) for eff = K1*K2/n gives
  eff_1 = [ n^(2m) * C^dim / (n*sqrt(2m/3+1))^dim ]^(1/m),  C=sqrt(dim/(2*pi*e))
with dim = 2m+1.  As m -> infinity this tends to 3/(2*pi*e) = 0.1756.
eff_obs = midpoint of the observed pass/fail transition in EXP U2/U3.

  curve                  m       n  eff_1 (r=1)   eff_obs  obs/pred
  8-bit/199              6     199       0.0539    0.2640      4.90x
  12-bit/2557            8    2659       0.0522    0.1955      3.74x
  12-bit/2677 FAIL      10    2647       0.0665    0.0985      1.48x
  17-bit sweep (pooled)  12   65719       0.0598    0.1000      1.67x

The observed wall sits ~2-4x above the r=1 point, i.e. LLL/BKZ recovers the
planted vector even when it is LONGER than the Gaussian heuristic.  The best
empirical split in EXP U3 is r = 1.379, not r = 1.  So the planted vector was
never required to be lambda_1 -- which is exactly why removing the trivial
vector (EXP U2) changed nothing.
