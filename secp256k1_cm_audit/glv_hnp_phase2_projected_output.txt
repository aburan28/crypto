EXP 7: is S_KANNAN = n mistuned?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXP 6 shows exact SVP on the embedded lattice (1/10) losing to exact CVP
on the un-embedded one (5/10) at K1=6 -- the embedding itself is leaking.
Standard Kannan wants the embedding factor ~ the target distance, which
here is ||(k1*S_K1, k2*S_K2)|| ~ n*sqrt(2m/3), not n.

EXP 8: list-decoding -- the actual information-theoretic ceiling
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXP 7 shows a tuned Kannan arm (3/10 at K1=8) beating exact nearest-point
CVP (1/10), so nearest-point decoding is NOT the ceiling.  A3 enumerates
every lattice point within R = 1.1 * sqrt(m*(K1*S_K1)^2/3 +
m*(K2*S_K2)^2/3) of t -- a radius computed from public parameters only --
and accepts if any candidate decodes to the true d.

#cand is the enumeration list size = the number of extra scalar
multiplications the attack pays; 2000.0 means the nr_solutions cap was
hit, so that row's success rate is a LOWER bound.

    K1    A1-LLL  A1-BKZ40  A2-CVP*  A3-LIST    mean #cand
