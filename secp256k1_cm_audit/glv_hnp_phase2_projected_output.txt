EXP U0: Gaussian-heuristic prediction of the K1 wall (analytic)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
det(L_O) = n^(3m+1)/(K1*K2)^m,  dim 2m+2,  ||v_O||^2 ~ n^2*(2m/3 + 4/3)
det(L_R) = n^(3m)  /(K1*K2)^m,  dim 2m+1,  ||v_R||^2 ~ n^2*(2m/3 + 1)
Both give, with eff = K1*K2/n and C = sqrt(3/(2*pi*e)) = 0.4191:
    L_O:  eff < C^2 * (C^2   / n)^(1/m)
    L_R:  eff < C^2 * (C^2*3/(2m+3) * (2m+1)/(2m+3) ... )^(1/m)   [numeric below]
i.e. dropping the d-column buys exactly one factor of  n*S_D  in the
determinant, which amortises as  n^(1/m)  -- an m-th root.  Predicted
gain is therefore SMALL.
