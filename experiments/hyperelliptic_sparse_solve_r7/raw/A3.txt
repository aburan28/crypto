S_walk is rho's walk alone (S_rho also carries the 16-branch
precomputation, a fixed cost that does not scale with sqrt(N) and so
inflates S_rho at these toy sizes). The collision itself arrives after
about sqrt(pi*N/2) = 1.25*sqrt(N) distinct points, but Floyd's cycle
finding spends 3 group operations per iteration, so S_walk ~ 3 is this
reference at its own optimum. A distinguished-point rho would cut that
by roughly 3x -- which widens the gap below, it does not close it.
S_ic/S_rho below 1 would mean index calculus won that instance.
