# Root-finding pipeline complete-cost calibration

- **Status:** `open`
- **Kind:** measurement gap; implementation gap for native-work accounting; independent-host validation gap
- **Affected work:** [ecbench root-finding assessment](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_assessment_20261012/REPORT.md), [arity trial](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_arity_20261013/REPORT.md), [batched pair-table trial](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_batchtable_20261013/REPORT.md), its [target-pinned follow-up](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_batchtable_20261013/followup/), and the [synchronized-lane and coverage study](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_lanes_20261013/REPORT.md)
- **Next action owner:** unassigned measurement and ecbench instrumentation task
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-14

## Problem

A qualified full-cost or wall-time advantage for the root-finding IC pipeline cannot be established from the 20-bit prime and 17-bit Koblitz sessions because the prime IC and matched rho arms leave native operations unpriced, every local wall record is L0, and the sealed sessions have only same-host replay. The original ecbench subtraction arm uses generic group arithmetic; a later current-binary `rootfind-subtract:m=2` receipt now exists on the 20-bit curve but does not price every native primitive or validate the paper kernel's timing on P-192 through P-521. The 2026-10-13 batched *pair-table construction* trial measures a different setup optimization.

The original assessment and small-curve measurement are complete at their stated scope. This record tracks the stronger performance conclusion that remains open; it is not a hypothesis or goal status.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- **Observed:** The two [sealed sessions](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_assessment_20261012/sessions/) have 128 verified executions each, 24 measured per arm, and [full](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_assessment_20261012/audit-prime20-all.json) [replay](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_assessment_20261012/audit-koblitz17-all.json) receipts with zero problems. The [summaries](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_assessment_20261012/summary-prime20.json) and [Koblitz summaries](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_assessment_20261012/summary-koblitz17.json) record `lower_bound`, `unpriced`, phase GAE, and process RSS. The host reports macOS arm64 with no CPU reservation; all measured wall grades are L0.
- **Observed, 2026-10-13:** The [B64 arity session](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_arity_20261013/REPORT.md) measured m3/m2 = 1.431345 in counted GAE (24 paired measured runs, 95% target-cluster interval 1.224624–1.716117); its auxiliary batched-subtraction arm has 32 setup errors because the retained binary predates that oracle. The separate [batched pair-table session](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_batchtable_20261013/REPORT.md) has 128 verified executions, [32/32 exact paired output and count checks](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_batchtable_20261013/pair-check.json), and [96/96 identical measured replays](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_batchtable_20261013/replay-all.json). Its scalar and batched arms each average 18,633.042 cold counted GAE; mean setup wall is 243.226 versus 128.380 µs at L0, with no admitted whole-solve wall result.
- **Observed, later 2026-10-13:** The [target-pinned sweep](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_batchtable_20261013/followup/reports/sweep/REPORT.md) used the rebuilt binary (SHA-256 `c5d2539d4d50976741982f32bc444d4b4e625a3a239bd9910f30606c3ccddb57`), eight fresh public targets, 256/256 verified executions, and 192/192 identical measured replays. B64 target pin / B64 checked full rank is 0.4313 (95% target-cluster interval 0.3987–0.4701) in cold counted GAE, with [24/24 exact first-pin matches](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_batchtable_20261013/followup/sweep-pair-check.json). The current-binary `rootfind-subtract:m=2` arm verified all 24 measured executions and matched the B64 table arm's trial count, relation count, rank and scalar in every paired row; its counted ratio was 42.4077 (39.2612–45.1413), with prime native work still unpriced. The [independent-target holdout](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_batchtable_20261013/followup/reports/holdout/REPORT.md) verified 160/160 executions and replayed 120/120 measured rows; B48/B64 target pin was 0.8758 (0.8191–0.9370) in lower-bound GAE, while B48/B40 remained uncertain. These are one-curve-size observations, not a full-cost or scaling result.
- **Observed, native walk follow-up:** The [fresh-target native session](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_batchtable_20261013/followup/reports/native/REPORT.md) on release binary SHA-256 `1ff5b6d5f717f79cdfc557fde0017b0fac7e40502fe26a28bd7cdb2a99354904` verified 128/128 executions and replayed all 96 measured rows identically. The 24 native/generic B48 pairs had equal answer, relation stream, rank, and counted GAE (ratio 1.0000); their L0 median solve-wall ratio was 1.3898 native/generic, with zero L2-admitted pairs. The separate lane kernel matched exact final points at widths 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 and used one inversion per synchronized step, but has not been integrated into a complete relation collector. Neither diagnostic establishes a runtime gain.
- **Observed, 2026-10-14 integrated lanes:** The [frozen follow-up](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_lanes_20261013/REPORT.md) integrated 4/8/16 serial and batched lane schedules into the full prime relation collector. Five sealed public-target sessions verified 1,088/1,088 executions and replayed all 816 measured records identically; [72/72 serial/batch pairs](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_lanes_20261013/lanes-pair-check.json) matched counted cost, relation counters, rank and answer. The focused test matched exact point/coefficient streams, including restarts. The batch-inversion primitive reduced inversions in the focused test, but the sealed ecbench record does not serialize `GroupOps.field_ops`, so a field-operation improvement is not established there. Train B48 did not remain a unique holdout winner: B56/B48 cold counted GAE was 0.979, interval 0.919–1.038. Fixed-stride coverage was close to the exact uniform occupancy control in both panels, with target-cluster intervals including parity; target-pinned stopping prevents a fixed equal-probe comparison. These are L0, lower-bound results.
- **Observed, equal-prefix control:** The [fresh-target fixed-prefix panel](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_lanes_20261013/FIXED_PREFIX_FRESH.md) used eight new public targets and the same first 8,192 surviving points for three B48 bases. Its predeclared 4,096-probe stride-23/stride-1 comparison yielded 2.750 additional independent rows per target (paired interval 1.000–4.625) and a rank-per-lower-bound-cold-GAE ratio of 1.095 (1.032–1.176). The bounded early-prefix criterion passed, while its effect attenuated by 8,192 probes. This closes the unequal-probe-budget gap for the component coverage question; correlated walk points, native unpriced costs, and a target-pinned full solve remain separate.
- **Derived from the new source loops:** At B=64 the pair-table builder uses 4,096 individual field inversions versus 64 batched inversions, while keeping 4,160 abstract table additions. This is a primitive-cost reduction in target-independent setup, not a measured whole-solve speedup; ecbench does not yet expose the table field-operation tally in the sealed record.
- **Derived or proved:** The source paper's two-summand group-law identity is algebraically sound under its generic affine and sign conventions. The measured counted-$S$ pair-table/subtraction quotients are 0.018861 prime and 0.057047 Koblitz, each at the two recorded curve sizes; only the Koblitz IC-vs-IC comparison has no ecbench.gae unpriced-work flag. A complete-cost ratio is not established.
- **Suspected explanation:** Native arithmetic, coordinate conversion, jump-cache lookup and inversion choice may change the prime wall ratio materially; their separate contributions are unmeasured. Shared-host contention makes the observed L0 wall ratios unsuitable for a qualified timing comparison, but it does not explain the deterministic operation counts.
- **Missing capability:** Serialization and common-unit pricing of native field operations, prime square roots, Legendre tests, hash probes, row operations, table field work, native coordinate conversion, and relevant rho canonicalization; an L2 isolated host for the same matched targets; independent-host replay. The integrated multi-lane collector and a separate equal-prefix coverage receipt exist, but inversion counts are absent from sealed ecbench records. The [L2 access receipt](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_lanes_20261013/l2-access-check.json) records no configured isolab URL or CLI and connection refusal from both configured SSH aliases on 2026-10-14. The current-binary batched direct-subtraction receipt covers `m=2` against the batched pair-table arm on the registered 20-bit curve, but not an L2 or fully priced comparison beside the generic subtraction arm.
- **What this evidence does not establish:** A scaling law, a P-curve complete solve, a qualified wall-time advantage, a matched full-cost improvement over rho, or a bound on the paper kernel's performance. The missing measurements are not negative mathematical evidence.

## What would unblock it

Run a new frozen, source-bound protocol with all pipeline arms and their matched rho reference on each declared target, with native operations charged in a common unit, isolated wall measurement, complete setup and memory, and independent replay.

- [ ] A new ecbench record for the same registered curve identities and eight public target IDs per curve, with 24 measured executions per arm, scalar verification, a completed full-rank IC solve, and `unpriced: []` for every arm; retain all attempts, including failures.
- [ ] L2 or stronger isolation for paired wall comparisons, passing ecbench's A/A gate, complete setup, relation, linear-algebra, recovery and verification timing, and peak memory for one cold target. Preserve host and binary receipts.
- [ ] Full independent replay of the frozen record set on a second host class, with the reviewer checking target, factor-base, binary, source and cost bindings. The now-implemented `rootfind-subtract:m=2` has a current-binary, exactly verified, same-host receipt beside the batched pair table; it still needs an L2, fully priced comparison beside generic subtraction. The earlier stale-binary setup errors remain preserved separately.
- [x] For the bounded component coverage question, record an equal, fixed prefix of surviving table probes for each declared base and the exact pair-key occupancy control, then compare independent useful rank per probe, lower-bound cold cost and structural memory on fresh public targets. The [fresh panel](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_lanes_20261013/FIXED_PREFIX_FRESH.md) meets this criterion at its declared 4,096-probe milestone. A full-cost or universal coverage-bias claim still requires the other gates above.

Meeting these conditions would permit a qualified performance comparison at the two declared sizes and identify how much the optimized prime kernel changes it. A larger-field claim would still need a separately frozen scaling design and matched baseline there; novelty and stronger F4 checks of the manuscript remain separate.

## Next discriminating step

- **Action or proof obligation:** Expose the already-counted native field operations in sealed ecbench records, price the remaining native operations, and run the frozen L2 lane panel on an isolated Linux host with rho A/A controls. Preserve the five 2026-10-14 L0 sessions and separate fixed-prefix receipts as distinct evidence.
- **Inputs and controls:** The current [specs](../research/ecbench_rootfinding_assessment_20261012/specs/) define the curve and target seeds; use separate arm names for generic subtraction and any batched kernel. Keep rho, signed-factor-base definitions, full-rank checks and scalar verification matched. Compare new charged phase rows to the sealed local rows without rewriting them.
- **Execution limits and stop condition:** One 8-target by 3-round measured session per curve under the new frozen protocol, plus its warmup and all-record audit. Stop on ecbench admission refusal, invalid answers, or missing cost fields and retain the failure receipt; do not silently substitute a smaller workload.
- **Outcomes and next actions:** If all arms validate, report cold total cost, wall intervals, memory and the matched ratio at each size; if the table loses its IC-vs-IC advantage after pricing, record that bounded result; if it retains the advantage but rho remains cheaper, keep those distinct. A timeout or infrastructure failure changes no mathematical conclusion and calls for a repaired run under the same frozen scope.
- **Recheck trigger:** Native field-operation serialization or pricing, or availability of an isolated independent host.

A planned step is not a completed experiment. The new protocol requires the repository's ordinary execution, review and archival rules.
