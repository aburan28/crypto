# Koblitz geometry at field exponents 37, 53 and 83

This panel uses the requested larger fields for exact structural arithmetic.
It supersedes no toy evidence: PRs #708 and #710 remain small-field controls.
It does not change the exhaustive toy solver's field parameters or claim
large-field decomposition experiments have run.

Curves: E_a: y^2+xy=x^3+a*x^2+1 over F_(2^n), for a=0 and a=1.
The two variants have opposite traces at these odd n, and identical conductor
factorizations. The original Koblitz curves have maximal endomorphism order
of discriminant -7. The conductor below belongs to the order generated by
the full-field Frobenius, NOT the original curve's endomorphism ring.

| n | Conductor of Z[tau^n] | Prime degrees allowing rational descent | Depth for each prime |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 37 | 194399 = 73 * 2663 | 73, 2663 | 1 |
| 53 | 68476319 (prime) | 68476319 | 1 |
| 83 | 347450761417 = 6473 * 53676929 | 6473, 53676929 | 1 |

Thus n=53 does not provide a small-prime descending edge over its stated base
field: its first prime-degree descent has degree 68,476,319. Field size alone
does not predict whether a small-degree descent exists. Changing n changes
the Frobenius-order conductor and therefore the available rational depths.
Depth is specific to one prime: n=37 and n=83 each have two distinct
depth-one volcano directions, not one universal level index.

For each listed prime ell, a first descending step changes the endomorphism
order from O_K to Z+ell*O_K, with discriminant -7*ell^2. The exact target
order discriminants are recorded in results.json. The survey does not
construct a target equation, j-invariant, kernel, or isogeny map. These
orders must not be described as concrete descended curve instances.

## Exact checks

The source uses only Python's standard library and integer arithmetic:

- Independent trace recurrences in a quadratic order and in the integers.
- Norm and discriminant identities, Hasse bounds, and ordinary-curve trace parity.
- Complete trial-division conductor factorizations; separate primality and
  product checks.
- Opposite traces/equal conductors for the two Koblitz variants at odd n.
- Deterministic output replay and source/result hashes.

Reproduce from the repository root:

```sh
python3 research/koblitz_structural_fields_20260924/verify.py
```

## Scope and decision

This answers which endomorphism orders and prime-degree descent directions
are available at the requested field sizes. It supplies no F4/F5 traces,
solving degree, regularity measurement, rational relation yield, or speedup.
All corresponding result fields remain null. No cloud workers were launched.
An explicit neighbor construction is a distinct missing result, especially
at the very large degree forced by n=53.

Reference: Andrew Sutherland, [Isogeny volcanoes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.5370),
and [lecture slides](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/PANTSXX.pdf). The structural
depth formula for ordinary curves and ell != characteristic applies here.
The numerical factorizations above are computed and checked by this panel.
