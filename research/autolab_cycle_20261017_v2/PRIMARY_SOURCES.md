# Primary-source scope review

Victor Shoup's author bibliography lists von zur Gathen and Shoup,
Computing Frobenius maps and factoring polynomials, Computational Complexity
2 (1992),187-224: https://shoup.net/papers/ . The author-hosted full paper
https://shoup.net/papers/frobenius.pdf was opened and its first page visually
reviewed because text extraction has an invalid font mapping. The abstract
identifies Frobenius/trace computations in polynomial quotient rings as the
central mechanism. Our quotient Frobenius, support and factoring components
therefore sit within established finite-field factoring techniques; this is
not a novelty proof or a complete priority search for the specialized branch.

Cantor, On arithmetical algorithms over finite fields (1989), DOI
https://doi.org/10.1016/0097-3165(89)90020-4, has publisher metadata/abstract
about additive-subgroup transforms. Full publisher access was403; no claim
of full-paper review is made. Do not characterize additive-annihilator/Gaussian
intersection as new based on this limited search.

Potential novelty must concern a proved and measured hard-family search
reduction or integration beyond these classical primitives, with exact
original-root and fiber preservation and a stronger matched fastest baseline.
No current observation establishes that novelty or the requested8x gain.
