Before any shared-index timing, `/usr/bin/time -l true` exited 1 in the
sandbox with `time: sysctl kern.clockrate: Operation not permitted`.
The ordinary `/usr/bin/time true` exited 0 but omitted peak RSS. The native
worker now records process peak RSS with `getrusage(RUSAGE_SELF)` after the
online interval. macOS reports `ru_maxrss` in bytes; other Unix systems
report KiB and the worker converts them to bytes. No speedup claim relies on
this host probe. CPU model `sysctl` may also be denied; the runner labels
the host unisolated and preserves that stderr.
