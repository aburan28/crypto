# Matrix-F5 compact AVX2 gather unpack: frozen experiment

## Hypothesis and reference

The qualified one-thread n24 degree-4 F5 call in PR #1024 took about 107.6 ms, including about 54.1 ms to materialize 13,734,979 output monomials. A compact `u32` column map plus scalar pair decoding was exact but measured 1.000× on the complete call. This experiment tests a distinct AVX2 path: decode four set-bit column indices at a time, gather four `u32` masks, widen them to `u64`, and write four `F2BoolMono` values together. The hypothesis is that grouped loads and writes overcome the scalar gather/control cost. The reference is the current direct scalar unpack in the same release binary, `KIC_F5_UNPACK_AVX2_GATHER=0`; the candidate is opt-in with `=1`. Its map conversion and allocation are inside the complete call. Enable it only when direct unpack is selected, AVX2 is available at runtime, AVX-512 unpack is off, `n_vars ≤ 32`, and there are at least 4,096 columns. Cap **actual** compact-map allocation at 256 KiB and fall back otherwise. Check packed-row width and padding once before any unchecked lookup. Keep a portable scalar fallback and handle tails of zero to three terms exactly.

The source reference is main commit `f613cb483a3a95f62d7992d7165eb9acbfca1df2`: `matrix_f5_f2.rs` SHA-256 `bf35680f431ab7ac13a7615d334ec9b060217b84bdde36f03ce00319491d42da`, `gf2_elim.rs` `6fd3f10ef8e681490784c624e703eeb8fa887c5817ec9bdf34844e3f943c7f5c`, benchmark `f4_f2_bench.rs` `d40f9f0c76e5c56c9f4a52596b9edef41f321aa3d70cfb023c4ba21fdde70336`, isolation wrapper `ff59e53fd5566078ad493c8be0d06168699c4fd194bc881d99df19fa20e007ef`. Qualified unchanged-source baselines and binary hashes are in merged #998 and the completed PR #1024 experiment. Absolute times from those hosts are context only; candidate ratios use explicit `=0/1` processes from one binary on the same runner. The exact output and counted-operation contract is unchanged.

## Workload, isolation, and decision

Run all seven F5 cases in `examples/f4_f2_bench.rs`, primary `f5_n24_m24_d4`, seeds `0`, `badc0de1`, `5eed2026`, `f5c02a28`, one and two Rayon threads separately. Hold selective echelon output, fused row count, direct packed rows, branchless AVX2 strip, AVX2 row XOR, Gray-code table reuse, and four-table elimination fixed. Disable deferred-above, word batching, AVX-512 unpack and experimental table construction. Per seed and thread count, run one warmup per arm, five A/A reference pairs and five alternating A/B pairs: 22 processes with all seven cases. Preserve every call, failure, timeout, OOM, source/binary hash, CPU/affinity, raw/canonical row fingerprints, rank, terms, columns, row/criterion/build counts, reduction word operations, route, actual map bytes, exclusive phases, and complete-call time. Release unit tests must cover partial words, odd bit counts, sparse/dense rows, out-of-range masks, budget fallback, and exact output against scalar unpack; CI must execute the AVX2 path on supported x86-64 hardware.

Build/test through `tools/isolated_bench.py busy` under one lock. Each timed seed block uses privileged `tools/isolated_bench.py reserve`, all SMT siblings reserved, one CPU left free, runner Rustup environment passed through, ten-second preflight, other-CPU rate at most 0.10, PSI at most 5.0, zero eligible user threads and zero contended samples at a two-second period. Attempt each seed at most three times in order, select the **first** block with reservation success and 22 exact calls independent of timing, and archive every refusal and partial call. If any seed lacks a qualified attempt, speedup is unknown. Do not combine absolute times across hosts.

The candidate advances only if the frozen one-thread primary complete-call paired reference/candidate median is at least 1.05, its exact 3,125-resample bootstrap 95% lower bound exceeds 1.02, no seed or smaller case has a complete-call median below its own A/A minimum, the two-thread control has no such regression, and actual compact-map allocation stays within 256 KiB. A passing candidate needs a separately frozen default-on confirmation. A failing candidate has its tested patch, workflow and raw receipts archived and its runtime option removed. The user's further 2× goal requires the frozen primary full-call median and lower bound both above 2.0 with all guards. With unpacking about half the reference call, a 2× complete-call result from this lever alone would require nearly eliminating that phase; any smaller gain must combine with improvements elsewhere. These are solver-stage measurements, not IC one-target online DLP or rho speedups.
