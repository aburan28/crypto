Frozen target and decision protocol on 2026-09-25, **before any L384 producer outcome**. This follows the independently replayed six-pair [n53 shared-log study](../autolab_shared_log_n53_20260925/RESULT.md) merged as `1909cf45577ee9e141fc1e26acf215c990db4760`. ## Exact arms, order, and stop limits

Run sequentially on one fresh GitHub-hosted Ubuntu runner. First execute exactly the same cold 512-target known-scalar training schedule/domain, compact producer arguments and orbit-factorized environment as the prior n53 study. Charge all 512 targets, including the 51 after first full rank; require rank 220 and all 220 solved base logs before any point arm. The full-rank base-log solution must be derived from this fresh producer output. Do not load prior run's solved logs. A fresh operational rank stage reconstructs relation rows, solves and checks consistency without access to the L384 withheld labels. Time this stage separately from the Rust training child and from the later independent group-law audit.

Then run **rho before IC** for this single fixed stream, with identical `points_L384.jsonl` in both child environments. Rho command: `koblitz_rho_batch_ks 53 0 signed_frobenius 384 531384`; `KIC_RHO_TARGET_POINTS_JSONL=<combined file>`, `KIC_RHO_BATCH_CORPUS=n53-combined-L384-20260925-v1`, `KIC_RHO_DP_BITS=4`, `KIC_RHO_PRECOMPUTE_WALKS=0`. It starts with an empty shared table and uses one table for all 384 Q values. IC point command: `koblitz_s5_sat_instance 53 0 1 10 natural 1 2000 1 internal`; `KIC_ALGEBRA_ENCODING=orbit_factorized`, `KIC_ORBIT_LAZY_RELATIVE_SUPPORT=1`, `KIC_ORBIT_BRANCH_ORDER=pair_then_pair`, `KIC_ORBIT_REP_ENCODING=one_hot`, `KIC_ORBIT_BATCH_ONLY=1`, `KIC_FACTOR_BASE_JSONL=<same certified header>`, `KIC_ORBIT_TARGET_POINTS_JSONL=<combined file>`. It builds one fresh regular-state index and queries all 384 Q values. A separate operational recovery stage converts point witnesses plus newly solved base logs into every scalar, without validator labels. No child receives a planted scalar label.

Apply fixed per-process wall caps: training producer 360 s; operational rank 180 s; rho 600 s; IC point producer 300 s; operational point recovery 180 s; independent audit 600 s. Peak resident memory must stay below 2 GiB per process group; global workflow ceiling 45 minutes. Record each attempted stage's exit code, timeout, wall, user+system CPU, peak RSS, load, commands/environment, raw stdout/stderr and SHA-256. If training fails rank, stop; otherwise attempt **both** rho and IC even when one fails, unless the global or memory ceiling prevents it. Do not substitute a seed or target. A timeout, missing log, wrong count, or failed audit is a retained censored/invalid result, never omitted.

## Accounting, replay and decision

Primary fully charged IC operational wall is the cold training producer **plus** mandatory rank/linear solve **plus** cold point producer **plus** mandatory scalar recovery. Rho operational wall is its single cold batch process, including setup, walks and table. Exclude compilation, fixed public-Q creation and the separate independent audit from both operational walls; disclose them separately. Report a conservative audit-inclusive IC upper and the incomplete two-Rust-child lower, but never call the lower a recovery cost. Sequential peak RSS is the maximum across stages and CPU is summed. Preserve exact IC training/query S3 calls, partner roots, indexed hits, group lifts, scans/index entries, failed queries and rho walks, group additions, table entries, collisions and all native charges. S3 calls and rho additions are not a common `S` unit; without a pre-frozen measured conversion, leave attack-speed crossover unset regardless of wall ratio.

A second arithmetic implementation must independently replay the 512 training relations, all 23,320 orbit labels, rank transition and 220 base logs, all 384 IC point relations/scalars, and all 384 rho scalars on the exact same Q rows; finally compare recovered values against withheld labels. Record each failure, hash all raw artifacts, and commit an immutable archive with a replay script. A fixed-stream **operational wall win** requires all 384 correct logs from both arms, zero hidden failed queries, and fully charged IC operational wall below single-table rho wall. If even the IC two-child lower exceeds rho, record a fixed-stream no-crossover; otherwise classify the comparison as inconclusive. One deterministic target order and one rho seed do not estimate dispersion or establish a general many-target break-even. Record the ratio and native-work table even on an inconclusive or censored run.
