## Hypothesis and fixed inputs

The primary hypothesis is that 'target_cyclic_v1' keeps the **fresh native ascending-x base** full rank on a new 512-target training stream and reduces its fully charged L384 logarithm cost relative to one cold same-Q signed-Frobenius rho process. Arm **B**, the new disjoint training stream, is the transfer test. Arm **A** repeats #823's exact 512 known-scalar training targets as a positive control. Both arms independently construct a new 220-column/23,320-point signed-Frobenius base, materialize and solve its own 512 training equations, construct its own point index, and recover the **same new 384 public Q**. One fresh rho table processes that Q once. Run order is fixed: rho, B, A. Run A even if B fails and B even if rho fails, subject only to frozen caps. Never cut A at its previously observed full-rank target 273, append targets, change policy/constants, choose a different base or replace a failure.

'generate_inputs.py' SHA-256-domain separates B and Q, reduces nonzero scalars modulo the n53 subgroup order, and derives each public point with a separately implemented binary-field group law. It fixes 512 B targets and 384 Q in their committed order. The generator asserts zero scalar/point overlap among A, B, Q, the older 512 training stream, and the older 384-Q stream, and reproduces every committed byte during preflight. Q's validator scalars are committed for **independent audit only**; neither IC point producer nor rho receives that file. A's scalar bytes equal #823 exactly. The frozen manifest records source/input SHA-256 and the disjointness proof. All inputs are public synthetic points, not an ECC2K-130 challenge log.

Native base construction receives **no** 'KIC_FACTOR_BASE_JSONL'. It emits its entire freshly constructed base header. The separately timed materializer checks 53/0, cofactor 428, order 21044858204113, generator, 220 columns, 23,320 unique ordered points/labels, 106 members per signed orbit, the ascending-x mode, and 465 scanned x values. It hashes the exact ordered arrays (points, labels, representatives) with SHA-256 canonical JSON and requires '2de8ec46916999dfe5e1ac95e68ea15a3f001eec27e013fa96d9525719b4f684', independently derived from #823's sealed native-cyclic child (whose archive and raw-member hashes are pinned). No old point or label is copied into the fresh header. The loader's 'base_hash' field is explicitly that SHA-256 array fingerprint, not #819's certified-base BLAKE3 identifier. Independent replay verifies all 23,320 group/orbit labels again; byte identity alone is insufficient.

## Accounting, replay, and decision

For **each** arm, complete operational IC wall and CPU sum its own fresh-base training, materialization, rank/base-log solve, 384-Q point-index/query child and scalar recovery. The lower bound sums only training, materialization and point child and is never reported as recovered-log cost. The matched rho charge is its single full cold process, shared as the same-Q reference for A and B rather than added twice. Independent group-law audit and compilation are reported separately. A complete result requires every stage and independent replay: 23,320 labels per arm, all successful training witnesses, full modular rank and 220 representative logs, every successful point witness, all 384 recovered IC scalars in **each** arm, and 384 rho scalars, all matching Q and withheld labels. The audit also replays every complete raw prefix if a later stage fails. Invalid, missing, timed-out, RSS-capped or rank-deficient runs are archived as partial/censored, never interpreted as a negative mathematical result. The archive verifier rehashes every raw file, rechecks receipts and stage order, reruns independent replay, and recomputes the cost/classification from sealed bytes.

If both arms and rho complete and replay, report B's fully charged operational ratio to same-Q rho as the primary fixed-stream observation; A is the positive-control ratio. A B wall below rho is a **fixed-stream engineering win** only. B wall at or above rho is a **fixed-stream no-crossover**; report the charged phase gap and whether any one stage or the pre-query subtotal could close it. One deterministic run gives no runtime distribution, common operation unit 'S', n131 transfer, general native-base yield or ECC2K-130 attack-speed verdict. The next decision must use the fully charged result, not the rank or point stage in isolation.
