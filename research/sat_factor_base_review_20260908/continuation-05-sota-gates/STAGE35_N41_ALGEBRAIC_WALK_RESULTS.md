# Stage 35 algebraic walked-descent result

Stage 35 completed from source commit `11dc73b8f1aed58d0d6f29d1d95bbc6a156a1387` on a GitHub-hosted Linux runner. It applies the walked two-summand individual descent to the same algebraic factor-base search and five public targets used by Stage 33. It does not use the pseudo-random subgroup-orbit factor base from the separate crossover experiments.

## Boundary and correction

Factor-base discovery compares the Frobenius union generated by masks `[1,2,4,8,16,32]` with its two-torsion saturation on 1,024 separate sampled subgroup points. It does not enumerate the subgroup, receive the five future targets, or use factor-base logarithm labels. The selected base is unchanged from Stage 33: 2,380 abscissae, 4,759 rational points, 60 signed Frobenius orbits, and 29 projected columns.

Relation collection still uses three summands. Only the individual descent uses two summands and walks its probes by `+G` in inversion-sharing batches. The workflow now reports both values explicitly.

The old workflow bound of 200,000 probes solved only one of the five exact targets, and the previous hard maximum of one million solved four. The remaining target completed after 1,054,786 probes. Stage 35 therefore raises the validation ceiling to the intended 100 million while preserving every other Stage-33 parameter.

## Relations, targets, and rho

The workflow collected 39 verified relations from 14,336 probes and group-certified all 29 factor-base logarithms after sparse filtering. All five public hash-derived targets completed without constructed or supplied expected scalars.

The walked descents used 2,417,575 probes and 0.246046139 seconds in total, or 0.049209228 seconds per target. Signed-Frobenius rho used 425,616 iterations and 0.504091212 seconds in total, or 0.100818242 seconds per target. Thus the post-precomputation online descent is 2.048767 times faster than rho on this finite panel. Relative to the Stage-33 online IC/rho ratio, the ratio improved by a factor of 4.608397.

The crossover does not survive broader accounting. Factor-base discovery, relation collection, and log computation consumed 14.300567224 seconds. Amortized over five targets, IC is 28.857106 times slower than rho. The complete fresh dependency/build plus one-CPU science wall is 105.117902093 seconds, or 208.529527 times the same five rho runs. Neither the amortized nor whole-process verdict crosses.
