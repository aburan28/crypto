# Literature matching and claim boundaries

Reviewed 10 September 2026 against the recorded Stage 13, Stage 18, completed
Stage 19 Amendment 03, and balanced Phase A/Phase B artifacts. This review
does not independently reproduce their mathematical computations.

The evidence supports an internal engineering and finite mathematical result.
It does not establish a new Koblitz index-calculus state of the art. Direct
MITM has the lowest median core time in every Stage 13 cell; the setup-charged
Stage 18 comparison favors signed-Frobenius rho by 2,742.274176 in aggregate
core time. Neither statement extrapolates to a complexity bound.

## Matching the published regimes

| Source | Relevant parameters and interpretation | Relation to this campaign |
|---|---|---|
| GGMP, Table 2 | n=31, ell=5, m=3; 200 experiments comparing standard, random, and Frobenius-invariant spaces | Our n31 dimensions match, but our standard cell uses a=1 and 31 factor points while our GGMP cell uses a=0 and 63 points. Their timing ratio cannot isolate the construction's effect. |
| GGMP, Table 3 | Includes n=41, ell=5, m=3 vector-space/fraction examples; the fraction examples are explicitly not Galois invariant; larger rows reach n=131 | Parameter matching alone does not reproduce the invariant construction. |
| WDSat, thesis Tables 7.2–7.6 | The prominent S3 n=41 benchmark has ell=20, m=2; the larger S4 table includes ell=9 at n=37,47,59,67 and ell=10/11 regimes | Our n41 ell5 m3 cell is a different PDP problem. The n59 ell9 m3 rung is relevant, but a scaling conclusion needs class-specific distributions and further regimes. |

These distinctions follow from the [GGMP conference paper, sections 3–5](https://sacworkshop.org/SAC20/files/preproceedings/18-IndexCalculus.pdf)
and [Trimoska's thesis, section 7.6](https://mtrimoska.com/Monika_Trimoska_these.pdf).

Direct ANF/SAT reasoning also predates this work. The 2025 SATIC paper by
Blomme, Cherif, Ionica, and Dequen studies watched ANF structures for
Semaev/Weil-descent systems and belongs in the external novelty review:
[ANF-Based Satisfiability for Weil-Descent Cryptographic Attacks](https://www.codit2025-virtual-event.com/web/content/1168?download=true&unique=08332185a80db65206de10342978b5b2225887bb).

The balanced panel's 50/50 allocation measures conditional solver behavior.
It does not estimate natural relation yield. The exact Phase A census gives
affine decomposability fractions near 2.27e-6, 1.43e-5, 1.15e-9, and 3.26e-11
in the four cells; those populations are not automatically the subgroup or
cofactor-projected population of a full IC computation. Planted sums can be
biased by decomposition multiplicity. Targets P and -P share an x-only
source system and require cluster-aware reporting.

A source-model SAT result requires rational point lifting before being counted
as a point decomposition. `Unknown`, timeout, invalid output, and nonlifting
models remain inconclusive. Calculator timing and memory headers are supporting
metadata; they do not replace process-scoped CPU and RSS. Independent internal
agent review and project-authored CI do not satisfy external reproduction.
