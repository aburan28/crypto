# Held cold-base n53 L384 matched repeat

Status: **pre-outcome design and implementation freeze**. The merged [#760](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/760) compared one compact IC index with one signed-Frobenius rho table on the same 384 public points. Its IC wall was 353.649 s against rho's 154.799 s, with 384/384 verified logs, but its runner loaded a pre-certified factor-base header before the first timed IC child. Its fixed-stream no-crossover finding stands. This successor repeats the same stream, Rust source and rho policy while charging native base construction. Its newly pinned dependency lock and toolchain mean it is a fresh same-Q matched IC-versus-rho arm, **not** a one-variable subtraction from #760's historical wall result. It is neither an independent target sample nor an n131 forecast.

## Same-Q work and charges

Build both examples from the frozen `Cargo.lock` with Rust 1.93.1 and Python 3.12 on one fresh GitHub-hosted Ubuntu x86_64 runner. Run stages sequentially from cold state: (1) native-base IC training on all 512 scheduled scalars; (2) fresh-base extraction/byte comparison/serialization; (3) operational sparse rank solve and verification of all 220 representative logs, requiring rank 220 and all 512 relations; (4) one fresh signed-Frobenius Kuhn–Struik rho process on the 384 Q file; (5) one fresh compact point-index process on **that same file**, loading only the just-emitted base header; (6) operational recovery of all 384 logs from new training logs and point witnesses; (7) separate independent audit. Rho is run before the IC point child, as in #760, with command `koblitz_rho_batch_ks 53 0 signed_frobenius 384 531384`, `KIC_RHO_DP_BITS=4`, `KIC_RHO_PRECOMPUTE_WALKS=0`, and corpus `n53-combined-L384-20260925-v1`. Each IC producer uses `koblitz_s5_sat_instance 53 0 1 10 natural 1 2000 1 internal`, `orbit_factorized`, lazy relative support, `pair_then_pair`, `one_hot`, and batch-only mode. The fixed stream, signed automorphism of size 106, rho seed, and distinguished-point policy are unchanged from #760.

Primary IC operational wall/CPU sums **training producer + base materialization + mandatory rank/solve + point producer + mandatory recovery**. Rho operational wall/CPU is its full cold one-table process. The incomplete lower bound sums training + mandatory base materialization + point producer; it is never described as recovered-log cost. Separately report the independent audit, compilation, fixture comparison and stage RSS. The fixture comparison is included in materialization wall, deliberately making the primary IC charge conservative. Process stdout/stderr, every command/environment/input/binary SHA, exit/timeout, user+system CPU, wall, sampled process-group RSS, host/load, native S3/query/root/index counters, rho additions/walks/table counts, and all failures are archived. The runner caps training at 360 s, materialization at 90 s, rank at 180 s, rho at 600 s, point IC at 300 s, recovery at 180 s, audit at 600 s, each process group below 2 GiB, and the complete sequential panel at 2,700 s including a stage's remaining global time. A timeout, failed base match, rank loss, hidden query failure or invalid scalar is censored/invalid and retained; no new seed or target can replace it.

The independent replay rebuilds all 23,320 signed-orbit labels and checks the fresh emitted base against the certified base, 512 training relations and rank transition, 220 base logs, 384 compact point relations/logs, and all 384 rho logs against the same public Q and finally withheld labels. Complete success requires 384/384 correct logs from each arm. A full-cost fixed-stream wall win requires IC operational wall below rho; if even the incomplete IC lower exceeds rho, record a fixed-stream no-crossover; otherwise the wall comparison is inconclusive. Preserve #760's no-crossover status as the prior observation regardless of this new result. One repeated deterministic stream/seed is not a dispersion estimate. S3 calls and rho group additions lack a frozen measured common operation unit, so `S`, an attack-speed crossover and an ECC2K-130 extrapolation remain unset even if a wall ratio changes.

The outcome runner and archive verifier are separate from hash-only CI. Freeze their exact hashes before release; archive the first run, including a cap or validation failure. Post-outcome replay rehashes all raw bytes, verifies every timed stage and the fresh-header provenance, reruns the independent arithmetic audit, and recomputes the charged totals from stage receipts. No merge or broader claim follows from a passing local preflight alone.
