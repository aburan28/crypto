The [frozen protocol](PROTOCOL.md) asked whether the exact #760 L384 same-Q arm could be repeated with native factor-base construction charged. The first and only dispatched [Actions run 36182222980](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/actions/runs/36182222980), attempt 1 at release source head `b3d7cf1c90473989170f8b27cb027229b99a54fd`, **stopped before rho or IC point recovery**. Its classification is `CENSORED_OR_INVALID_FRESH_BASE`. The immutable raw evidence was committed at `e7457bf3d891329f8ad80d685b5734ba5e33517c`; its 459,598-byte [archive](evidence/evidence.tar.gz) has SHA-256 `2ff3d4587dd9a576de393abacabfc390c2f838997b5dcbcc9b1e2e4554c5c23d`.

The fresh training child exited successfully after 115.236 s and reported 512/512 extracted target relations with no failed target. It had no pre-existing factor-base input. The subsequent 0.116 s materializer failed the frozen equality check, first at `cofactor`: the producer emitted integer `428`, whereas the certified header stores string `"428"`. This encoding mismatch is **not the only difference**. [Post-outcome failure replay](failure_replay.py) of the sealed raw output finds that the native constructor scanned 465 x values, versus 400 in the certified fixture. Both contain 220 signed-Frobenius columns and 23,320 coordinates, but their coordinate sets and distinct point x sets have **zero** values in common; the coordinate-array SHA-256 values are `952128e2cdd7529167c0a728d51df8c4bba85018aa84687d19e11b265795b191` fresh and `655c5ab0f0bb94753db3179426ae9c32b399ba33dbd8b22f43e2ff93110936d4` certified. None of the 220 representatives coincide, and the label arrays also differ. Normalizing the cofactor type would therefore not admit the same factor-base arm.

The source-level cause is the x-selection schedule. The certified header carries `factor_base_seed=0xd1b54a32d192ed03`, and its [rank-fixture constructor](../../../examples/koblitz_rank_fixture.rs#L3211-L3227) uses that seed mixed with n and wanted columns to generate x values by a fixed LCG for n>32. The new [compact producer constructor](../../../examples/koblitz_s5_sat_instance.rs#L146-L156) instead enumerates x as `0,1,2,...`. Both cofactor-project and collect signed-Frobenius orbits, but they are different deterministic bases. The certified header's `point_selection` phrase says "coordinate order"; the actual n53 source uses the LCG, so that phrase is insufficient to reproduce the certified base. The frozen protocol's reference to "400-x base discovery" was likewise an incorrect expectation for the new native constructor. The 400 and 465 scan counts refer to different schedules and are **not** a measured improvement or regression in base construction complexity.

The frozen [archive verifier](verify_archive.py) passed rehashing all ten archived files and both completed stage receipts, reporting `PASS_CENSORED_OR_INVALID_RAW_INTEGRITY_ONLY`. This is raw-integrity replay, **not** the complete independent group-law audit: no rank solve, rho process, IC point process, scalar recovery or 384-Q correctness checks ran. No within-run IC/rho wall ratio, fully charged cost verdict, common-operation-unit `S`, attack-speed crossover or n131 transfer follows. The merged [#760 result](../autolab_combined_l384_n53_20260925/RESULT.md)—353.649 s IC versus 154.799 s rho with a pre-certified base and a fixed-stream no-crossover—remains the prior observation.
