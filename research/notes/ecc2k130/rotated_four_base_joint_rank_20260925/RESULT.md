# Frozen n19 four-base rank and point-only archive-oracle result

**Decision: the preregistered bounded semantic follow-up gate passes.** The four bases selected by exact support census [#775](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/775) contribute 12 globally distinct signed `[4]F0` columns, three per base with no cross-base duplicate. The 256 unconditional SHA-selected training Qs supplied 516 first-witness rows across the four archives and exact joint rank 12/12 modulo `q=130873`; beta `3` alone supplied 113 rows and rank 3/3. On the same disjoint 64 point-only holdouts, 58 four-base scalars and 29 beta-3 scalars were recovered from archived witness rows, checked by `[k]H=Q`, then matched against sealed labels. The +29 difference passes the frozen threshold of 12. Six of the 64 four-base holdouts had no archived witness.

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), source and input SHA-256 values, and literal target split were frozen at head `878d37dbb88c44a252dce1892c1c63c4b7c89d51` in draft [#779](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/779) before outcomes; hash-only CI was green. Both arms use precisely the same training and holdout Qs. The target order was not filtered by support. Training uses each base's first archived projected witness when present; all 64 holdouts were excluded from all training rows. The [independent verifier](verify.py) regenerated every selected row and exact rank using the separate bit-serial/Fermat group law, rebuilt the archive oracle, checked the point of every solved column log and recovered scalar, then opened the sealed labels. It passed on the first frozen run.

By base, the training misses were 143 (`3`), 114 (`338435`), 114 (`303097`) and 137 (`464276`). The joint arm's 64 archive-oracle queries made 129 fixed-order base probes; beta `3` made 64. Those are probes of an existing complete witness archive, not measured solver calls. The point-only recovery child received Q coordinates, solved column logs and stripped archive rows, with no k, source indices, witness indices or sealed labels. Its point checks and the independent replay both passed. Isolation is source/argument-level, not an OS sandbox.

The first cold local run read 23,927,716 uncompressed archive bytes in **each** of the training and oracle children. Their wall times were 3.307 and 2.929 seconds; point recovery and independent replay took 0.061 and 2.099 seconds. The training child peaked at 313,278,464 bytes RSS, under the 512-MiB cap; every child exited zero under its wall cap. [Compact raw results and complete receipt](EVIDENCE.md) preserve the inputs, outputs, hashes, exact commands, counters and bounds. The prior #767/#769 archives already certified every labelled `7^6` tuple in each base; this PR replays all selected first witnesses, not that entire census.

This is a **toy archive-oracle semantic result**, not an index-calculus performance result. The training archive was already built and the recovery oracle looks up saved witnesses. There is no implicit S6 PDP search, negative-PDP certificate, full relation-acquisition cost, matched rho reference, end-to-end `S`, n131 target support, ECC2K-130 challenge log, or IC/rho speed ratio in this receipt. In particular, 58/64 is a fixed held-out count at n19; it cannot be transported to n131. The passed gate justifies trying an actual charged implicit-PDP solver against the same frozen target stream, with timeout and negative costs retained, before promoting this portfolio as an attack direction.
