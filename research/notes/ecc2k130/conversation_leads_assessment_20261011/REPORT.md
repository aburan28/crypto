## Ranked leads and cheapest falsifiers

1. **Compact-orbit factor bases plus cheaper rank and target decomposition.** This is the most evidenced executable IC path in the export (conversations `877d18ed-ed3d-4701-9526-caafccab2de0`, `a3bb6356-5815-4a0b-8761-c4f4f4cb95d1`, `f16172d4-6740-4fce-93a8-997dda6936db`; [subfield-curve paper](https://sacworkshop.org/SAC20/files/preproceedings/18-IndexCalculus.pdf)). Frobenius collapses columns, but the cold panels show that base/index/rank preparation still dominates fresh one-target cost. The latest a=1/K600 fresh-target panel gives mixed prepared-online results against tuned strong rho, strengthening the case for reducing preparation without promoting an online winner. The immediate falsifier is a frozen, fixed-useful-column comparison on new disjoint points: count natural relation yield, novel rank per accepted row, complete base/index/rank and target phases, bytes, and matched optimized rho. First isolate the hot second S3 root call under an identical rank trace; the pair-swap result indicates why an index-only saving can miss the target path. Class: **engineering candidate**, with an open possibility of a new relation/rank regime only if the measured ratio to the fixed boundary changes.

2. **Target-coupled higher-arity joins and alternative factor-base geometry.** Conversations `877d18ed-ed3d-4701-9526-caafccab2de0`, `d57e1315-4f00-4f55-92a4-97d9b9cd66f6`, and `ee67891a-e959-4dbb-9dd1-b47f2b4dfe60` propose more summands, shifted/Frobenius domains, and exact pair joins. The n131 W15 result proves a fixed-family filter, while the public-point misses and n83 bounded PDP show why supply and solve cost must be measured jointly. The next bounded gate is the existing N41 target-coupled seed-family elimination, with ordinary targets, constructed correctness controls, complete candidate-family counts, useful-rank checks, memory, and charged work; advance toward N131 only with a validated scaling law and a native Rust execution path. Class: **proposed decomposition route**, not an observed complete-method improvement.

3. **Early-prunable algebraic or SAT/F4/F5 systems.** Conversations `370c1202-ba41-4a45-ba9c-c41a7c3bf8f0`, `6108ece5-7c36-4f1b-94f7-2b837d3318b3`, `d57e1315-4f00-4f55-92a4-97d9b9cd66f6`, and `59fceced-c301-4865-a848-0e04b0a047fb` focus on first-fall degree, solver structure, syzygies, and symmetry (see the [WDSat paper](https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/313.pdf) and [last-fall-degree analysis](https://eprint.iacr.org/2015/573.pdf)). A smaller formula or fewer SAT decisions matters only if it changes ordinary-query cost under the same complete factor-base domain, or improves a full charged run. The falsifier is a frozen ordinary-query panel with valid SAT, UNSAT, timeout, and producer-failure distinctions, matched formula domains, exact group replay, and a measured growth curve across sizes. The n83 120 s `UNKNOWN` is censored, not evidence of no relation. Class: **unverified mechanism with possible exponent relevance**; the export's categorical claims about first-fall degree should not be treated as a universal theorem.

4. **Prime-field covers, Jacobian representations, and isogeny transport.** Conversations `49ed070e-eeaa-46f4-bafe-3af132e055ae`, `ce127e8d-13d1-4f58-ac88-15e038b856b9`, and `c68795ee-9d88-4ba7-ae79-4a2d483729dd` raise the highest-upside structural question for prime fields. Their existence claims do not yet supply an executable target map, preserved prime-order subgroup, cheaper destination DLP, or charged setup/transfer/recovery. A prime extension degree excludes a *proper intermediate subfield* in the particular [Joux-Vitse tower route](https://eprint.iacr.org/2011/020.pdf); it does not exclude every cover or alternative representation. The cheapest falsifier is one exact curve/subgroup and explicit map with independent group-law replay, then a destination relation-yield/rank test with map costs and matched rho. Class: **speculative transfer hypothesis**. The [endomorphism rules](../../../../docs/endomorphism-rules.md) govern this distinction.

5. **Multiple targets and hardware acceleration.** Conversations `f16172d4-6740-4fce-93a8-997dda6936db`, `9989b36c-cace-48bc-b5ef-92f946a543f6`, and `33371c38-2625-4bfe-8f59-08cf777b3faa` discuss cached algebra and GPU/TPU execution. These can lower amortized or primitive cost, but require setup divided by the actual number of reused targets and a separately reported one-target result. They do not themselves establish a better mathematical relation mechanism. Class: **secondary amortization and engineering**.

## Decision and unresolved work

First run the fixed-useful-base S3/rank cost experiment, then the N41 target-coupled family-elimination gate. Keep early-pruning solvers as a parallel mathematical design track and prime-field transfers as an explicit-map track. No experiment was run for this assessment. The next experiment requires a versioned protocol with frozen inputs, a same-point rho reference, success and stop conditions, and complete phase accounting. Existing private conversation text is context; only committed receipts can promote a finding.
