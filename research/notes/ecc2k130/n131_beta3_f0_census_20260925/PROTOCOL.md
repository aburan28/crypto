# Frozen protocol: exact beta-3 F0 size and signed-column census

Status: **preregistered, no pilot or full census outcome yet**. This study
follows the n=131 structural and sampled lift gate in [PR #762](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/762), the complete n13/n19 projected corpus in [PR #767](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/767), and the semantic reference in [PR #770](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/770). Its sole n=131 outcome is the exact number of rational F0 abscissae and distinct canonical signed `[4]`-projected F0 columns. It measures neither decomposition support nor rank, solver time, end-to-end cost, rho ratio, or an ECC2K-130 log.

## Model, exact definitions and proof obligations

The fixed n=131 model is `E: y²+xy=x³+1` over `F2[x]/(x^131+x^13+x²+x+1)`, beta=3, m=6,d=21, q=680564733841876926932320129493409985129. The factor abscissae are exactly `V0=span_F2{beta^(2^(6j)):0≤j<21}`. The input and 64 literal sample masks are frozen in `INPUT.json`; the n13 and n19 controls use exactly the beta-3, d=2 models from #767. A mask is enumerated once in Gray ordinal order `g(t)=t xor (t>>1)`, `0≤t<2^21`, updating x by one basis vector at `ctz(t)` for t>0. The 32,768-mask pilot is the first Gray ordinals and is a diagnostic prefix, not a density estimate.

Let **L** be the exact number of nonzero x in V0 admitting rational points. For nonzero x, `z=y/x` obeys `z²+z=x+x^-2`, so it is liftable iff `Tr(x+x^-2)=0`. Trace is unchanged by squaring, giving the implementation test `Tr(x)+Tr(x^-1)=0`; `Tr(x)=parity(mask)` because the normal beta has trace 1. Every such x has exactly two rational points `(x,y)` and `(x,y+x)`. The zero mask has x=0 and the single point `(0,1)`, with `[4]P=O`, and is excluded from L. The two x=1 points have order four and would also project to O, but x=1 cannot occur in V0: the 131 conjugates are an F2 basis and sum to `Tr(beta)=1`, while V0 uses only 21 of the 131 basis coordinates. The code must independently assert the rank, conjugate sum, span nonmembership and zero observed x=1.

For x≠0,1, the sign-independent doubling identities on this curve are `u=x([2]P)=(x+x^-1)²` and `v=x([4]P)=(u+u^-1)²`. The canonical signed column is the pair `{[4]P,-[4]P}`, identified exactly by its nonzero abscissa v. **C_nonzero** is the number of distinct v keys from liftable nonzero F0 x. It counts ± pairs, not individual group points and not independent log unknowns or matrix rank. The identity O is one additional zero column and never a logarithm unknown. The full physical F0 point count is `1+2L`, while its distinct projected point count is `1+2*C_nonzero`. Multiplicity `k` counts distinct x mapping to one nonzero signed column. Since rational ker[4] has four points, a signed column has at most four such x preimages (up to eight physical points), hence `ceil(L/4)≤C_nonzero≤L`. Every multiplicity must be in 1..4; zero/torsion is kept separate. Extra Frobenius or relation dependencies may reduce useful logarithm rank below C_nonzero.

The rational 2-torsion point `(0,1)` maps a nonzero P's x to `x^-1`. If both x and x^-1 lie in V0, they must map to the same signed column. Count that guaranteed collision subset and check its group-law identity on frozen samples. It need not explain every collision. The independent small-model control must enumerate all four F0 coefficient masks for each of n13 and n19, reconstruct both rational lifts, verify each `[4]` group-law x against the rational formula and compare the resulting signed-column partition with all four rational E[4] torsion translates. It must explicitly check the out-of-factor x=1 torsion branch. The 64 frozen n131 masks must be checked with independent field inversion, half-trace rational lifts, on-curve equations and twice-doubling group law wherever liftable. These checks precede admission of the full count.

## Stages, costs, independent replay and preservation

The producer uses the parent #762 bit-polynomial field implementation for structural setup, Euclidean inverses, and exact full enumeration. It records one SHA-256 digest of canonical `mask,x,lift,column` rows, independent 8,192-row chunk digests/counts and the final multiplicity histogram. It retains compact raw chunk records instead of committing approximately two million per-mask rows; all rows are reproducible from the frozen source and full independent replay. The verifier reconstructs each Gray mask x from its coefficient bits rather than the producer's incremental update, uses its own polynomial-division Euclid inversion and field arithmetic, recomputes every trace and projection key, and compares the row digest, L, C_nonzero, zero/torsion counts and multiplicity histogram. Different point-lift and group-law code checks the small full censuses and frozen n131 sample. A replay that disagrees on any deterministic field fails; wall, CPU and RSS are machine observations only.

Run the pilot producer and independent pilot replay first. Its hard producer cap is 90 s and the verifier cap is 150 s; both have a 768 MiB sampled/ru_maxrss cap. The full stage is admitted only if the pilot completed, `64*pilot_total_wall≤1200 s`, and its high-water RSS≤256 MiB. Otherwise preserve a censored full-stage decision without modifying the algorithm. The full producer cap is 1,800 s and its independent verifier cap is 3,600 s, each with a 768 MiB cap. The runner has 30 s grace above each child wall cap for cleanup, samples child RSS with psutil 7.2.2 at 50 ms and preserves command, UTC start/stop, exit status, sampled RSS, child stdout/stderr SHA-256, producer phase operation counts, chunk receipts and partial failures. The counts and exact identities are primary; wall, CPU and RSS only assess feasibility. All setup, inversions, trace tests, x projection, hashing/serialization, small controls and independent replay are charged or reported separately. A cap or invariant failure is a retained negative/censored result; there is no unpreregistered size reduction or replacement input.

`FROZEN.json` pins exact source, parent source and `INPUT.json` SHA-256 values. The focused workflow independently pins the `FROZEN.json` SHA-256 literal before any pilot or full outcome, runs pre-outcome self-tests, and later replays committed compact evidence without rerunning a timed producer. Pilot and full runs occur only after a draft PR with this protocol, source/input freeze and green hash-only CI. A correction after failure retains the original failed receipt, updates the source version and reruns freeze-only CI before another measured attempt. The next decision is whether the exact L and C_nonzero materially revise the nominal `2^21` column proxy from #762; it is not an n131 factor-base support decision.
