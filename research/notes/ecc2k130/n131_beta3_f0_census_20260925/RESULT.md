# Exact n=131 beta-3 F0 cardinality and signed `[4]` columns

**Decision: the exact census passed, and the old `2^21` signed-column proxy is about twice the actual count.** The frozen beta=3, m=6, d=21 abscissa space contains `2,097,152` masks. Exactly **1,048,134 nonzero x** admit rational points. Each gives two signs, the zero mask gives `(0,1)`, and x=1 is absent, so F0 has **2,096,269 physical points**. Their `[4]` images have **1,048,134 distinct nonzero canonical ± pairs** and O: **1,048,135 signed columns including O**. The projected point set, before sign folding, has `2,096,269` points. Every nonzero signed pair has exactly one F0 x preimage; bins 2, 3 and 4 are zero. A second implementation independently reconstructed all 2,097,152 mask rows and matched the producer's exact row digest and multiplicity histogram.

This is an **accounting correction**, not an attack improvement. The nominal `2^21=2,097,152` F0-column proxy in [PR #762](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/762) is `2.0008434` times the measured nonzero canonical signed-column count. It happens to be close to the physical point count, which is only 883 below `2^21`. The exact count does not measure independent relation rank, a PDP target hit rate, solver cost, rho cost, or an ECC2K-130 discrete logarithm. Additional Frobenius or row dependencies can reduce useful rank below this column count.

## Why these columns are exact

The fixed model is `E:y²+xy=x³+1` over `F2[x]/(x^131+x^13+x²+x+1)`, beta=3, with F0 basis `beta^(2^(6j))` for `0≤j<21`. The 131 beta conjugates have full rank and XOR to `Tr(beta)=1`; that all-coordinate representation proves x=1 cannot lie in F0. The zero x contributes only `(0,1)`, a 2-torsion point whose `[4]` image is O. For nonzero x, writing `z=y/x` gives `z²+z=x+x^-2`; rational liftability is exactly `Tr(x+x^-1)=0`. The full nonzero lift fraction is `1,048,134 / 2,097,151 = 0.4997894763`, while #762's fixed 16,384-mask descriptive sample reported 8,198 lifts. The sample was neither used to select this base nor substituted for the exact count.

For x≠0,1, `u=x([2]P)=(x+x^-1)²` and `v=x([4]P)=(u+u^-1)²`. v is sign-independent and identifies one canonical pair `{[4]P,-[4]P}`; O is separate. Since the rational `[4]` kernel has four points, one canonical signed pair has at most four distinct rational x preimages. The producer checked that upper bound for every insertion, and the independent verifier found the same all-singleton histogram. Adding `(0,1)` sends x to x^-1; no pair of distinct full-F0 x values related by this map occurs here. That collision control does not assume other 4-torsion translates exhaust possible coincidences; the global v-key census resolves them.

The producer updated x by one basis vector per Gray ordinal and used Euclidean field inversion. The verifier rebuilt each x from its coefficient bits, used a separate polynomial-division Euclid inversion and squaring implementation, and compared the SHA-256 digest of every ordered `mask,x,lift,column` row. Both digests are `b8b03e28e783ebec12be04c82542d74448896e3a128c0a5784a77a0a688da30d`. Complete n=13 and n=19 F0 mask controls independently checked both rational signs, actual `[4]` group law and all four rational 4-torsion translates: they have respectively 5/7 physical points and 2/3 nonzero canonical ± columns. All 64 frozen n=131 sample masks were checked with independent half-trace rational lifts and twice-doubling group law; 32 nonzero samples were liftable. These controls certify the rational formula used for the exact all-mask enumeration without pretending to perform two million full affine point doublings.
