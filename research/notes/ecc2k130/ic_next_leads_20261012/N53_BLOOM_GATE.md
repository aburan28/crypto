## Hypothesis and fixed comparison

With `KIC_S3_ROOT_BATCH=1` in both arms, enabling the exact root-table Bloom filter can reduce unsuccessful hash-table probes in rank and target PDP enough to lower full N53/K220 cold and online work. The sole algorithmic variable is `KIC_ROOT_BLOOM=0|1`. Fix `KIC_PAIR_SYMMETRY=0`, the constructed 23,320-point/220-column base policy and rank stream, and all other options. Use curve `EC1N53Ce0hb097de99be9a` and prime subgroup order 21,044,858,204,113. The reference is the same-point signed-Frobenius strong-rho runner with its setup and scalar check.

Before timing, use the native public hash-to-curve fixture and independent native scalar replay to select three new, distinct subgroup points disjoint from all committed N53 targets. Commit verifier-only scalars separately from point-only runner inputs. Fix three rank seeds and three rho seeds, schedule one control, one Bloom candidate and one rho cell for each target/seed pair, and rotate arm order. Keep one worker per fresh process, 90 s wall and 16 GiB observed RSS caps, mirroring the preceding panel. Any failed cell remains in the record; do not replace a target or seed.

Instrument the root table in native Rust to count lookups, Bloom definite negatives, Bloom false positives, exact table probes, construction insertions, allocated bytes and hash operations. Count both setup and rank/target calls; preserve prefetch work. Verify identical base dumps, table contents, rank transitions, accepted relations, factor logs, target witnesses and scalars under independent replay. A difference in consumed relation or rank trace invalidates the paired exact-path comparison, though the outcome must still be retained.

Report each exclusive phase, field-operation counts, full process CPU/wall, online time, peak RSS, all nine paired ratios and target-block uncertainty. The screening gate is nine valid exact pairs, zero false negatives or bad relations, median candidate/control cold at most 0.95, online at most 0.90, and candidate/control peak RSS at most 1.10 in every pair. A miss is a measured rejection of this configuration at this rung. The full operation-normalized `S` and floor ratio stay unset until field operations, normal-basis transforms, memory work and rho operations are calibrated into one unit. Host isolation and second-host replay remain separate admission gates for a CPU or cross-method claim. Preserve setup/online and single-target/multiple-target estimands separately.
