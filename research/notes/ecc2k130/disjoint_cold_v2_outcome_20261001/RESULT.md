The unit is Linux `wait4` child user+system CPU seconds for a **fresh complete process**. Each compact process builds its factor base, obtains full rank, solves the linear system, descends all public Q, checks them, and writes its output. Rho is a fresh 32-walk signed-Frobenius Kuhn–Struik normal-basis batch process on the identical Q block. For each block, the compact cost is the geometric mean of its A and B processes, divided by that block's rho process; the table reports the median and preregistered two-sided log-ratio t interval across 20 or five independent blocks. The per-arm CPU medians are descriptive and need not divide to the paired median. Each cell had its own Ubuntu x86-64 runner (Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C or AMD EPYC 7763/9V74), so absolute seconds across cells are **not** a cross-degree fit. Wall time is not used because the runner's 0.2-second polling coarsens short arms.
