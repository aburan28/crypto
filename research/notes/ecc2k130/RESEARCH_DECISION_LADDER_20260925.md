# ECC2K-130 index-calculus decision ladder (2026-09-25)

This is the current execution order after the compact-orbit, isogeny, and rotated-base measurements. It supersedes the unfinished gates in [the earlier decision note](NEXT_EXPERIMENT_DECISIONS_20260925.md); that note retains its preregistered reasoning. [The scoreboard](../../../docs/index-calculus-scoreboard.html) is the ledger for comparable end-to-end measurements. Every new or negative experiment needs a frozen protocol, raw evidence, independent replay, and a PR under [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md). Open-PR results are provisional until reviewed and merged. A tuple-count ceiling, toy support result, or solver-stage time is not an ECC2K-130 discrete logarithm or an attack-speed estimate.

## Next experiments and stop rules

A parent merge alone does not release a stacked draft: its post-merge freeze,
exact-head CI, and pre-outcome peer review must also pass before the run.

1. **Complete toy correctness and compare actual representation cost.** After #786 merges, run draft [#787](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/787) on the same 32 targets across CryptoMiniSat, Kissat, and CaDiCaL. Its frozen promotion rule requires charged sparse/dense portfolio ratios below 0.90 in both exporter orders and all three engines, with every SAT model independently lifted and status matched. After #790 merges, run draft [#794](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/794) for the other 22 exact negative branches. A timeout or over-cap is unknown, never UNSAT. These gates decide toy representation and correctness, not attack speed.

2. **Measure explicit-state growth, then export and test the symbolic full-point circuit.** After #786 merges, run draft [#795](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/795) on n19 with its frozen 100,000-state-group, 500,000-pair, 250,000-path, 20-MB CNF, 180-s export, and 512-MiB RSS caps. Its independent verifier covers all 117,649 signed tuples and 33 targets. Even a passing n19 table does not imply n131 feasibility. Reviewed open [#796](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/796) independently matched all 8,011 affine n13 points to a full-point μ4 chart, while correctly refusing n131 admission at raw chain floors of 1,048/1,179 bits for unequal m9/m10. Merged [#802](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/802) built a solver-neutral multiword XOR/AND DAG for complete O, inverse, doubling, and distinct-x addition. Its independent archive replay passed all 576 n2/n3 point-triple rows, 2,560 valid-point model evaluations, 3,264 invalid-role assignments, and a 920-input limb-decoding control. These are representation checks, not a CNF/SAT solver or an n131 PDP. Stacked draft [#804](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/804) freezes the next single-edge DAG-to-CNF gate: independently lift solver models and check negative proofs on a bounded toy panel, then use an n131 single-edge export smoke for width and size only. Its outcomes remain held pending a post-merge source freeze, exact-head CI, and peer review. A following composed-chain gate must pass the fixed #781/#785 32-branch rotated toy panel and resource caps before an n131 m10 PDP is admitted. A single-edge n131 export does not establish a complete m10 PDP or attack cost.

3. **Test placement only where support can move.** Run #797 only after its exact pinned #793 head is merged and its own frozen CI and peer review pass. Compare the fixed equal-(N,B) pairs (0,4), (0,5), (4,5), and (1,2) using all q target multiplicities and the same 64 Qs. Advance only if a matched pair differs by at least 1,309 supported targets and at least 2,618 in support symmetric difference. Otherwise retire this b18-placement support lever at n19. The first-witness row rank and second moment remain diagnostics.

4. **Charge portfolio and phase selection as algorithms.** Reuse #779's frozen bases and same-Q training/holdout split, replacing archive lookups with an admitted complete PDP solver. Compare the frozen four/five-beta orders with #807's one-base spread phases at K=4 and K=8 on the same 64 Qs. Charge cold base/log construction, each Frobenius target transform, every positive and negative solver/export/proof attempt, model lifting, independent relation rank, linear algebra, point-only descent, and scalar verification. Compare the identical batch against automorphism-aware batched rho. An easier-PDP selector enters only after per-instance solver costs exist; its feature must vary over subgroup targets and pay for abstentions and wrong choices. Stop expansion if coverage gains disappear in total verified-log cost.

5. **Admit solver families on the same complete system.** Gray-code/FES, WDSat, CryptoMiniSat, MiniSat, msolve, F4/F5, and crossbred can be timed only after an exporter, parser, and independent model/proof checker implement the same field, variable order, full-point relation, and resource budget. Compare first valid witness on supported targets separately from complete certified refutation of negatives. Ready-for-review [#801](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/801) restores the historical solver-admission replay from pinned source bytes and separately confirms that the current generic direct/reused Gröbner builders report the 88-bit n19/m6/d2 layout as unsupported and inconclusive. The current u64 symbolic FES/ANF/Gröbner wrappers do not admit n131 m9/m10 merely because numeric curve arithmetic is multiword. Stop any adapter that truncates variables or labels over-cap as UNSAT.

6. **Return to the public n131 instance only after end-to-end gates.** Use [#771](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/771)'s literal ECC2K-130 P/Q fixture. Price useful signed columns, relation acquisition, independent full rank, sparse linear algebra, target descent, maps if used, and verified scalar recovery. Demonstrate cold verified-log economics on fully solvable smaller rungs against same-Q automorphism-aware batched rho. State explicit n131 transfer assumptions and a bound for every stage. A faster SAT branch, larger tuple count, or different endomorphism discriminant does not satisfy this gate.

The active Codex goal remains a reproducible, PR-reviewed ECC2K-130 index-calculus feasibility verdict: either a measured end-to-end crossover with explicit n131 transfer assumptions, or a quantitative no-go for tested routes with the next evidence-ranked direction. The public challenge logarithm has not been recovered.
