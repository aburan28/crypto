The single algorithmic variable is `KIC_PAIR_SYMMETRY=0` versus `1`, with
`KIC_RANK_PROBE_CAP` absent in both arms. The control calls S3 for every
ordered `(left,right,relative)` pair. The candidate calls S3 once per pair
orbit under `(left,right,relative) -> (right,left,-relative mod 53)`, stores
the swapped root alias at its original scan position, and skips only the
second member encountered by each circular target-query scan. The scan origin
is computed from the same public point or rank seed in both arms. The two
arms therefore share the same ordered index states and first inserted
root-table witness. The candidate must keep the exact root-table key/value
map, full 220-row relation sequence, and final target scalar. The small-field
index-key unit test and archived development controls check this before the
held-out Q is generated.
