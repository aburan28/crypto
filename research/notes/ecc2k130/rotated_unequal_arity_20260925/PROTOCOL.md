## Fixed construction and question

Use the public ECC2K-130 model `E:y²+xy=x³+1` over `GF(2^131)` with polynomial `z^131+z^13+z²+z+1`, beta `3`, and prime subgroup order `q=680564733841876926932320129493409985129`. Let `b_j=beta^(2^j)` for `0≤j<131`; independently check their GF(2) rank is 131. For each listed `m`, write `131=m*d_low+r`. Assign **high** dimension `d_low+1` to slots `i=0..r−1` and **low** dimension `d_low` to slots `i=r..m−1`, with slot-i basis `b_(m*j+i)` for `0≤j<d_i`. The four frozen arms are m7 `(r=5, d_high=19, d_low=18)`, m8 `(r=3,17,16)`, m9 `(r=5,15,14)`, and m10 `(r=1,14,13)`, in that order. The basis indices must be exactly `0..130`, the slot dimensions must be as frozen, and the combined rank must be 131. Do not move high dimensions to other slot indices, change beta, or select an arm after seeing a count.

Inverse Frobenius sends every slot to one of two normalized spaces: `V_low=span(b_0,b_m,...,b_(m(d_low−1)))` or `V_high=V_low+span(b_(m*d_low))`. Thus `V_low⊂V_high`. Enumerate all `2^d` masks **separately** and exactly once for each low and high space. A nonzero x is rationally liftable iff `Tr(x+1/x)=0`; each such x contributes two physical points and one signed ±[4] projected column keyed by `v=(u+1/u)²`, `u=(x+1/x)²`. The zero x contributes `(0,1)` and projected O. Record L, `F=1+2L`, every projected x multiplicity up to four, and exact nonzero signed-column sets and counts `C_low,C_high`. Independently establish `columns(low)⊆columns(high)`; the **global normalized** signed-column count is exactly `C_high`, not `r*C_high+(m−r)*C_low`. This is a column *universe*, not relation-matrix rank or a count of solved logs.

The exact ordered physical tuple count is `N=F_high^r * F_low^(m−r)`. The necessary uniform-target support ceiling is `min(1,N/q)`. Use the **same frozen two-axis gate as #778**: `N≥q` and `C_high≤16384`. Choose the smallest m passing both only as a candidate for a later branch-complete implicit-PDP feasibility test; if none passes, stop this four-arm unequal family. Compare each arm with #778's balanced m10,d13 result (`F=7977`, `C=3988`, `F^10/q=1.5329446704`) after #778 is merged and verified. Count raw affine-chain variables as `131 + 131*(m−2)` for the 131 factor bits and `m−2` intermediate x states, before exceptional branches or solver auxiliaries. The tuple product and C count say nothing about actual supported target sets, independent relation rank, SAT/Gröbner cost, an ECC2K-130 challenge log, or IC/rho speed.

## Independent replay, caps, evidence

For both spaces of each arm, the producer uses Gray-order x updates, #762's Euclidean field inversion, a precomputed trace mask and per-8192-row SHA-256 chunks. It saves exact counts, multiplicity histograms, a full `mask,x,lift,v` row-stream digest and normalized column-set digest. The independent verifier reconstructs every x directly from natural coefficient bits using its own bit-serial product and polynomial-division inverse, rederives trace and both x-doubling formulas, and checks all row/chunk digests and exact counts. For 64 SHA-selected ordinals per low/high space it also constructs a rational y lift by half trace, checks the full curve equation and two group-law doublings against v. Check low-column set containment by **actual computed sets** in both programs. These 512 sampled semantic checks, including group-law checks on the rational-lift cases, supplement rather than replace the full arithmetic mask replay.
