`s5.py` composes four copies of the #802 complete `P+Q=R` Boolean relation in one shared DAG, with roles `F0..F4,S2..S4,SUM`, independent slope words and 25 exactly-one selector bits. At n13 the primary width is `9*(2*13+1)+4*13+25=320`. Each selector fixes *every bit* of that slot's signed full point, and `SUM` is the exact `Q+T_torsion` full target, not the torsion point alone. `query.py` appends exactly 27 `SUM` pin units for one of the frozen 32 labels and checks the resulting ordinary Tseitin DIMACS by an independent parser. An O prefix, inverse, doubling, generic addition, both rational signs and exact target O are represented by the parent full-point relation; no affine-prefix restriction or x-only equality is permitted.

`admission5.py` requires an exact complete SAT model, every parsed CNF clause and auxiliary Tseitin gate, exactly one selected factor per slot, canonical full points, all four independently recomputed group sums, determined slopes, and the correct pinned label. Its separate group law uses the #767 bit-serial/Fermat implementation, rather than the Euclid-inverse witness constructor.
