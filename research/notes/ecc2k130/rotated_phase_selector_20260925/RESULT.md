# n19 Frobenius-phase selector: exact projected support and oracle probes

**Decision: the preregistered primary cap-8 priority screen passes; cap-4 dominance fails.** On the fixed beta-3 six-slot rotated base, the spread order \(s_t=5t\bmod19\) supports 128,751 of 130,873 projected subgroup targets after at most eight phase queries, with 276,497 free perfect-oracle probes over the complete target population (mean 2.112712 per target). This exceeds the frozen cap-8 support threshold 126,265 and stays below \((9/4)q=294,464.25\) probes. At cap 4 it supports 121,419 targets in 253,355 probes, missing the fixed four-beta comparator by 432 targets and costing 3,528 more oracle probes. The predeclared contiguous control is worse at both caps. This result admits a separately frozen *charged complete-S6 solver comparison*; it is not a solver-time or ECDLP speed result.

The source is the independently replayed #767 n19 beta-3 projected histogram: 62,389 supported targets including O from 117,649 labelled six-tuples, with six seven-point physical factors and three nonzero canonical signed \([4]F_0\) columns. Both phase orders use the **same** factor product and columns. The target transform is \(\tau^sQ=[41811^s]Q\); the archived hit for \([4]\tau^sQ\) is undone in the row by \(41811^{-s}\). No new factor-log unknown is introduced by a phase. The full 19-phase support is 130,645, leaving 228 exact misses: exactly 12 complete nonzero 19-point Frobenius orbits. Those orbits cannot be reached by any phase of this fixed beta-3 base. This is an exact structural limit of the one-base selector, rather than the 19-fold independent-hit estimate that would ignore orbit correlations.
