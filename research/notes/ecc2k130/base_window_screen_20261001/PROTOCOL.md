## Question and boundary

On the registered Koblitz rung `icv1-f2m41-tm2308219-7f48b14a`, can **any one** of four deterministic, disjoint, equal-useful-size 255-column bases reduce complete W64 compact CPU enough to approach a same-Q strong rho reference? The existing [orbit-disjoint cold result](../disjoint_cold_v2_outcome_20261001/RESULT.md) is 1.3137× rho at 255 columns and 1,024 Q per block, with a 23.9% complete-CPU gap to its median rho. The [phase admission](../selector_phase_admission_20261001/DECISION.md) says a second full index would require about 94% lower rank-plus-target cost to break even under an optimistic CPU-share proxy. The degree-7 [charged two-base selector](../m3_base_selector_20260930/RESULT.md) also failed after paying for two scans and two exact scores. The [public-Q coordinate predictor](../pdp_difficulty_features_20261001/RESULT.md) failed its frozen holdout gate. These results justify screening **base variation with one index per arm** before inventing another predictor.

This screen does **not** implement a practical selector. Its per-block hindsight minimum over four fully measured policies is an optimistic lower envelope that pays each policy's own scan and one index but pays **zero** selection overhead. It is deliberately favorable to the base-window family, and is not a measured attack policy, `S` value, solver breakthrough, or ECC2K-130 speed claim. If even that envelope stays above rho under the frozen eligibility rule, a nonnegative-cost selector restricted to these same four implementations cannot cross on the observed blocks. Other bases, shared-scan implementations, PDP solvers, degree-263 descendants and n=131 remain open.

## Frozen analysis and stop rule

For block `b`, let `C[w,b]` be the geometric mean of window `w`'s two **generator-plus-compact** charged CPU totals and `R[b]` the same-block rho CPU. Report each window's five paired `C/R` ratios and its A/B drift, with preregistered two-sided 95% log-ratio t intervals (`t₄=2.7764451051977987`). The timing gate requires the v2 quiet/PSI preflight, zero sampled contention above the fixed 0.10 other-CPU-second threshold, and for every window an A/B median in `[0.90,1.10]` whose interval contains one. If any timing gate fails, retain correctness but classify that timing row `CENSORED`; no best-of-four CPU conclusion is eligible.

Compute the **post hoc** per-block lower envelope `H[b]=min_w C[w,b]/R[b]`, with all four charged candidate policies available in that block. Show all five `H[b]`, their median, and a descriptive log-t interval. Classify `NO_WINDOW_OPPORTUNITY` only when every eligible `H[b]>1` and the lower endpoint of that descriptive interval also exceeds one. Classify `FIXED_WINDOW_CROSSOVER_CANDIDATE` only when all five blocks are eligible and a single predeclared window's upper paired 95% `C/R` endpoint across those blocks is below one; that requires a second independently frozen Q/host confirmation and a common operation-equivalent `S` before any speed claim. If median `H[b]<1` but no fixed window passes, classify `HINDSIGHT_VARIATION_ONLY`: the four fixed bases are the same in every block, so a target-blind rule cannot turn the per-block hindsight choices into a policy. A future randomized-candidate study would need an independently justified, frozen **pre-index** score and its complete charged selection cost. All other complete cases are `INCONCLUSIVE`.
