## Accounting, replay, and decision

For every attempted process retain monotonic cold wall, child user/system CPU, peak RSS, exact command/env, source and executable SHA-256, input/output hashes, stdout/stderr, exit/timeout and host. Compact reports regular/exceptional states, index entries, per-query S3 calls, partner roots, index hits, lift attempts, base/scan/index/query time, rank, and full point-log recovery; rho reports steps, table entries, collisions, all operation charges and setup. A timeout or failure is raw evidence. Independently time modular rank/base-log solve and point-log recovery; include these attack computations in the conservative IC upper cost. The primary per-block IC upper wall is the one cold 128-query training wall plus rank/recovery wall plus that block's cold point wall. The lower bound is the training child wall plus point child wall. Also report the actual three-block portfolio with training paid **once**, and the conservative per-block comparison that charges it each time. Rho cost is its complete cold batched process on the exact Q block; validation-only audit time is separate.

A fixed-stream favorable **same-host wall** observation requires **all three** paired blocks at the declared L to finish all Q logs correctly, with the conservative charged IC upper wall below each same-Q rho wall. A fixed-stream negative wall observation requires **all three** paired blocks complete, with the IC lower wall above rho in each. If any block censors or remains unrun, the declared L is censored: successful or slower earlier blocks do not create an all-block verdict. Anything between the two complete-block rules is unresolved. Report paired ratios and a descriptive interval, plus S3/field work, rho operation charges and RSS; these native counters can reveal regressions but are not a common group-addition-equivalent `S`. A calibrated operation crossover requires that common unit under `AGENTS.md`. Three blocks cannot give a distribution-free 95% paired sign/permutation interval excluding parity. A general crossover claim requires newly preregistered independent blocks and a 95% paired interval excluding parity; no distributional or asymptotic conclusion follows from this panel alone. No n=131 cost or challenge-log claim transfers from these rungs. Commit raw evidence, independent replay, decision note and canonical scoreboard in the same PR; merge only after CI and review.
