The verifier accepts the pinned CPU as a member of the recorded reservation. The Q, candidate binary source, runner, reference, order and acceptance rule are unchanged; the source lock pins the corrected workflow and verifier before the first measurement retry.

The workflow now waits 20 seconds and retries that same fail-closed preflight at most six times; each rejected attempt's log is retained, and a partially started panel is never retried or overwritten. The candidate source, Q, same-Q arms, caps, CPU-ratio decision rule and PSI threshold remain unchanged. This retry policy is committed and source-locked before any evaluation timing exists.
