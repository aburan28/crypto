# Protocol: degree-7 factor-base replication and bounded degree-263 smoke

Registered 2026-09-25 before the new relation measurements. This follows the one-stream negative degree-7 pilot [#716](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/716) and the exact normalized dual [#750](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/pull/750). No solver variant or base may be chosen after seeing holdout outcomes.

## Frozen toy construction

Use the exact #716 toy curve and field: F_(2^21) with modulus 0x200005, E:y²+xy=x³+1, #E=2,099,948, order-r subgroup r=421, cofactor 4,988, degree-7 twist kernel. Use #716's deterministic complete eight-line kernel search and its lexicographically first non-self line; require the same selected kernel and codomain b as its saved result. Use #716's seeded generator construction, but derive a **new** planted Q scalar by SHA-256 of `degree7-replication-secret-v1` modulo 421. The scalar is audit-only. Build a normalized dual using #750 and the first other distinct order-7 twist line; require both dual identities on the order-421 generator.

Two independent base-construction seeds are 2026092511 and 2026092512. For each seed and each of the source/leaf curves, scan x from the SHA-256 sequence `degree7-base-v1|seed|source-or-leaf|trial` reduced to 21 bits, skipping repeated x; inspect both rational points, multiply by cofactor 4,988, discard infinity and duplicate projected points. Fix the same four source signed-Frobenius orbit IDs for both constructions: the first four lexicographic canonical nonzero orbit representatives of the source order-421 subgroup. Retain exactly four useful points from each orbit, giving 16 per base and **identical orbit-occupancy vectors** for original and descendant-native bases. Classify native candidates by the charged dual pullback `[7^{-1} mod 421] dual(point)` and source signed-Frobenius canonicalization; the resulting pulled points are the pullback control. The transported base is phi(original). Cap each x scan at 4,096 trials and preserve an incomplete-base failure.

Enumerate the ten nonzero source signed-Frobenius point orbits, each expected to have 42 points; this orbit audit and any lookup construction are charged as common setup. Split sorted orbit representatives into first five (holdout A) and last five (holdout B). Independently generate 512 **accepted** targets per holdout from SHA-256 coefficient pairs `degree7-target-v1|holdout|trial|u-or-v` with u modulo 421 and v in 1..420. Reject infinity and targets outside the assigned orbit set; charge every rejected candidate, target generation and orbit screen to the next accepted attempt. Cap at 8,192 candidate draws per holdout. The two accepted streams must have disjoint source signed-Frobenius orbit IDs; no base or solver choice may inspect their hit outcomes.

## Paired relation/rank/cost gate

For each of two base constructions and both target holdouts, compare original, transported original, descendant-native and pullback bases on the **same** accepted (u,v) sequence. Build all 136 unordered two-summand pair sums per base; use the first witness only, maintain rank over F_421 for 17 unknowns, preserve every miss/dependent row, and solve/verify every base log and [k]G=Q at the first full rank. Preserve all 512 target/witness outcomes even after rank. Pair invariance must hold exactly for original/transported and native/pullback. An independent verifier recomputes full target points, witnesses, rank changes and final scalars without the runner's incremental rank tracker.

Report one cold field-multiplication unit including inversion internals, plus separate squarings/inversion calls/group additions/CPU/RSS. Charge field setup, complete kernel search, forward or dual setup as used, subgroup generator/Q, both source and leaf base scans, every cofactor projection, native dual pullbacks and orbit classification, target-stream rejection work and target generation prefix to rank, codomain generator/target generation, pair tables, relation misses, rank and recovery verification. Per-target phi(T) is an audit equality check, not candidate work; keep it separate. The degree-421 full inverse table from #716 is replaced by the certified dual, with its construction/evaluation charged. A failed full-rank cell remains a failure, not a censored success. Do not compute a speed ratio to ECC2K-130 or transplant toy costs to n=131.

## Conditional exact-target smoke

Run only if every toy cell reaches independently verified rank 17, all invariance and cost-ledger checks pass, and both independent base constructions differ. On the public F_(2^131) curve, use saved seed 20260924 lines [1,0] and [1,4] as the two non-conjugate descending representatives, plus the #750 certified dual. For each line, construct 16 distinct nonzero useful points by x-prefix scan and cofactor-4 projection on source and leaf, transport the source base and pull back the native base through `[263^{-1} mod r] dual`. Use 32 SHA-256-derived public (u,v) combinations of the actual challenge P,Q (v nonzero); form original and leaf targets and verify full map covariance. Build 136-pair tables for all four bases; record exact hits, misses, independent rank and every group identity, without expecting full rank. Charge all field/map/dual/base/cofactor/target/orbit-classification work and failures. Stop after 32 targets, 300 wall seconds, or 2 GiB peak RSS per line, preserving a feasibility/failure receipt. Pair-sum hit expectation under uniform subgroup targets is at most 32*136/r per arm; this is only a bounded m=2 smoke, not a Semaev PDP feasibility claim.

## Decision

A toy native advantage requires lower fully charged cost to the same verified rank on **both** disjoint holdouts for **both** base constructions, without a loss of independent useful rows or any failed check. Otherwise retain the negative/mixed outcome and rank the next primitive. The exact smoke tests representation and round-trip feasibility only. Full ECC2K-130 PDP yield, whole-ECDLP cost, rho crossover and speedup remain unset. Preserve source/input hashes, raw cases or compact lossless ledgers, all failures, commands, host, bounded wall/RSS, independent receipt, and an updated canonical scoreboard in the PR.
