# Protocol: round 2 of fiber-aware relation generation across a conductor gap

Frozen 2026-10-11, before the six sessions below ran.  Before freezing, two
smoke runs were made: one target on two curves of `e1_scale_h1568` and two
curves of `e2_glv_crater_floor`, outside the repository and never cited as
evidence.  They showed that the specs run and that every record verifies.
Their numbers are quoted in §E2 and §E1 only where marked "smoke".

Result class for every lead: **stage diagnostic and boundary control**
(AGENTS.md §3).  These are 26-bit and 22-bit synthetic prime curves with
subgroups of 2^14 to 2^22.  No deployed-curve claim, no key recovery, no
speedup unless the whole-pipeline ratio to the matched rho moves.

Round 1 is [`../isogeny_conductor_gap_ic_20261010/`](../isogeny_conductor_gap_ic_20261010/)
(PR #1647).  Its pairs (`pairs/prime_pairs.json`) are reused unchanged, and
every curve cited here is already registered.

## What round 1 left open

1. **The closed-table set-up asymmetry.**  Round 1 recorded, on the 26-bit
   D = −7, ℓ = 13 pair (h = 5408), oracle set-up of 6.56M additions on the
   crater, 20.3M on the floor and 5.72M on an *isomorphic model of the
   floor*.  An isomorphic model has the same group, so the factor 3.5
   between the floor and its model cannot be arithmetic.  The same pattern
   held at h = 1568 (crater 1.66M, floor 2.89M, model 1.66M).

   Cause, found on reading `ic_framework::fiber::cofactor_fiber`: the
   subgroup closure walks every multiple `jK`, `j < ord(K)`, of each new
   generator `K` and adds it to every old element.  This continues even after
   `jK` has fallen back into the old subgroup, where no addition yields a new
   point.  The cost is `ord(K)·|old|` where `[⟨old,K⟩ : old]·|old|` suffices.
   The excess depends on how many base points the sampler needs before it
   generates `E(F_p)[h]` (`fiber_samples`: 9 on the crater, 7 on the floor,
   1 on the model).  That count depends on the base points' x-coordinates,
   not on the group.  It is a **counting defect** in a set-up phase, not a
   conductor effect.

   Round 1's crater/floor verdict (degree, yield, cost and wall equal at both
   ends) is not changed by this: the defect was noise on top of a set-up
   charge that both ends pay.  But the closed and lifts arms' `S` on
   large-h curves is overstated by up to the excess.
2. **Whether a partial fiber beats the whole one.**  Round 1 found that the
   closed table wins overall only for h ≤ 25.  Its set-up is
   `pairs × |H|` additions, and above h ≈ 25 that set-up dominates.  Round 1
   only tried `H = E(F_p)[h]` (whole) and `H = {O}` (blind).
3. **The one place a conductor gap changes the group.**  Crater and floor
   are isogenous, so `#E` is equal.  But the group *structure* differs:
   - The floor has rank-1 ℓ-torsion (round 1's certificate: `(π−1)/ℓ ∉ End`).
   - The crater, for the pairs whose crater has full rational ℓ-torsion, has
     rank 2.

   Any method that uses subgroups of `E(F_p)[h]`, and not only their size,
   can see the gap.  The partial-fiber sweep is such a method.
4. **The other place: automorphisms.**  On D = −3 and D = −4 pairs, the
   crater has j = 0 or 1728 and an automorphism group of order 6 or 4.  The
   floor has order 2.  Round 1 ran the same prime-abscissa base on both ends,
   which ignores this.

## Leads

### A0. Fix the closure, under a new parameter, and re-measure

`fiber_gen=coset` closes the subgroup at the first multiple `jK` already in
it.  `fiber_gen=legacy` is the default and is byte-for-byte the round-1
procedure, so every committed session replays with its counts.  A
non-default build adds `fiber_gen_coset` and `fiber_scale` to the set-up
record; the default adds nothing.  Unit tests check two things:
- Both closures produce the same point set.
- The default build counts exactly what `cofactor_fiber` counts.

**Prediction P-A0.**
- Coset set-up on crater, floor and model agree within the variation of the
  closure term, which is now at most `h` additions plus `fiber_samples`
  scalar multiplications.
- Legacy reproduces round 1's asymmetry.
- Whole-method `S` falls by the excess on curves where the legacy floor
  needed several generators, and nowhere else.

Sessions:
- `a0_coset_prime26`: the 8 26-bit pairs with 98 ≤ h ≤ 1568, crater, floor
  and model.
- `a0_coset_h5408`: the h = 5408 pair alone, 2 targets and 2 rounds, so it
  fits between host reboots.

Arms: `rho.negation`, closed legacy, closed coset.

### E1. Partial fiber: the subgroup `[d]E(F_p)[h]`

`fiber_scale=d` (needs `fiber_gen=coset`) builds the fiber-closed table over
`H_d = [d]E(F_p)[h]`, generated by `[r·d]P` over every base point.  Its size
is not known in advance, so every base point is sampled.  A hit `Σ P_i = T + K`
with `K ∈ H_d ⊆ E(F_p)[h]` is a valid row exactly as before.

Crude model, with `π` the pair count of the table (≈ 1,000 for a folded
32-column prime-abscissa base), `s = |H_d|`, `N = h·r`, and `c` relations
needed:
- The set-up is `π·s` additions.
- A probe hits with probability `π·s/N`.
- So the relation phase is `c·N/(π·s)` probes.
- The total is minimised at `s* ≈ √(c·N)/π`.

For the h = 1568 pair (`r = 21589`, `c ≈ 33`) that gives `s* ≈ 33`.  For
h = 726 (`r = 229753`) it gives `s* ≈ 74`; for h = 200 (`r = 1393963`),
`s* ≈ 96`.

**Prediction P-E1.**
- Cost against `s` is U-shaped, with its minimum within a factor 4 of `s*`.
- The best partial fiber beats the whole fiber on every h ≥ 200 pair.
- It also beats blind.

The smoke on two h = 1568 curves already showed whole-fiber `S` ≈ 11,000
against ≈ 150 at `s = 8`, so the first part is not in doubt.  The minimum's
location is what the session tests.

**Prediction P-E1-gap.**
- At **equal `d`**, crater and floor can have different `s = |H_d|`.  The
  crater's ℓ-part is `Z/ℓ^a × Z/ℓ^b`; the floor's is cyclic.  The smoke
  shows `s = 32` on the crater and `224` on the floor at `d = 7`.
- At **equal `s`**, cost is the same at both ends.  The table is a function
  of the group and the base, not of `End(E)`.
- So the conductor gap is visible only as a different *menu* of subgroup
  sizes, not as a different cost at matched size.

A result where matched-`s` cost differs by more than run-to-run noise
between crater and floor would refute this and is the interesting outcome.

Sessions `e1_scale_h1568` (d ∈ {1,2,4,7,8,14,28,49}),
`e1_scale_h726` (d ∈ {1,2,3,6,11,22,33,66}) and
`e1_scale_h200` (d ∈ {1,2,4,5,10,20,25}).  Each uses 4 targets and 2 rounds
on crater, floor and model.

### E2. Floor → crater transfer with the automorphism fold

A DLP on the floor can be moved to the crater along the vertical
ℓ-isogeny chain of length `v_ℓ(f)`.  That is `v_ℓ(f)` Vélu steps of degree
ℓ, `O(v_ℓ(f)·ℓ)` field operations per point.  This is negligible here
(≤ 6 steps of degree ≤ 7) and is charged as zero in the comparison; the
README states the bound.  On the crater, `glv-orbit:group=auto` folds each
orbit of the order-6 (j = 0) or order-4 (j = 1728) group onto one column.
On the floor `group=auto` is negation only.

**Prediction P-E2.**
- The fold divides the column count needed per point by 3 (j = 0) or 2
  (j = 1728).
- But the MITM pair table is quadratic in *points*, and at a fixed 32 seed
  abscissae the folded base has 3× or 2× the points.  So set-up grows 9× or
  4×, while the relation phase shrinks by at most 3× or 2×.
- Expected: `ic-glv-auto` on the crater costs *more* than `ic-glv-neg`
  there, and more than either arm on the floor.  The transfer does not pay
  for a pair-table MITM.  (Smoke: crater `S` 247 auto against 37 negation,
  at h = 25.)
- Rho with the order-6 group would gain √3.  This repository has no prime
  rho over the order-6 group, so that gain is stated as the textbook bound,
  not measured.

**What would refute it:** `ic-glv-auto` on the crater below `ic-glv-neg` on
the floor on any pair.

Session `e2_glv_crater_floor`: all 12 D = −3 / −4 prime pairs (22 and
26 bits), crater, floor and model.  Arms: `rho.negation`,
`glv-orbit:size=32,group=auto`, `glv-orbit:size=32,group=negation`.  Every
IC arm uses `mitm-fiber:fiber=closed,negation_folded=1,fiber_gen=coset`.

### Deferred, and why

- **E4. 2-torsion-symmetrised summation polynomials, crater against floor.**
  Rational 2-torsion lets the summation polynomial be written in invariants
  of translation by a 2-torsion point.  A rank-2 crater has three such
  points; a rank-1 floor has one.  This needs a prime-field Gröbner solver
  over `F_p` with `p` ≈ 2^26, which the framework does not have (`f4-f2` is
  boolean).  Not run.
- **E5. Round 1's combined descent (A3) on a stable host.**  The spec is
  `../isogeny_conductor_gap_ic_20261010/specs/fiber_combined_n17_koblitz.json`.
  This host reboots every 20 minutes to 2 hours, and the A3 target needs
  more than one hour.  Not run here.

## Measurement and stop

- The sessions run one at a time in the order above, via `run_sessions.sh`
  under `--wait`.
- Each completed session is audited with `ecbench verify --replay 12`.
- A session cut by a reboot is moved to `interrupted/` with its log and
  rerun once.  A second cut is reported as such and not rerun.
- No build while a session runs.
- `S`, set-up group operations and wall time per record are read from
  `records.jsonl` and tabulated in the README.
- A lead stops at its first refutation.  No parameter is tuned after its
  session has been read.
