# X86 guarded direct F5 output unpack: frozen paired experiment

## Why this is distinct from the local ARM screen

The [earlier bounded local screen](../f5_bounds_free_unpack_20260930/RESULT.md) was exact but flat on Apple ARM64 and was rejected under its frozen local promotion gate. It did not measure x86. A separate, representative Rust 1.98.0 `x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu` release assembly probe at `-C target-cpu=x86-64` shows a `cmpq`/`jae` bounds branch inside the checked loop for every emitted term and no per-term bounds branch on the validated path. The [probe source](asm_probe.rs) and [assembly](asm_probe_x86.s) are archived with their command in [ASM_PROBE.md](ASM_PROBE.md). This is a code-generation hypothesis, not a measurement of the full crate or proof of a speedup. The frozen n24 degree-4 call emits about 13.7 million terms, so x86 may respond differently from Apple ARM64. This experiment tests that question directly; it does not reinterpret or replace the earlier rejection.

The reference is main commit `bff733dc58c5a4f6bbc011607df5266e5b450cec`: `matrix_f5_f2.rs` SHA-256 `bf35680f431ab7ac13a7615d334ec9b060217b84bdde36f03ce00319491d42da`, shared `gf2_elim.rs` `6fd3f10ef8e681490784c624e703eeb8fa887c5817ec9bdf34844e3f943c7f5c`, benchmark `f4_f2_bench.rs` `d40f9f0c76e5c56c9f4a52596b9edef41f321aa3d70cfb023c4ba21fdde70336`, isolation wrapper `ff59e53fd5566078ad493c8be0d06168699c4fd194bc881d99df19fa20e007ef`. The POPCNT candidate in #1033 has already failed its qualified gate and its runtime path is absent from this source.

## Candidate, workload, and accounting

`KIC_F5_X86_GUARDED_UNPACK=0` runs the exact-preallocated checked direct decoder. `=1` validates each row's column span and partial-word padding once, then loads column values without per-term bounds checks. The candidate is selected only on x86-64, when direct unpack is active, AVX-512 unpack is off, `n_vars >= 24`, degree is four and there are at least 4,096 columns. Malformed shapes fall back to the checked decoder. It keeps the same `Vec<F2BoolPoly>` output, term order, column representation, row count, rank and counted operations. Keep a portable CPU fallback and compare exact raw/canonical row fingerprints. A one-thread full F5 call includes criterion, build, elimination and complete output materialization.

Run the seven F5 cases in `examples/f4_f2_bench.rs`, primary `f5_n24_m24_d4`, seeds `0`, `badc0de1`, `5eed2026`, `f5c02a28`, with one and two Rayon threads separately. Fix selective echelon, fused row build, direct pack and unpack, branchless AVX2 strip, AVX2 row XOR, Gray-code table reuse and four tables; disable deferred-above, word batching, AVX-512 unpack and experimental table construction. Per seed and thread count, run one warmup per arm, five A/A reference pairs and five alternating A/B pairs: 22 processes with seven cases each. Preserve all outputs, failures, timeouts, OOMs, source and binary hashes, host/affinity, route, exact fingerprints, rank, terms, rows/columns, criterion/build counts, reduction word operations, exclusive phases and complete-call time. Release tests must compare valid sparse/dense/partial-word rows and malformed fallback; run them on the x86 host before timing.

Build and test through `tools/isolated_bench.py busy` under one lock. Reserve all SMT siblings for each timed block with `tools/isolated_bench.py reserve`, one CPU left free, Rustup environment passed through, ten-second preflight, other-CPU rate at most 0.10, PSI at most 5.0, zero eligible user threads and zero contended samples at a two-second period. Attempt each seed at most three times and select the **first** exact isolated block without reading its timing. Archive every refusal and partial call. If any seed lacks a qualified attempt, speedup is unknown. Do not compare absolute times across hosts.

## Frozen decision

Advance only if the one-thread frozen-primary complete-call paired reference/candidate median is at least 1.05, its exact 3,125-resample bootstrap 95% lower bound exceeds 1.02, no seed or smaller case has a complete-call median below its own A/A minimum, and the two-thread control has no such regression. A passing candidate needs a separately frozen default-on confirmation. A failing candidate has its tested patch, workflow and raw receipts archived and its runtime option removed. The user's further 2× goal requires a qualified one-thread full-call median **and** lower bound above 2.0 with all guards. These are solver-stage measurements, not IC one-target online DLP or rho speedups.
