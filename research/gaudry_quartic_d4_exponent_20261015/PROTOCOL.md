# Protocol: the `k = 4` exponent of index calculus on `E(F_{p⁴})`, measured

Registered 2026-10-15, before any ladder session below. Code frozen at
commit `0bea75038` (branch `claude/k4-measured-exponent-20261015`, stacked on
PR #1863's head `0da192814`, itself on PR #1792; neither is merged).
`target/release/ecbench` sha256 `8627cd29…0aa3`. Class: **measurement**
(exponents and constants of a known method; no new algorithm claim).

## Question

PR #1863 measured `S ≈ 1.7·10⁶` (1.39·10⁶× rho) for Gaudry/Diem index
calculus with the `D₄` oracle at one size, `p = 29`, and put the crossover
with rho at `2^{103}` (`h = 2`) by §11.16's model, which extrapolates the
exponents (relations `∝ q`, linear algebra and rho `∝ q²`). Here:

1. Measure, end to end in ecbench with full pricing (verified DLPs,
   replayed), the method and rho on a ladder of `p`, and fit `log cost`
   against `log p` per phase (relations, linear algebra) and in total.
2. Measure the single and double large-prime variations on the same
   targets and fit again.
3. From the measured fits, not the model, give the crossover with rho, with
   an interval, beside the model's figure, and say how far it extrapolates.

## What is new in the code (all opt-in; defaults unchanged)

- `linalg=wiedemann` (`ic_framework::linalg::Wiedemann`): the target's
  logarithm from a left-kernel vector of the relation matrix by Wiedemann's
  method (`O(m·nnz + m²)` multiplications mod `r`, each one `row_op`, the
  unit the eliminations count in; the same pricing, 1 `F_p` multiplication
  per `row_op`). The pipeline's default `incremental-gauss` is a dense
  elimination (`O(m²·k)`), which is not the `q²` linear algebra of the
  analysis; it runs on the same rows as a **shadow** and its `row_ops` are
  reported (`shadow_gauss_row_ops`, at the moment it pins the target) and
  not priced. `shadow_gauss_agrees` records that both give the same value.
- `gaudry-d4:…,large_primes=1|2` (`ic_framework::summand_large_prime`): the
  oracle is unchanged; a decomposition over the whole `fghr-y-line` base `V`
  is a full relation when all its columns are small, a partial with one (or
  two) large columns, and partials merge by elimination on the large columns.
  Small base: the `Y = 0` column and the first `⌈N^θ⌉` of the `N` other
  columns, `θ = 1 − 1/(n + ½) = 7/9` (one large prime) and `θ = 1 − 1/n = 3/4`
  (two), `n = 4`, scale 1. Merge work (`lp_merge_row_ops`) is priced as
  `row_ops` in the relations phase.

Unit tests: `wiedemann_pins_the_planted_target`,
`merged_relations_hold_and_carry_no_large_column`. Pilots before
registration (scratch, not evidence): one `p = 29` session (plain Wiedemann
and LP2 arms verified, `S` 1.44·10⁶ both) and a rho-only pass over the rungs
to read each curve's `r` and `h`.

## Analysis (frozen; `examples/gaudry_quartic_d4_exponent_fit.rs`)

Quantities per IC record, in GAE (ecbench's unit): `relations` (the phase,
solver work priced by count and, for LP arms, merge `row_ops`),
`linear_algebra` (Wiedemann), `other` (factor base + set-up + verification),
`total` (`cost.total_gae`). Derived and labelled as such: the shadow dense
elimination priced at the same `row_op` price
(`shadow_gauss_row_ops · la_gae/la_row_ops`), and a "Gauss-priced total"
`relations + other + shadow` (this is not an ecbench `S`). Rho: `total_gae`
of every distinct `rho-neg` record (deduplicated by workload and seed).

1. **Per-rung means** of each quantity over the rung's targets.
2. **Fits.** OLS of `ln(mean)` on `ln p` over the rungs: slope `β`, constant.
   Intervals: (a) 95 % from the OLS standard error (Student `t`, `n − 2`
   d.f.); (b) bootstrap, 10,000 replicates resampling targets within each
   rung, 2.5–97.5 percentiles. **The headline interval is the union of (a)
   and (b).** Also local slopes between consecutive rungs.
3. **Rho.** Fit `ln(mean gae)` on `ln p`, and `S_rho = gae/√r` pooled over
   all rho records (mean, bootstrap interval), with its slope against `ln p`.
4. **Crossover (measured).** The IC cost is the sum of the fitted power laws
   of its phases, `Σ A_k p^{β_k}` (relations, linear algebra, other); rho is
   `S̄_rho · √(p⁴/h)` for `h ∈ {2, 4, 16}` (`4 | h` on this family; `h = 2`
   only for comparison with the model's headline). `p*` solves equality;
   reported as `log₂ p*` and `log₂ p*⁴`, with the 2.5–97.5 % interval over
   bootstrap replicates (each refits every phase and `S̄_rho`), and separately
   with rho's own fitted power law in `p`. Repeated for the Gauss-priced
   total (dense elimination) and for each large-prime arm. If the cost never
   meets rho, that is the result.
5. **Model, side by side.** §11.16 as in #1863:
   `S/rho = √h (181 (p/4) C₄/(126 p²) + r∞/4)`, quoted from #1863
   (`2^{103.0}`–`2^{103.7}` at `h = 2`, `2^{105.4}`–`2^{106.5}` at `h = 4`).
6. **Reach of the extrapolation:** `log₂(p*/1009)` (or the top rung that
   ran) and the same in group size.

## Predictions

- **P1.** Every run verifies; every replay reproduces.
- **P2.** `ic-d4-w` relations slope `β_rel ∈ [0.9, 1.3]`.
- **P3.** Wiedemann linear-algebra slope `β_la ∈ [1.6, 2.4]`.
- **P4.** Shadow dense elimination slope `∈ [2.4, 3.3]`.
- **P5.** Rho: `ln gae` on `ln p` slope `∈ [1.6, 2.4]`; `S_rho` slope
  against `ln p` within `[−0.3, 0.3]`.
- **P6.** Crossover (`ic-d4-w`, `h = 4`) `log₂ p*⁴ ∈ [88, 115]`; with the
  dense elimination priced instead, no crossover below `2^{200}`.
- **P7.** `ic-d4-lp2` linear-algebra slope `∈ [1.1, 1.9]`; its relations
  slope above `ic-d4-w`'s; its total `S` at least `ic-d4-w`'s on every
  paired target at `p ≥ 101` (the measured range is relation-dominated).
- **P8.** `ic-d4-lp1`: total `S` at least `ic-d4-w`'s on every paired target
  at `p ≥ 61`.
