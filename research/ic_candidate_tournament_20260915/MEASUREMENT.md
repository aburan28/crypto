This is the schema-v2 admission work for the September 24 IC goal. Historical
rounds keep their sealed evaluator and original labels. New comparisons must
use canonical identities and eleven exclusive scientific phases. The existing
worker still emits combined intervals: **a correct solve and a closed legacy

## Identity

| Record | Identity and contents |
|---|---|
| Curve | `EC1N<n>C<tag>h<12hex>` hashes the exact field and curve/subgroup record, excluding the curve ID itself |
| Candidate | `IC1N<n>C<tag>fb<B>PDP<m><solver>RC<collector>LA<solver>TD<descent>ISO<0|1>h<12hex>` hashes the entire canonical method record |
| Workload | First 12 hex digits of its record hash; exact curve, targets, input law, seeds, target count, cache policy and resources |
| Run | `<candidate-id>W<workload-id>R<number>`; failures keep this same key |

`N` is field degree, never subgroup bit length. In this adapter `kb0` and `kb1`
denote coefficient `a2` of `y² + xy = x³ + a2*x² + 1`; the exact record remains
authoritative. Compact codes describe the full pipeline. `LA` is relation
elimination modulo the subgroup order; a Gröbner solver's internal matrix kernel
is recorded under `point_decomposition`.

Canonical JSON has sorted keys, compact separators and UTF-8 encoding. Integers
remain integers; nonintegral parameters are exact decimal strings. Floats,
filesystem paths, run seeds, timestamps and measurements are not method identity.
Factor-base construction seeds do belong to its exact construction. The
implementation section binds source-manifest and component content hashes plus
algorithm-affecting flags. The stage field sets are executable in `STAGE_FIELDS`.
They require explicit encoding, kernel, collection, matrix and descent policies.
Unknown stage wiring cannot enter the resolved-candidate constructor.

The independent oracle proves that the binary defining polynomial is
irreducible before accepting the curve, using the exact finite-field criterion
in [HAC Algorithm 4.69](https://cacr.uwaterloo.ca/hac/about/chap4.pdf). The test is
cross-checked against exhaustive polynomial trial division through degree nine.
Valid point arithmetic on a producer-supplied polynomial alone does not establish
that it defines the claimed field.

The candidate record retains unknown endomorphism conductors as `null`; a claimed
conductor or volcano level needs a proof digest. This adapter currently admits
`isogeny: "none"` only. `ISO1` requires an independently checked ordered route,
maps, kernel/subgroup and log transport, and charged transport; it is not enabled
by supplying an unverified neighbor or changing a string.

Unresolved methods stay catalog proposals (`Q<number>`, `candidate_id: null`,
measured costs null). A new family needs a checked adapter, not an exception to
the validator. A truncated digest collision must be extended; immutable writes
reject different full contents under an existing filename.

## Factor-base census

`factor_base_inventory` independently decodes every geometric point and expands
compact Frobenius representatives. Duplicate raw points and the identity are
rejected. It records both the raw set digest and the ordered digest: relation
indices depend on order even when support is unchanged.

For `column_convention=representative`, each point must lie in the prime-order
subgroup. For the historical `cofactor` convention the usable base is explicitly
the image `[h]B`; identity images and image collisions are recorded and removed
before counting `B`. This does not pretend that raw lifted points were already
subgroup points. The exact projection map and original geometry remain in the
candidate. The usable set digest is independent of its input ordering.

`fb<B>` is this actual distinct, nonidentity usable count **before** sign/Frobenius
folding. Nominal bound, geometric point count, killed/duplicate images and folded
column count are distinct fields. The checker regenerates the orbits and checks
the declared matrix columns. It never derives `B` from a nominal dimension.

## Costs, outcomes and failures

The exact exclusive phases are:

```
setup + isogeny + factor_base + precompute + queries + pdp + relation_check
  + matrix_build + relation_la + target_descent + recovery_check
```

The operation sum must equal an independently collected whole-process count in
one declared unit. Startup, encoding, reporting, cleanup and all failed attempts
must be assigned once. Recursive target PDP is charged to target descent once.
An intentionally absent phase may be zero with an explicit reason. Unmeasured
costs are `null`. Missing any phase or the process checksum leaves the complete
cost unknown even when the final DLP certificate verifies.

`legacy_ledger` preserves old combined intervals and their process checksum as
diagnostics, with all eleven scientific phases unknown. It cannot manufacture a
split. Native cold process nanoseconds are a separate run field, never substituted
for instruction counts. Warm workloads cannot acquire a cold total.

The default empirical question is one previously unseen public target. Its
headline wall time starts when target-dependent IC work begins, after reusable
preparation, and ends after independent scalar replay. `producer/timing.py`
checks the schema-3 native interval and its exclusive target phases. Rho includes
its target-dependent setup and solve on that exact point. Fixture generation,
process launch, input, reusable preparation and report serialization remain
outside the online interval. Retain the supplementary whole-process cold cost;
the goal's cold improvement gates are additional requirements. Profiled wall
time cannot stand in for native time, and repetition cannot stand in for fresh
target sampling. An incomplete result has no verified online speedup.

Ordinary-query diagnostics retain attempts, verified/proved-unsat/timeout/budget/
error/lift-rejected/unresolved outcomes, verified relation yield, distinct rows,
rank and columns. A bounded search returning no witness is `unresolved`, not
`proved_unsat`. Zero-yield cells remain records. Multiple rows from one query are
not multiple successful queries. Planted cases are separate correctness controls.
The full producer will additionally supply base/matrix memory, rank trajectory,
matrix digest, phase wall times and replay evidence; absent fields remain unknown.

Every run rechecks the admitted method and base, workload/resource binding, report
digest and independent certificate. Timeouts, OOMs, budget exhaustion, errors and
insufficient relations retain their immutable keys and null complete totals. No
individual run record can promote itself. Promotion additionally needs the frozen
paired panel, qualified IC/rho references, uncertainty, all-target completion,
confirmation and replay. Scoreboards must show incumbent, candidates and rho in
one unit, with complete totals, `S = operations/sqrt(r)`, applicable predeclared
floor/reference ratios and failures. Missing costs imply unknown speedup.
