Reading: a stable base folds the factor base, the relation count and the linear algebra by n
(the orbit length), a poly(n) factor that does not move the exponent.  Where the nearest stable
dimension is far from n/(m+1) or n/m, the fold costs more than it buys; where it is '-', it
does not exist and only an orbit-union base (materialised, not a subspace) is available.
