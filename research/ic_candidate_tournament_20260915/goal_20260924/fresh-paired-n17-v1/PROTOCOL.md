# Fresh paired n17 one-target campaign, version 1

Status: predeclared protocol only. No source publications, point card, target
registration, four-arm result or isolated-host receipt exists under this
protocol. The three earlier confirmation sets and every consumed controller
remain closed. A stage control or a partial natural panel is never a complete
one-target result.

## Entry gates and point custody

1. Finish the existing one-use, 512-query, one-million-conflict CryptoMiniSat
   natural preparation without retry or refill. Run its original frozen
   auditor once. Continue only if it admits all 512 source-bound attempts,
   independently replayed full rank 29/29 and all 29 factor-base logs. Keep
   every timeout, budget-inconclusive row and zero-yield cell.
2. Build the post-buffer-marked CMS binary only after that pass. Run the nine
   predeclared disclosed transport roles and their separate data-only audit.
   A failed role or audit blocks the SAT target arm; do not substitute the
   accepted cold CMS binary or the earlier 60-second failed control.
3. Publish complete, immutable source/build archives for **SAT, MatrixF5,
   incumbent IC and rho** before drawing a target. Retain a distinct external
   seal, full archive digest, source manifest, executable hashes, verified
   absence of the as-yet-undrawn point/seed and publication chronology for
   each. Historical disclosed fixtures may remain in source; they cannot be
   the fresh point. An independent
   checker must open the archives and verify their bytes; the four small
   descriptors supplied to the point-card generator are assertions, not proof.
   Freeze the source commit and all files included in its manifest at this
   point; a later documentation or code edit creates a different source and
   requires a new pre-card publication.
   The F5 worker may use a post-card target config in a new one-use capsule if
   the independently audited final source manifest and executable bytes match
   its pre-card publication. Its old target capsule is never reused.
4. Only then draw one random 64-bit seed with `icprog target-card-generate`.
   Its native `ecbench` `hash_to_subgroup_v1` workload fixes the public point,
   index zero, derivation counter, one target and no known scalar. Preserve
   the card's original bytes and hash. Recompute the native workload and
   independently verify exact curve, nonidentity subgroup membership and
   `[d]G = Q` for every returned scalar. Check that the point was absent from
   source publications and prior target records; a failed novelty check is
   retained and does not authorize redrawing a favorable point.

The card's `ecbench_workload_id` cross-checks the point across arms. It is not
the campaign workload ID: the latter is minted from the canonical curve,
target, input law, seed, one-target count, shared setup rule and resource
envelope. Candidate IDs require exact factor-base enumeration and source
manifests. Keep all IDs null until those records exist.

## Frozen four-arm execution

The proposed common point is supplied to F5, SAT, the registered incumbent
and the strongest compatible one-target rho reference. Freeze the arm order,
algorithm seeds, source/binary pins, resource envelope and timeout policies
before dispatch. Randomize or alternate arm order across independently drawn
future cards; never choose the order after seeing a result. For this first
card, every arm gets exactly one irreversible run, including failures.

The F5 target policy is its disclosed degree-three MatrixF5 search with at
most eight seeded queries and the 900-second outer watchdog, supplied with
the separately audited 512-query F5 log table. The proposed SAT target policy
is at most eight seeded queries, one million CMS conflicts and a 120-second
solver watchdog per query, with the same 900-second outer target watchdog.
The SAT policy matches the registered natural panel's solver budget; target
query and model receipts must stay tied to the exact source files. These are
policy bounds, not permission to retry or increase a limit after looking at
the fresh target. A one-use controller consumes each registration before
launch and retains original stdout, stderr, child PID/drain and attempt files
even when an arm fails or times out.

For the primary comparison, start each IC clock at the first target-dependent
query after reusable factor-base/index/log preparation and stop after scalar
replay. Charge every failed attempt to that target. The five exclusive
intervals are target query, target PDP, target relation check, target descent
and recovery check; their sum must equal recorded IC online time. Rho starts
at its first target-dependent walk computation and ends after independent
scalar replay on the **same point**. Record all target-dependent conversion
and validation separately, with an explicit decision on whether they fall
inside each method's online boundary. Use identical target and physical
resource limits, and retain target-independent preparation separately.

The first outcome is correctness and complete-solver admission, not a speed
claim. Original frozen per-arm auditors must verify source/build custody,
publication chronology, one-use claims, exact target/workload, every attempt,
ordinary natural yield, mathematical witnesses, full-point lifting, final
scalar and timing closure. A failed or unverified arm remains a row with
unknown speedup. On an ordinary or contended host, wall ratios remain
exploratory. Promote a CPU speedup only with the repository's isolated-host
receipt and paired run order, raw failures and uncertainty.

## Tournament decision

Keep the incumbent and a diverse frontier of F5, SAT, factor-base and
collector policies until complete, source-bound one-target evidence exists.
Use natural relation yield, rank per ordinary query, failed-attempt costs and
matrix work to decide which mechanisms deserve further trials; do not score a
planted decomposition or cost per successful row as if it were a full solve.
Reserve future fresh cards for both strong stage performers and distinct
families with plausible end-to-end paths. A local winner at one n17 point is
not a global IC winner. Report the full candidate × workload × run ledger,
uncertainty and resource envelope before changing the incumbent or claiming
an IC-over-rho result.
