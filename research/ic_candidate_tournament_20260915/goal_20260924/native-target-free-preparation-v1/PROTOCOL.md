## Fixed mathematical and algorithmic scope

six-dimensional standard polynomial subspace. Its plan has no target, field,
curve, point or imported-log parameter. It verifies subgroup 65587, cofactor 2,
generator (43693,23339), eigenvalue 17184 and modulus terms [0,3] before use.
It builds all 63 geometric points and 29 folded relation columns from production
constructors; the downstream independent gate reconstructs the complete fibres,

The native F5 entry constructs one reusable collector, dispatches actual
bounded MatrixF5 degree 3/node budget 8192 on three summands, disables direct

`probe_scalar` law. It does not describe this engine as a complete incremental
F5 algorithm. The CMS entry accepts source/model output from a separately sealed

expanded CNF assignment, and re-adds the lifted points. It never dispatches the
worker's Rust CDCL backend. The actual native CMS executable/exporter, budgets,
environment, watchdog and durable child receipts still require the new frozen
worker/controller integration.

Plans are strict JSON, schema 1, question
`native-target-free-preparation-n17-v1`, family `matrix_f5` or `cryptominisat`,
integer algorithm seed, and a fixed planned count between 1 and 512. The proposed
future natural panel remains 512 queries/seed 2026100311; this is not an

## Panel and row accounting

Each trial has a durable start callback and a completion callback. Every
negative, timeout, budget-incomplete and failure stays an attempt. False
witnesses, changed source/query inputs, invalid models or transport failures
stop with the original claim/source receipts and the completed prefix retained;
no query is retried or silently refilled. A completion callback failure retains
the completed row in the returned report. A killed process still needs the outer
controller's terminal/timeout and last-start records; a partial transcript must
not be presented as a complete panel.

Collection continues through the planned count; the production matrix filters
duplicates and verifies every offered witness. One final dense relation-LA
attempt derives and group-replays the logs after collection. Earlier full rank
does not stop the query panel. The independent auditor reconstructs novel rank
per query and distinguishes repeated/dependent rows; producer row count is not
a rank claim. No known logs or target scalar are read by the preparation path.

Every initialization, query and final-LA window has exclusive integer phase
clocks whose sum must equal its wall interval. Controller/observer work between
windows is charged to setup so the aggregate preparation interval also closes.
Initial matrix construction enters `matrix_build`. The production row consumer
keeps witness verification under `relation_check` and enters a nested
`matrix_build` scope when inserting each verified, nonduplicate row. These
clock boundaries change accounting only, not arithmetic, filtering or row order.
Final report rendering and publication are outside that declared interval and
need separate outer receipts. Unentered phases remain null in the window
records. These are preparation stage diagnostics, not a complete-DLP total or
online timing. Missing operation counts remain null.

The producer emits the strict mathematical input required by
`icprog ordinary-preparation-audit` alongside original per-query evidence,
matrix, log report and phase windows. `source_bound_execution_admitted`, fresh,
headline, promotion and full-goal flags stay false; online wall and speedup stay
null until independently admitted by the complete frozen runtime/comparison
workflow. No self-reported source receipt establishes custody.
