# Generic supplied-target and online-interval controls

Follow PR 799's query accounting with an explicit one-public-target interface.
Measured IC/rho jobs must accept exactly one canonical, on-curve, nonidentity
point in the declared toy subgroup. Seeds are optional provenance; only fixture
mode generates points. The worker remains restricted to odd field degrees 5..31.

Start IC's online interval after factor-base logs, indexes, column maps and the
reusable individual-log solver are ready. End after independent scalar replay.
Preserve the paid online interval on unsuccessful descent, with no speedup claim.
For rho, place the same boundary after reusable field/Frobenius constants and
before target-dependent walk setup. Report the actual arithmetic kernel and
require prepared/unprepared entry points to agree on every deterministic walk
counter and recovered scalar. Report construction and process startup are outside
these online intervals; the externally measured process remains the cold job.

Freeze correctness controls at K_0 and K_1 over F_(2^9), public fixture seeds
2026092555, one target, and the checked-in autolab dependency lock. Reject malformed,
noncanonical, out-of-range, off-curve and wrong-subgroup inputs; reject measured
seed-only or multi-target jobs. Changing provenance seeds must not change the
supplied target. Cross-check all seven existing PDP engines and both LA modes on
the same public points, with group and query-certificate replay. Stop on a failed
control and retain its output.

This adds the outer online interval only. Existing combined instruction dumps
must remain labelled legacy intervals: exclusive scientific phases, query-law
replay and full source/base/matrix admission are still required before comparing
generic pipelines. No performance panel or improvement round is run here; costs,
S, rho ratios and speedup remain unknown as comparative results. The three-round
goal remains active, with one completed round and two remaining.
