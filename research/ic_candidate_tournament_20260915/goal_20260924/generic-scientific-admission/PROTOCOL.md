# Generic scientific admission

Depends on merged PRs 799, 803, 805 and 812. This is correctness, provenance
and accounting admission for the bounded synthetic worker (odd field degrees
5 through 31), not an improvement round. Keep the archived producers, sealed
round-one evaluator, incumbent and two remaining improvement rounds unchanged.

Hypothesis: the generic worker can provide independently reproducible evidence
for its exact declared source, factor-base recipe, executed dispatch, relation
matrix, stopping policy and recovered scalar. Reuse the existing independent
group, query-law, inventory and exclusive-phase checkers. A solver name or a
source digest alone cannot establish an executed method.

Require a build record binding the exact source/dependency manifest, compiler,
target, build flags and executable. Unbound binaries remain diagnostic-only.
Reject changed source, binary, job, dispatch, base, rows, chronology or timing.
Record the actual arithmetic kernel and the actual collector/descent policy.
Frontend counters must agree with the selected engine, including failed calls.
Dense subgroup Gaussian elimination and sparse filtering/block Wiedemann remain
distinct from Boolean Macaulay reduction inside PDP.

Independently reconstruct all six supported factor-base recipe families:
factor, divisor, Frobenius union, sampled subgroup orbits, torsion saturation
and pruning. Retain the exact ordered geometric points and the separately
counted usable cofactor image before orbit folding. The current subgroup-orbit
constructor cofactor-projects a sampled lift and checks the target after batches
of eight distinct projected abscissae; it does not merely reject non-subgroup
samples. The current factor enumeration omits degrees above 24. Bind these
implementation rules explicitly and do not label a truncated list complete.
Independent reconstruction must never call the measured factor-base builder.

Reconstruct canonical projected columns and every accepted row from the
retained query witnesses. Compare the actual matrix snapshot, RHS, duplicate
counts, per-query rank trajectory, batch solve attempts and terminal state.
An early success needs certified column logs and full independent rank; failed
or undetermined LA and terminal failed descents remain in the evidence. The
existing oracle independently verifies every complete target and its derivation.

Keep native one-target online timing primary, with cold instruction/native
accounting separately closed. Map scientific phases explicitly and justify
intentionally absent stages. Do not turn an unentered or missing phase into
zero. A valid admission receipt is not a performance qualification or promotion.
Charge added diagnostics and observers, and qualify their scheduling/overhead
effects against the optimized references before competitive rankings.

Freeze controls at seed 2026092557: the existing seven built-in PDP engines,
dense/sparse LA and both n9 curves, plus the n9/n13 subgroup-window and deliberate
preparation-failure cases from the query-law panel. Add base-only recipe controls
on n9/n13 to cover all six families and nested saturation/pruning; these are
constructor controls, not ordinary-query yield measurements. Keep one supplied
public target per measured job, one Rayon thread, 60-second child timeout and,
on Linux profiling, one CPU and 8 GiB. Repeat only when a changed implementation
or failed control justifies another pass; preserve prior outputs and sources.

Adversarial controls must reject a changed recipe even when the listed points
form a valid base, a solver-tag substitution with true group equations, altered
matrix coefficients/RHS/column order, missing batch attempts, premature stopping,
changed build identities and complete claims with missing scientific evidence.
Stop admission on any mismatch. Export immutable canonical candidate/workload/run
records only after all applicable checks pass; preserve failures without wins.

The fourteen frozen inventory recipes are factor(0), divisor([0,2]) at n9 or
divisor([1]) at n13, Frobenius union([1,2,4,8]), subgroup orbits(seed=43,
requested points=6n), torsion saturation of that union, pruning of the saturated
union to abscissa orbit 9 at n9 or 3 at n13, and saturation of that pruned base.
The pruning masks were chosen by independent arithmetic before these worker
runs to keep nonzero cofactor images. They are constructor controls only.

Runtime controls inject eleven undeclared names: KIC_F4_INHERIT, KIC_F4_KERNEL,
KIC_GF2_SIMD, KIC_SCAN_SIMD, F4_F2_MAX_ROWS, SOLVER_SPLIT_RULE, IC_TEMPLATE_MEMO,
IC_CACHE_LOCAL_BYTES, IC_REDIS_URL, IC_F2_BACKEND and IC_ARTIFACT_CACHE. Each must
fail before a measured session. Only the existing driver defaults
IC_F2_BACKEND=cpu and IC_ARTIFACT_CACHE=off are allowed; neither enables a cache
or alternate backend. Strict mode also requires one Rayon worker. Build
receipts bind actual dependency-tree contents, literal include inputs, compiler
and native tools, flags and binary, and reject inherited Cargo configuration.
They establish an audited local build, not hermetic compilation or remote
attestation. No cross-host executable identity is assumed.

Cold scientific mapping retains setup, base, precompute, ordinary queries/PDP,
relation checks, matrix build, final LA and recovery as their own phases. Sum
target_query, target_pdp, target_relation_check and target_descent once into
cold target_descent. Isogeny is explicitly zero because no route executes.
An unentered target child makes the aggregated target cost unknown. The five
online IC phases stay separate and close to the headline interval. Worker
general-group scalar replay is inside that interval; the independent Python
audit is outside it and carries a separate measured audit time. Native process
startup/reporting remainder is charged once to supplementary cold setup.

showed that the n9/a0 pair-table sequence has three novel rows but rank one

batch_trials=1, dense/sparse LA, max_trials=4/256. The shorter cases must retain
a paid unsuccessful LA call; the longer cases must retain unsuccessful calls

earlier certificate trace, not a new performance sample or tuned candidate.

and merge_max_weight=0, retaining every other original setting. They must
