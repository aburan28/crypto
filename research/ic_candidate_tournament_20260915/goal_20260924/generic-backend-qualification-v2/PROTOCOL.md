# Generic backend qualification v2 — registered before measurement

The first registered F4/F5/SAT complete-DLP campaign
([generic-backend-qualification](../generic-backend-qualification/RESULT.md))
was **operationally censored**: Actions run
[36532455386](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/actions/runs/36532455386)
hit the six-hour job cap and artifact upload failed. Trial counts, natural
yield, full costs and paired rho ratios are unknown. That registration must
not be redispatched. This protocol is the fresh comparison required there.

## Hypothesis and stop conditions

**Hypothesis.** At least one registered generic F4/F5 arm and at least one
SAT arm can each complete independently verified one-target solves on every
**executed** smoke job of the five-cell toy panel under the resource envelope
below, with full scientific admission and exclusive phase closure.

**Success (smoke family qualification).** At least one F4/F5 arm and at least
one SAT arm must each complete *every* scheduled smoke job (five cells × one
point × three repetitions). Each verified job independently binds
source/build, usable base, query law, observed engine dispatch, stored
matrix/rank, factor logs, target descent, scalar replay and native/profile
closure. A complete scalar without the checked IC path fails. Retain separate
cold/online IC and rho leaders, cell tradeoffs and all alternatives. No
familywise promotion inference is made on this development panel. Development
fixtures are prepared and frozen as exclusions but are **not executed** in
this registration; a later development campaign is a separate protocol after
smoke family qualification.

**Stop / negative qualification.** If an arm cannot complete verified smoke
solves within the declared timeout/memory, or fails admission, it remains a
table row with null competitive costs. Do not widen the envelope, drop cells,
substitute a cheaper stage diagnostic as a complete-DLP win, or promote an
incomplete schedule. Soft wall-clock exhaustion that retains a packaged
archive is an operationally censored incomplete run, not a retry of this
registration.

**Inadmissible moves.** Redispatching seed `2026092901`; reusing any point
from that reconstructed fixture set or from the three sealed improvement
rounds; retuning on sealed confirmation/replay; changing the operation unit
after seeing results; counting stage diagnostics as end-to-end speedups; or
claiming ECC2K-130 transfer from this toy panel.

Cap 300 executed pairs. Linux amd64, Rust 1.94.1, Valgrind 3.22.0, one pinned
CPU and one Rayon thread, 8 GiB and **180 seconds** per child. Failures,
timeouts and OOMs remain rows. Never retry or overwrite a measured execution.

**Job envelope.** The measured workflow step soft-stops at **300 minutes**,
leaving at least **45 minutes** inside the 360-minute job limit to build a
single `tar.zst` archive of the output tree and upload that one file. The
runner must checkpoint after prepare and after each executed stage so a soft
stop still retains fixtures, receipts and command logs. Uploading an unpacked
million-file tree is inadmissible after the censored first dispatch.

Primary metric on executed jobs: verified one-target native online wall time
after reusable preparation through scalar replay. Supplementary: complete cold
native time and user-space guest instructions with exclusive phase closure.
Report `S = Ir / sqrt(r)`, actual B/K, paired rho ratios and the weak
collector floor where applicable. An online gain does not imply a cold-start
or total-operation gain; a stage gain does not establish a verified online
speedup. Leave end-to-end speedup unset until a complete verified schedule
exists.

Audit every ordinary sampled collection query before reporting natural
relation yield. Timeouts without a full report are censored, not zero-yield.
Process repetitions on one point are not additional independent points.
