# Bounded improvement campaign, frozen before new measurements

This protocol implements the active September 24 goal. The primary display is
single-target online native time after reusable preparation through scalar
replay. Promotion additionally requires at least 20% lower complete cold guest
instructions and complete cold native time than the qualified archived IC
incumbent. A stage improvement or a development rank is not a promotion.

## Diverse selection and complete costs

At most 16 complete IC pipelines, including the unmodified incumbent, enter a
round. Retain up to six eligible challengers: preserve online, cold instruction
and cold native leaders, then non-dominated mechanism families/cell specialists,
plus one seed-fixed exploration slot from the remaining eligible arms. Report
any shortfall if fewer than six complete valid challengers survive. Failed and
unretained arms remain evidence, not silent deletions. Include explicit
combinations of mechanisms, including plausible combinations with individually
slower parents; no sum of cheap phases from different candidates is a pipeline.

Select exactly one challenger on the separate selection points by minimizing
the larger of its aggregate cold instruction and cold native ratios; online
ratio, then lexical alias, breaks ties. Online cost also participates in
portfolio dominance and has a protected leader slot. This selection is fixed
before reading confirmation/replay results.

Retain canonical run keys, actual usable factor-base count and folded columns,
exclusive phase ledgers, ordinary-query status/yield, novel rank, final matrix
construction/LA, descent, scalar replay, memory and unsuccessful work. The cold
instruction unit is complete user-space guest Ir; S = Ir/sqrt(r). Kernel/device,
profiler and external audit work have no invented conversion to Ir. The existing
K-instruction full-rank-collector floor is weak and implementation-specific;
it is inapplicable to rho and cannot establish a generic IC lower bound.

## Predeclared familywise rule

The estimand is the equal-cell mean of paired per-point log cost ratios, using
the median of three process repetitions for each algorithm on that same point.
Exponentiate for the reported geometric-mean ratio. The six cells are fixed;
there is no inference to a randomly sampled curve family. Repetitions and
clipped rho widths are not independent target samples.

For the selected challenger versus IC incumbent, resample paired target records
with replacement **within each fixed cell**, preserving that cell's target
count. Use the same sampled indices for cold Ir, cold native ns and online
native ns. Use 50,000 bootstrap draws and seed 914223 + round number (separate from fixture generation). Work
on log ratios; the one-sided basic upper bound is `2*estimate - lower_quantile`,
then exponentiate. The lower order statistic at `floor(draws/360)-1` fixes the
Monte Carlo convention. Report ordinary 95% descriptive intervals separately.

Allocate alpha=0.05 across **3 rounds x 2 final stages x 3 metrics = 18 upper
bounds**, giving alpha=1/360 to each. Requiring both confirmation and replay
cannot replace this allocation; their target samples are dependent. No alpha
is recycled after early stopping. This is a **nominal familywise 95% bootstrap
rule**: Bonferroni controls the family when the component bounds have their
stated coverage; bootstrap coverage is approximate, conditional on the declared
target law and measured environment. It is not an exact finite-sample guarantee.
See [NIST's Bonferroni construction](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section4/prc463.htm)
and [Efron and Narasimhan on bootstrap interval coverage](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7958418/).

Every confirmation and replay job must be independently verified and fully
priced. In **both** stages, require:

- Aggregate cold Ir ratio <=0.8 and cold native ratio <=0.8.
- All three familywise upper bounds strictly below 1, including online time;
  aggregate online ratio must also be <=1.
- Every measured per-cell geometric-mean ratio <=1.1 in each metric. This is a
  per-cell point-estimate guard, not a simultaneous per-cell confidence claim.
- A/A instruction controls pass, no missing phase/target, and independent group,
  relation, scalar-field rank/log, nonempty descent and scalar-replay checks pass.

Rho comparisons are paired descriptive reference comparisons, not additional
familywise claims. Preserve both online and cold references in the tables and
keep primary online and supplementary complete cold boundaries explicit.

Merge each implementation/evidence PR and update the canonical scoreboard from
frozen results. The goal finishes only with an audited qualifying winner or an
audited no-winner finding within these three rounds; infrastructure or controls
alone are not completion.
