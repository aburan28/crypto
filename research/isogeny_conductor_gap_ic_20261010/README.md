# Fiber-aware relation generation, and index calculus across a large conductor gap

**Date.** 2026-10-10.  **Status.** Measured on public synthetic curves,
sessions committed under `sessions/`, every session audited with twelve
exact replays.  **Result class.** Part A: *engineering* on the relation
phase of cofactor curves (AGENTS.md §3), with one negative result (the
combined algebraic system).  Part B: *boundary control* for protocols
I-4 and I-5 of
[`../isogeny_class_difficulty_20261008/`](../isogeny_class_difficulty_20261008/).
`S`, end-to-end cost and speedup are reported from the sessions and
never read as a speedup: every index-calculus arm here is above its
matched rho.  No deployed-curve claim; no key recovery.

## 1. The two questions

**A. Fiber-aware relation generation.**  Every index-calculus pipeline
in this repository builds its factor base from raw curve points and
writes relations over the cofactor projection `[h]P`.  A subgroup
target `T` therefore stands for its whole **cofactor fiber**
`{T + K : K ∈ E(F_q)[h]}`, the `h` raw lifts the n = 83 five-sum notes
call "the four raw target fibers under multiplication by four".  The
landed oracles accept only `Σ P_i = T`.  Three fiber-aware variants were
built (`fiber.rs`): **lifts** (every lift probed or descended),
**closed** (a pair table holding `P_i + P_j + K` for every `K`, one
probe), and **combined** (one Weil-descended system with the target
abscissa free and the fiber polynomial `Π_K (X + x(T + K))` adjoined).

**B. The same index calculus across a large conductor gap.**  Two
`F_q`-isogenous curves at the two ends of an `ℓ`-volcano, the crater
(`End = O_K`) and the floor (`End = Z[π]`), joined by an explicit
kernel-certified chain of degree `ℓ^h` (the gap), under identical
factor base, oracle and rho arms.  The measured quantities are the
relation yield, the F4 solving degree (binary), the cold whole-pipeline
`S`, and the online wall.

## 2. The pairs

See the first table of [`TABLES.md`](TABLES.md) ("Certified pairs") for
every pair with its order, subgroup, cofactor, Frobenius conductor, gap
and certificate.  In short:

- **prime fields**, 38 pairs: CM by `D_K ∈ {−7, −8, −11, −19, −3, −4}`
  with `f_π = ℓ^h` (`2ℓ^h` for `D_K = −7`), gaps 121 to 1458, at two
  sizes (`p` from `2^21` to `2^29`).  The crater is the class-number-one
  `j(O_K)`; every level above the floor has rank-2 `E[ℓ]`; the floor has
  rank-1 `E[ℓ]` for every `ℓ ∣ f_π`; each Vélu step preserves `#E`,
  carries the generator to a point of order `r` and transports a planted
  scalar; the floor also gets an isomorphic model (the I-4 control).
  Two configurations (`D_K = −7, ℓ = 2`) found no prime pair in the
  window and are recorded as failures in `pairs/prime_pairs.json`.
- **Koblitz**, 5 pairs: the `a = 0` craters over `F_{2^19}` and
  `F_{2^23}` (`icv1-f2m19-t797-b6cf2467`, `icv1-f2m23-t5197-69e76b73`)
  and the `a = 1` craters over `F_{2^17}`, `F_{2^19}` and `F_{2^23}`
  (`icv1-f2m17-tm101-00378d4e`, `icv1-f2m19-tm797-9c54981b`,
  `icv1-f2m23-tm5197-1f85e9e1`), each descended by one isogeny of
  prime degree `f_π ∈ {457, 967, 271}` with the kernel built in
  `F_{q^m}`, `m = ord_ℓ(t/2)`; the floor is certified by rank-1 `E[ℓ]`
  over the same extension.  (The `a = 0` curve over `F_{2^17}`,
  `icv1-f2m17-t101-e6c4b64d`, and the even degrees have no
  prime-order subgroup of cofactor 2 or 4 and are refused by the
  constructor.)
- **random binary**, 3 pairs: classes over `F_{2^17}`, `F_{2^19}`,
  `F_{2^23}` with `ℓ = 17, 41, 23 ∥ f_π`, found by scanning 600 random
  curves per degree with the Koblitz traces excluded (the first scan
  re-found the Koblitz class; it is kept in
  `pairs/binary_pairs_koblitz_class_rediscovered.json`).  A scan for
  `ℓ ≥ 60` over 3000 curves per degree found none
  (`pairs/binary_pairs_large_ell.json`).
- **extension fields** `F_{p^k}`: not attempted (no explicit-curve
  constructor in `ecbench`).

## 3. Results

Every number below is quoted from [`TABLES.md`](TABLES.md), which the
analyzer writes from the committed sessions.  A ratio is a median over the
curves or pairs named, with the range in brackets.  `S` is the cold
whole-pipeline cost over √r; every index-calculus `S` here is a lower
bound and sits above its matched rho.

### 3.1 Part A: fiber-aware relation generation

Ratios against the blind oracle on the same curve, same factor base and
same targets:

| family | curves, h | arm | S ÷ blind | yield ÷ blind | relation-phase cost per relation ÷ blind |
|:--|:--|:--|--:|--:|--:|
| prime, h ≤ 25 | 17, h ∈ {1, 9, 12, 18, 25} | closed | 0.50 [0.21, 2.36] | 11.7 [1.0, 30.8] | 0.094 [0.042, 1.0] |
| prime, h ≤ 25 | same | lifts | 1.83 [1.00, 3.02] | 11.7 [1.0, 31.1] | 1.01 [0.77, 1.60] |
| prime, h ≥ 50 | 17, h from 50 to 784 | closed | 4.7 [2.3, 75] | 116 [34, 935] | 0.017 [0.005, 0.062] |
| prime, h ≥ 50 | same | lifts | 3.8 [1.8, 29] | 114 [34, 933] | 0.86 [0.40, 1.75] |
| random binary, n = 17, 19 | 8, h ∈ {4, 6, 8} | closed MITM | 5.8 [3.9, 7.9] | 3.5 [3.0, 4.0] | 0.65 [0.57, 0.77] |
| random binary | same | lifts MITM | 1.02 [1.01, 1.03] | 3.5 [2.9, 4.0] | 0.99 [0.86, 1.11] |
| random binary | same | lifts F4 | 1.45 [1.26, 1.88] | 3.5 [2.9, 4.0] | 0.99 [0.89, 1.08] |
| Koblitz, n = 17, 19, 23 | 4, h ∈ {2, 4} | closed MITM | 3.0 [1.9, 4.0] | 2.2 [1.7, 2.8] | 0.78 [0.69, 0.84] |
| Koblitz | same | lifts MITM | 1.01 [1.00, 1.01] | 2.2 [1.7, 2.8] | 1.04 [0.99, 1.18] |
| Koblitz | same | lifts F4 | 1.21 [1.07, 1.30] | 2.2 [1.7, 2.8] | 1.03 [0.99, 1.12] |
| Koblitz, Frobenius-orbit MITM | 2 | closed | 3.0 [1.9, 4.0] | 1.47 [1.45, 1.48] | 0.86 [0.75, 0.97] |

- **Yield scales with the fiber, as predicted (P-A0).**  Probing every lift
  multiplies relations per trial by up to `h`.  The measured factor falls
  short of `h` where the factor base is small enough that many lifts
  repeat a relation already found.
- **Only the closed table cuts the relation phase's cost per relation, and
  it pays for that in set-up (P-A1).**  It holds `h` times the blind
  table's entries.  On prime curves with `h ≤ 25` the cut wins: `S` falls
  to 0.21 to 0.36 of blind on the 25-bit curves.  With `h ≥ 50`, and on
  every binary and Koblitz curve, the table costs more than it saves.
  At `h = 784` closed `S` is 75 times blind.
- **Lifts gains per target and nothing per operation (P-A2).**  Its cost
  per relation stays at the blind value; its `S` is the blind `S` plus the
  extra probes.
- **The F4 solving degree does not move for lifts.**  Blind and lifts
  descents both solve at mean degree 2.96 to 3.00, maximum 3, with solver operations per
  relation within 10 percent of each other.
- **The blind oracle fails on large cofactors.**  On the cofactor-784 curve
  `icv1-fp24-t6316-2e757e7a` every blind run exhausted its trial cap,
  while both fiber arms recovered every target; a blind table holds only
  about `512 / h` in-subgroup sums.
- **No fiber arm beats rho.**  The best cell, closed MITM on the 25-bit
  curves, has `S ≥ 21` against rho's 1.0 to 1.4 on the same curves.

**The combined Weil descent is a negative result, measured on warm-up runs
only.**  Host reboots ended all four attempts at the A3 session before it
finished (amendments 7 and 8; the partial sessions and run logs are in
`interrupted/`).  A reboot leaves a session marked `running`, which
`ecbench verify` does not audit, so the figures below come from records
that the harness wrote but that no audit has checked:

| curve | arm | runs | online wall per target | F4 solver operations ÷ blind | solving degree, mean (max) | peak memory |
|:--|:--|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| `icv1-f2m17-tm101-00378d4e` (`h = 2`) | combined | 3 verified, 2 targets | 335 to 578 s | 629 and 654 | 3.05 to 3.07 (4) | 0.70 to 0.76 GB |
| same | blind | 6 verified | 0.7 to 1.4 s | 1 | 2.96 to 3.00 (3) | 14 MB |
| `icv1-f2m17-tm285-f6a04bec` (`h = 6`) | combined | 2, both timed out | over 3600 s | – | – | 0.27 GB at timeout |
| same | blind | 2 verified | 1.1 to 1.9 s | 1 | 2.98 to 2.99 (3) | 14 MB |

The first Koblitz target was run twice, in two different sessions, and
both runs counted exactly 59,916,380,641 solver operations.  Adjoining the
fiber polynomial raises the maximum solving degree from 3 to 4.  It costs
more than 600 times the blind solver work for at most `h` times the
relations, and on the `h = 6` curve it does not finish within the hour.
A3 should be re-run with `specs/fiber_combined_n17_koblitz.json` on a host
that stays up for an hour.

### 3.2 Part B: the same index calculus across the conductor gap

Floor ÷ crater on each certified pair, same arms, factor base and targets:

| family | pairs | gap | arm | S | yield | online wall |
|:--|--:|:--|:--|--:|--:|--:|
| prime | 38 | 121 to 1458 | fiber-closed MITM | 1.00 [0.88, 3.10] | 0.99 [0.80, 1.39] | 1.04 [0.64, 1.23] |
| prime | 38 | same | rho | 0.97 [0.67, 1.71] | – | 1.00 [0.81, 1.48] |
| Koblitz | 5 | 271, 457, 967 | F4 descent | 0.96 [0.84, 1.00] | 0.95 [0.87, 1.07] | 0.94 [0.83, 1.12] |
| Koblitz | 5 | same | MITM | 0.96 [0.88, 1.08] | 0.95 [0.87, 1.07] | 0.89 [0.85, 1.08] |
| Koblitz | 5 | same | rho | 1.01 [0.76, 1.51] | – | 0.95 [0.77, 1.01] |
| random binary | 3 | 17, 41, 23 | F4 descent | 0.97 [0.92, 1.06] | 1.17 [1.01, 1.22] | 0.93 [0.84, 1.06] |
| random binary | 3 | same | MITM | 1.13 [0.98, 1.21] | 1.17 [1.01, 1.22] | 1.02 [0.97, 1.06] |
| random binary | 3 | same | rho | 1.05 [1.03, 1.11] | – | 1.01 [0.88, 1.16] |

- **The solving degree is the same at both ends.**  On all 8 binary and
  Koblitz pairs, crater and floor F4 descents run at mean degree 2.98 to
  3.00, maximum 3.
- **Yield, `S` and wall are unchanged across the gap.**  Every median is
  between 0.89 and 1.17.  The ranges are those of the run-to-run spread,
  which rho, a method blind to the endomorphism ring, shows as well.
- **The prime outliers are set-up, not relations.**  The `S` ratios far
  from one, up to 3.1, are the five pairs with cofactor 25 or 784 to 5408.
  There the closed table's set-up cost differs between crater and floor,
  while relation-phase cost per relation stays within 25 percent.  This
  round does not explain the set-up difference.  *Explained in round 2*
  ([`../isogeny_conductor_gap_round2_20261011/`](../isogeny_conductor_gap_round2_20261011/README.md)):
  the oracle's subgroup closure overcounted additions. The overcount grew
  with how many base points the sampler needed, not with the curve.  Under
  the corrected closure, crater, floor and model pay the same set-up
  within 0.3 percent, and the 3.1 ratio becomes 1.00.  The sessions here
  are unchanged and still replay.
- **The isomorphic floor model is a null control.**  Its `S` over the
  floor's is 1.00 [0.28, 1.11] for the closed MITM and 1.01 [0.54, 1.51]
  for rho, across 38 pairs.
- **The Koblitz crater keeps its Frobenius folds and the floor has none.**
  The Frobenius-orbit arms run on the crater only
  (`koblitz_gap_crater_folds`); the floor's endomorphism ring
  `Z[π]` admits no extra fold beyond negation.


## 4. Decision

**Part A is engineering on the relation phase.**  It is not an advance.
Fiber-aware oracles multiply the relation yield by up to the cofactor.
The closed table cuts the relation-phase cost per relation by up to 200
times, and wins whole-pipeline `S` only on prime curves with `h ≤ 25`.
No fiber arm moves an index-calculus cell below its matched rho.  The
combined Weil descent is a negative result: adjoining the fiber polynomial
raises the solving degree and the solver work faster than the fiber adds
relations.  The oracles stay in `fiber.rs` for any curve with a large
cofactor, where the blind oracle exhausts.

**Part B is a boundary control for I-4 and I-5.**  Moving between the two
ends of an `ℓ`-volcano, `End = O_K` to `End = Z[π]`, at gaps up to 1458,
changes none of the measured quantities beyond run-to-run spread: not the
solving degree, not the yield, not `S`, not the wall.  Index calculus here
sees the group and the factor base, not the endomorphism ring.  The one
place the ring shows is the Frobenius fold, which the Koblitz crater
admits and its floor does not.  That fold is a rho and orbit-folding
effect, already priced elsewhere, not an index-calculus one.  No class
ranking of I-4 or I-5 changes.


## 5. Reproduce

```bash
cargo build --release --bin ecbench --example isogeny_gap_pairs --example isogeny_gap_analyze
B=./target/release/examples/isogeny_gap_pairs; D=research/isogeny_conductor_gap_ic_20261010
$B --family prime   --out $D/pairs
$B --family koblitz --n 19,23,20,17 --out $D/pairs --out-name koblitz_k0_pairs
$B --family koblitz --a 1 --n 17,19,23 --out $D/pairs --out-name koblitz_k1_pairs
$B --family binary  --n 17,19,23 --seeds 600 --out $D/pairs
$B --family specs --pairs-dir $D/pairs --out $D/specs
$B --family fiber-specs --pairs-dir $D/pairs --out $D/specs
$D/run_sessions.sh                       # sessions, audits, tables (hours)
./target/release/examples/isogeny_gap_analyze --sessions $D/sessions --pairs $D/pairs \
  --out $D/TABLES.md --json $D/tables.json
```

Host: the session's `host.json` (Linux x86-64 cloud container, 4 logical
CPUs, run CPU reserved whole; levels per run in every table).  Wall
times are a practicality note at L1/L2 on a virtual machine and are not
the result; operation counts and solver operations are.
