## 1. The two questions

**A. Fiber-aware relation generation.**  Every index-calculus pipeline
in this repository builds its factor base from raw curve points and
writes relations over the cofactor projection `[h]P`.  A subgroup
target `T` therefore stands for its whole **cofactor fiber**
`{T + K : K ∈ E(F_q)[h]}`, the `h` raw lifts the n = 83 five-sum notes
call "the four raw target fibers under multiplication by four".  The
landed oracles accept only `Σ P_i = T`.  Three fiber-aware variants were
built (`fiber.rs`): **lifts** (every lift probed or descended),
**closed** (a pair table holding `P_i + P_j + K` for every `K`, one
probe), and **combined** (one Weil-descended system with the target
abscissa free and the fiber polynomial `Π_K (X + x(T + K))` adjoined).

**B. The same index calculus across a large conductor gap.**  Two
`F_q`-isogenous curves at the two ends of an `ℓ`-volcano, the crater
(`End = O_K`) and the floor (`End = Z[π]`), joined by an explicit
kernel-certified chain of degree `ℓ^h` (the gap), under identical
factor base, oracle and rho arms.  The measured quantities are the
relation yield, the F4 solving degree (binary), the cold whole-pipeline
`S`, and the online wall.

## 2. The pairs

See the first table of [`TABLES.md`](TABLES.md) ("Certified pairs") for
every pair with its order, subgroup, cofactor, Frobenius conductor, gap
and certificate.  In short:

- **prime fields**, 38 pairs: CM by `D_K ∈ {−7, −8, −11, −19, −3, −4}`
  with `f_π = ℓ^h` (`2ℓ^h` for `D_K = −7`), gaps 121 to 1458, at two
  sizes (`p` from `2^21` to `2^29`).  The crater is the class-number-one
  `j(O_K)`; every level above the floor has rank-2 `E[ℓ]`; the floor has
  rank-1 `E[ℓ]` for every `ℓ ∣ f_π`; each Vélu step preserves `#E`,
  carries the generator to a point of order `r` and transports a planted
  scalar; the floor also gets an isomorphic model (the I-4 control).
  Two configurations (`D_K = −7, ℓ = 2`) found no prime pair in the
  window and are recorded as failures in `pairs/prime_pairs.json`.
- **Koblitz**, 5 pairs: `K_0/F_{2^19}`, `K_0/F_{2^23}`, `K_1/F_{2^17}`,
  `K_1/F_{2^19}`, `K_1/F_{2^23}`, each descended by one isogeny of
  prime degree `f_π ∈ {457, 967, 271}` with the kernel built in
  `F_{q^m}`, `m = ord_ℓ(t/2)`; the floor is certified by rank-1 `E[ℓ]`
  over the same extension.  (`K_0/F_{2^17}` and the even degrees have no
  prime-order subgroup of cofactor 2 or 4 and are refused by the
  constructor.)
- **random binary**, 3 pairs: classes over `F_{2^17}`, `F_{2^19}`,
  `F_{2^23}` with `ℓ = 17, 41, 23 ∥ f_π`, found by scanning 600 random
  curves per degree with the Koblitz traces excluded (the first scan
  re-found the Koblitz class; it is kept in
  `pairs/binary_pairs_koblitz_class_rediscovered.json`).  A scan for
  `ℓ ≥ 60` over 3000 curves per degree found none
  (`pairs/binary_pairs_large_ell.json`).
- **extension fields** `F_{p^k}`: not attempted (no explicit-curve
  constructor in `ecbench`).

## 5. Reproduce

```bash
cargo build --release --bin ecbench --example isogeny_gap_pairs --example isogeny_gap_analyze
B=./target/release/examples/isogeny_gap_pairs; D=research/isogeny_conductor_gap_ic_20261010
$B --family prime   --out $D/pairs
$B --family koblitz --n 19,23,20,17 --out $D/pairs --out-name koblitz_k0_pairs
$B --family koblitz --a 1 --n 17,19,23 --out $D/pairs --out-name koblitz_k1_pairs
$B --family binary  --n 17,19,23 --seeds 600 --out $D/pairs
$B --family specs --pairs-dir $D/pairs --out $D/specs
$B --family fiber-specs --pairs-dir $D/pairs --out $D/specs
$D/run_sessions.sh                       # sessions, audits, tables (hours)
./target/release/examples/isogeny_gap_analyze --sessions $D/sessions --pairs $D/pairs \
  --out $D/TABLES.md --json $D/tables.json
```

Host: the session's `host.json` (Linux x86-64 cloud container, 4 logical
CPUs, run CPU reserved whole; levels per run in every table).  Wall
times are a practicality note at L1/L2 on a virtual machine and are not
the result; operation counts and solver operations are.
