# Two-word Koblitz step-rate gate: measured outcome

This is the outcome of [`PROTOCOL.md`](PROTOCOL.md), frozen before the
sessions. It is an **engineering stage diagnostic**. The three sessions
measure the tuned signed-Frobenius walk's per-operation cost and confirm
that its two-word port charges the same operations as the word-size
implementation. They do not establish a complete m = 83 comparison.

- **H1 passes exactly:** the six measured n = 61 narrow/wide pairs
  share workload ids, seeds, recovered scalars, counters, charged phase
  operations and total GAE. The warm-up executions also verified.
- **H2 falls inside the frozen band:** the median wide/narrow step-rate
  ratio at n = 61 is 2.56, against the preregistered 1.3–4 band.
- **H3 falls inside the frozen band:** the median m = 83/n = 61 wide
  step-rate ratio is 1.40, against the preregistered upper bound 1.5.
  Per-run m = 83 rates range from 2,665 to 6,327 ns. With all runs at
  L0 and no A/A floor, this is a descriptive observation, not a
  hardware-portability or runtime-speed conclusion.

The signed-Frobenius generic collision floor at m = 83 is
`sqrt(pi * r / 332) = 1.513e11` walk steps. Multiplying that derived
floor by this host's **descriptive** median gives 196 hours on one core;
using the preregistered 3–8% walk overhead gives 202–212 hours. This
is an **illustrative extrapolation**, not a timed full solve or a measured
method `S`. It rests on a narrow-size overhead estimate and L0 step
rate. The protocol's advance estimate of “hours per target” understated
the observed projection: it is about eight to nine days per target on
this host. The next cost estimate needs a dedicated L2/L3 host and a
wide strong-rho reference.

## Audit correction and decision

The first m = 83 audit returned `ok` while replaying zero records:
the auditor selected only verified answers and skipped deterministic
budget exits. Its original receipt is preserved as
[`audits/m83-pre-exhausted-replay-fix.json`](audits/m83-pre-exhausted-replay-fix.json).
Commit `76178cff9` changed replay selection and checks the exit status;
the corrected audit above replayed all six measured m = 83 records.

The two-word walk is qualified for counted step-rate diagnostics and
cross-architecture replay in CI. Its L0 timing does not qualify as an
admitted wall result. The m = 83 IC and strong-rho paths, two-socket
NUMA run, and fully priced SAT/PDP comparison remain separate gates.
No end-to-end or scaling advantage is claimed by this result.
