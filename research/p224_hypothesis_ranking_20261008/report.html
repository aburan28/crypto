The supplied claim

The supplied text ranks four hypotheses “by how compatible each is with the public evidence and 1999-era compute”:

  1. a weak class recognisable from (p, a, b) without point counting, found by grinding SHA-1 seeds. It cites b_seed_profile and the Solinas-correlation experiment as nulls;
  2. the prime, not the curve. It says hidden-SNFS primes (Fried–Gaudry–Heninger– Thomé) have no ECDLP analogue. It calls P-224’s 46-bit-smooth p − 1 “the strongest structure of this kind in the NIST set”, affecting “only DLP in F_p^*, not the curve group”;
  3. hidden endomorphism or isogeny structure. It says all public checks pass (embedding degree, anomaly, CM discriminant and class number “enormous”) and that the isogeny walk found no weak curve at small degree;
  4. an unpublished ECDLP algorithm: the unfalsifiable residual.

The ranking itself is an interpretation. This report checks the factual claims it rests on, for P-224.

Separate track: implementation, not an algebraic trapdoor

These do not bear on hypotheses 1–4 and do not refute them (AGENTS.md).

  • Twist (C4). #E′ = 3²·11·47·3015283·40375823·267983539294927·ℓ′ with ℓ′ ≈ 2^117.1 a probable prime. Expected rho in the largest twist subgroup is about 2^58.4, matching SafeCurves. P-256’s twist gives about 2^120.3. An x-only implementation that skips point validation therefore leaks P-224 keys at about 2^58 work. This repository’s research/p224_attack/ demonstrates the related invalid-curve route. This closes the walk README’s “212-bit composite, unfactored” twist cofactor.
  • Square roots. v₂(p − 1) = 96 forces a 96-step Tonelli–Shanks (or equivalent) for point decompression. P-224 is the only NIST prime with v₂(p − 1) > 1.

Interpretation (labelled, not a finding)

The run does not change the supplied ranking’s order. It changes two of its statements:

  • hypothesis 2’s smooth-prime structure is not unique to P-224’s p − 1;
  • that structure is the stated prerequisite of a published ECDLP approach, PKM, whose status in this repository is inconclusive rather than closed.

Hypothesis 2 for P-224 is therefore an open, testable lead, not a dead end. Its next step is the tower-oracle programme at 2-power Kummer depth matched to P-224, with the full-cost and matched-rho gates of AGENTS.md §§1–8.

Hypothesis 1’s grind bound is a scenario, not evidence. Koblitz–Menezes (ePrint 2015/1018) argue that a seed-ground weak class large enough to find would be too large to have escaped public notice. Bernstein et al. (BADA55, ePrint 2014/571) show how much freedom a seed procedure leaves a designer. The seed preimages remain unpublished: the 2023 Valsorda bounty reports no recovery in the sources checked.
