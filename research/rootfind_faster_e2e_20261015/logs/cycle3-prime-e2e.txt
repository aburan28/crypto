=== Prime a=−3 curves: complete DLP, root finding vs Buchberger vs msolve ===

msolve found; charged its own process CPU only (harness spawn/file I/O excluded). Each decide is paired with an adjacent trivial-ideal control run (order alternating) and the mean paired difference is what is charged. For scale: one msolve process on the trivial ideal costs 4.83 ms child CPU (σ 830.7 µs, 200 up-front runs). A per-decide value is reported only if it clears 2 standard errors of the paired mean; otherwise the 2·SE bound is charged.
A baseline is measured in full when its projected wall is under 900.00 s (E2E_FULL_BUDGET_S); otherwise it is COMPOSED
from a sampled per-target cost (100 targets for Buchberger, 400 for msolve) × the root finder's measured target count on the same stream.
