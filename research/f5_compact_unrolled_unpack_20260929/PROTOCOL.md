# Matrix-F5 compact, two-at-a-time output unpack: frozen experiment

## Hypothesis and reference

The exact n24 degree-4 F5 call writes 13,734,979 monomial masks into 6,924 output polynomials. Its current direct scalar unpack takes roughly 35–55 ms on measured x86-64 hosts. A prior compact 32-bit column map matched every output and showed an **unqualified exploratory** 1.042× complete-call / 1.081× unpack median, below its preset full-call gate; its [result](../f5_compact_cols_unpack_20260929/RESULT.md) is not promoted. Byte- and nibble-subset copy tables then regressed even with a 1.05 MiB footprint. This experiment combines a compact `u32` column map with a two-at-a-time bit decode and direct pair writes, aiming to overlap independent column loads without per-chunk subset lookups. The reference is the existing exact direct scalar unpack. Conversion and allocation belong inside the complete call. The candidate is opt-in as `KIC_F5_UNPACK_COMPACT_UNROLL=1`; `=0` is reference. Limit it to direct unpack with at least 4,096 columns and at most 32 variables, cap actual compact-map allocation at 256 KiB and fall back otherwise. Preserve valid-row bounds once per row before any unchecked column read.

The frozen source is commit `154faab572a133425a031b0ae76a520a939a85a9` on merged branchless-strip PR #998: `matrix_f5_f2.rs` SHA-256 `bf35680f431ab7ac13a7615d334ec9b060217b84bdde36f03ce00319491d42da`, `gf2_elim.rs` `6fd3f10ef8e681490784c624e703eeb8fa887c5817ec9bdf34844e3f943c7f5c`, benchmark `f4_f2_bench.rs` `d40f9f0c76e5c56c9f4a52596b9edef41f321aa3d70cfb023c4ba21fdde70336`, isolation wrapper `ff59e53fd5566078ad493c8be0d06168699c4fd194bc881d99df19fa20e007ef`. Qualified unchanged-source baselines and binary hashes exist in [strip run 36658327789](https://github.com/aburan28/crypto/actions/runs/36658327789) and PR #1013; absolute times from those hosts are reproducibility context, never a cross-host ratio denominator. Candidate ratios use explicit `=0/1` processes from one release binary on the same runner.

## Workload, isolation and decision

Run all seven F5 cases in `examples/f4_f2_bench.rs`, primary `f5_n24_m24_d4`, seeds `0`, `badc0de1`, `5eed2026`, `f5c02a28`, at one and two Rayon threads separately. Hold selective echelon output, fused row count, direct packed rows, branchless AVX2 strip, AVX2 row XOR, Gray-code table reuse and direct scalar unpack fixed. Disable deferred-above, word batching, AVX-512 unpack and experimental table construction. Per seed and thread count, run one warmup per arm, five A/A reference pairs and five alternating A/B pairs: 22 processes with seven cases each. Preserve all calls including failures, timeouts and OOMs, source/binary hashes, CPU/affinity, raw and canonical row fingerprints, rank, terms, columns, row/criterion/build counts, reduction word operations, route and actual compact-map bytes, exclusive phases and complete-call time. Release F5 tests must compare partial words, sparse/dense patterns and cap fallback.

Build/test with `tools/isolated_bench.py busy` under one lock. Each timed seed block uses privileged `tools/isolated_bench.py reserve`, all SMT siblings reserved, one CPU left free, runner Rustup environment passed through, ten-second preflight, other-CPU rate at most 0.10, PSI at most 5.0, zero eligible user threads and zero contended samples at a **two-second** period. Attempt each seed at most three times in order, select the **first** block with reservation success and 22 exact calls independent of timing, and archive every refusal and partial call. If any seed lacks a qualified attempt, speedup is unknown. Do not combine absolute times across hosts.

The candidate advances only if the frozen one-thread primary complete-call paired reference/candidate median is at least 1.05, its exact 3,125-resample bootstrap 95% lower bound exceeds 1.02, no seed or smaller case has a complete-call median below its own A/A minimum, the two-thread control has no such regression, and allocation stays within 256 KiB. A passing candidate needs a separately frozen default-on confirmation. A failing candidate has its tested patch, workflow and raw receipts archived and the runtime option removed. The user's further 2× goal requires the frozen primary full-call median and lower bound both above 2.0 with all guards. These are solver-stage measurements, not IC one-target online DLP or rho speedups.
