Sparse multiplication, reduction losses [derived]

  • Sparse MM closed twice over. The paper (Sec. 6): its techniques "do not seem to give new algorithms for sparse matrix multiplication" (balanced case untouched). Ours stacks on top: block Wiedemann is sparse matvecs over Z/rZ -- wrong operation and wrong ring. Greps for girth, sparse-MM hardness, and balanced-triangle vocabulary return nothing outside our own study files.
  • Kept saving is the metric. Hardness reductions reused as algorithms keep only a fraction of the exponent saving (half, a third); SVX27 black-box optimality forces tighter transfers to use problem structure. That is our S rule in other words: end-to-end kept savings only, never headline exponents.
  • Gray boxes mirror the audit. Merely 3SUM/APSP-hard problems (reductions going the wrong way) get no speedup -- consistent with round 3's unaffected classifications.

Standing watch condition [proposed]

The technique family reaches our workloads only through two independent breakthroughs: (a) a new base identity lifting the ceiling past eps* = 0.1204 (the paper's open computer-search problem), and (b) a reduction reshaping an algebraic IC workload into thin small-integer products with known sparse W. Either alone changes nothing. Next action: parameterize screen_workload on the six table2.json rows after PR #1493 merges (existing tests unchanged).
