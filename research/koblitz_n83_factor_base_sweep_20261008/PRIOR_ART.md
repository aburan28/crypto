## Earlier N83 geometric-subspace comparator

The repository also contains a directly relevant **different factor-base representation** that must be accounted for before extending the retained-orbit SAT search. `build_standard_subspace_factor_base` in `koblitz_index_calculus.rs` enumerates rational points whose *source* x-coordinate lies in a standard polynomial subspace. Its dimension-18 K0 instance has 261,447 geometric points, 261,444 nonidentity points after cofactor-four projection and 130,722 sign-folded columns. The exact five-summand chained-S3 system uses 90 source-coordinate bits, 339 Boolean variables and 332 equations; it does not need the retained-orbit bases' explicit 83-bit finite-coordinate domain clauses. These facts and the planted arithmetic check are retained in [the dimension-18 result](../f6_n83_fivesum_d18_20261005/RESULT.md). The source subspace is not Frobenius-invariant, so signing and cofactor projection do not turn its 130,722 columns into the K=2,048 retained base's 2,048 signed-Frobenius columns for free.

The [factored-S3 SAT result](../f6_n83_factored_s3_sat_20261005/RESULT.md), [basis-aware encoding result](../f6_n83_basis_sat_20261005/RESULT.md) and [recursive carryless-product result](../f6_n83_karatsuba_sat_20261005/RESULT.md) provide three source-pinned representations of that same dimension-18 system. The final representation reduced the ordinary offset-zero input to 20,450 AND gates, 50,089 XOR gates and 111,771 mixed constraints, but each of four ordinary 120-second CryptoMiniSat calls timed out without a model. Its fully fixed planted control verified the circuit and full-group lift; the partially fixed control timed out. These are solver feasibility observations, not a measured relation yield or complete-runtime comparison. [The source-prolongation result](../f6_n83_five_source_prolongation_20261005/RESULT.md) reached a 1.5-million-column cap before deriving a source-only pruning row. Earlier [six-summand prefix work](../f6_n83_sixsum_prefix_20261005/RESULT.md) also failed its registered root-pruning gate. Repeating these approaches under a slightly larger wall cap would not establish a base winner.

The closer comparator is the [dimension-12 projected Frobenius closure](../f6_n83_mixed_base_20261004/ORBIT_RESULT.md): **332,166 subgroup points in 2,001 signed-Frobenius columns**, near the stored K=2,048 base's 339,968 points in 2,048 columns. Its exact five-multiset count/order ratio is 13.9373 versus 15.6528 for K=2,048. The closure's points come from only 4,054 cofactor-projected source points; before projection, their x-coordinates lie in a 12-bit polynomial subspace. Applying one of 83 Frobenius phases and a sign gives the closure. This is a possible *structured membership representation* for a new SAT backend, but no complete phase-selection circuit, ordinary relation, rank or cold runtime was measured. The old result is an inventory and capacity screen, and this particular closure has not been published as an object in this sweep's S3 panel.

The historical geometric runs used public T001 `(355fb5df7a905f16921eb,5900a390f42d290f1bbe)` and four torsion preimages. This sweep's primary fixture zero is a different subgroup point, selected from `pilot-01/probe-corpus.json`. Their timeouts and the current retained-base conflicts therefore cannot be compared as matched solver times. A fair next comparison must pin one target and source revision, charge construction and rank, use the same resource limits, and independently verify any natural relation and complete group equation.

Primary literature inspected on 2026-10-08:

- Galbraith, Granger, Merz and Petit, [On Index Calculus Algorithms for Subfield Curves](https://sacworkshop.org/SAC20/files/preproceedings/18-IndexCalculus.pdf): invariant factor bases and Frobenius symmetry. Its domain conditions motivate the degree-83 invariant-module gate.
- Trimoska, Ionica and Dequen, [Parity (XOR) Reasoning for the Index Calculus Attack](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.11229): ANF-aware WDSat and branching preprocessing. Its solver-stage results are not imported as complete-pipeline improvements.
- Bouillaguet, Cheng, Chou, Niederhagen and Yang, [Fast Exhaustive Search for Quadratic Systems in F2 on FPGAs](https://perso.lip6.fr/Charles.Bouillaguet/static/publis/SAC13b.pdf): derivative Gray-code evaluation for quadratic Boolean systems. Hardware and equation-family assumptions remain explicit.
- [Index calculus with double large prime variation for curves of small genus with cyclic class group](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0606607): large-prime variation in curve class groups. Transfer to the exact elliptic point/orbit representation is an implementation and empirical obligation, not an automatic consequence.

The native order test derives the degree-83 cyclotomic-block fact directly. This study proposes no new polynomial identity, so the repository's prime-field `identity.certificate/v1` polynomial-evaluation certificate does not apply to that finite modular-order computation.
