## Queries and unresolved work

Queries covered `weak coverings isogeny classes density conjecture`,
`elliptic covering quadratic cubic simultaneously`, `Weil restriction
hyperelliptic automorphism prime genus`, and exact author/paper titles.
Broad searches surfaced the 2013 classification missing from the current
catalogue's source list. A guessed Smith ePrint number returned an unrelated
IBE paper and was excluded; the correct primary Smith source above was
checked. The 2006 full-text parsing failure remains an audit gap.

The exact candidate statements are authored research hypotheses. Their
novelty status is **unresolved against the bounded reviewed corpus**. Before
a paper claim, read all relevant classification tables, trace citation chains
and later revisions, and compare hypotheses, isogeny condition, genus,
field of definition, model equivalence and class-support denominator.

Published classes missing from our implementation are a catalogue and
verification backlog. The reported 17 additional classes cannot simply be
added to the existing 21-entry count: overlap and the distinction between a
family, a condition and a mechanism must be resolved first.
