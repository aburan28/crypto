# N83 Tier 1 K3072 single-target online result

K3072/root11 achieved a verified Tier 1 online speedup on **all five
previously unused N83 public points** against independently solved
same-point Pollard-rho references. The median paired ratio was **208.72×**;
the paired log-ratio 95% interval was **38.25× to 2510.24×**, with its lower
bound above one. All five pairs passed independent point, relation and scalar
replay, the alternating-order rule, source/base identity, resource admission,
and the frozen 10% rho-calibration A/A gate. The exact candidate is
`IC1N83Ckb1a1fb509952PDP4rootRCguidedLAgaussTDdirectISO0ha2d30fb17dd0`.
