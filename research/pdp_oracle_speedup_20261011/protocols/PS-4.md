# PS-4: candidate reduction by symmetry

Part of [`../PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md); frozen when the plan merges.  Repo: crypto.
Needs PS-2.

**Hypothesis.**  On a Frobenius-stable base, enumerating only one
representative per Frobenius orbit of the first summand and using the
2-torsion symmetry cuts the candidate pairs by a factor close to `2n`
without changing any verdict or solution set modulo the symmetry, and the
per-pair cost of the weight-base leaf stays within 2× of the subspace leaf.

**Frozen inputs.**  A Hamming-weight base `wt ≤ w`, `w ∈ {2, 3}`, in a
type-II optimal normal basis at the degrees where one exists
(`n ∈ {11, 23, 29, 33, 35, 39, 41}`), with the targets generated by
`examples/hamming_nb_sat_panel` (6 uniform and 2 planted per setting, seed
20261011) and decided by its exact pair-table oracle; the subspace-leaf
control is PS-0's corpus at the nearest `ℓ`.  The 2-torsion coordinate
`u = 1/(x + 1)` is the one the repository's existing symmetrisation uses.

**Unit.**  Candidates enumerated per target, counted bit operations per
candidate, and total counted bit operations per target, reduced against
unreduced, for both leaves.

**Success.**  Identical verdicts and identical solution sets modulo the
symmetry on the whole corpus; candidate count falls by `2n` within
boundary-orbit corrections; total counted operations fall by at least 0.8 of
the candidate factor.

**Stop.**  The weight-base leaf costs more than 2× the subspace leaf per
pair (it needs the roots and a popcount, not the `gcd` shortcut): report the
measured ratio; the reduction is then a constant that does not pay.

**Accounting rule.**  The product law already divides by `n` for a
Frobenius-stable base, so this task's factor is a stage constant.  PS-6 must
not credit it a second time to the whole method.

**Inadmissible.**  Comparing a reduced weight-base run to an unreduced
subspace run; skipping orbits whose representative lies on the boundary of
the symmetry without counting them.

**Class.**  Engineering.  **Effort.**  3 days.
