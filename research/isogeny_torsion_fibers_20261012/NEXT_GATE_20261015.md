## Gate A: direct polynomial root and quotient controls

**Question.** Did the bounded χ finite-root scan miss a useful algebraic
implementation effect? The [χ run](chi_polynomial_20261015/reports/run-20261015T1651Z/REPORT.md)
used cleared chord/tangent equations and exact candidate checks, but scanned
its finite signed base. It matched projected-pair relation outcomes at higher
CPU cost. A direct root algorithm remains a distinct method. Semaev's
[original summation-polynomial proposal](https://eprint.iacr.org/2004/031)
states its speed implication conditionally on an efficient solver for the
polynomial equations; the finite scan does not discharge that condition.

- **Frozen input and reference:** reuse the four `F_(p²)` public fixtures,
  `G,Q,r`, raw line and projected bases, random streams, caps and memory
  policy in [χ V1](chi_polynomial_20261015/RUN_SPEC_V1.json). The answer
  fixture is accessible only to independent replay. Keep projected-pair,
  χ finite-scan, χ orbit index, source/isogeny/scaling transport,
  negation rho A/A and six-class rho controls.

  **Custody correction to the frozen wording:** the V3 native redaction
  process read the private fixture to produce the public one. The solver
  process received only the public fixture; the independent checker read
  both. See the [versioned correction](chi_polynomial_20261015/PUBLIC_ONLY_PROTOCOL_V3_CORRECTION.md).
- **New arms:** (1) native direct `S3(x_1,x_2,x_R)` line-resultant and
  root finder over the frozen field, with exceptional vertical, tangent
  and infinity cases; (2) direct quotient-invariant polynomial in
  `x(φ(P))` on the same projected subgroup, charging degree-three map,
  polynomial construction and roots. Implement native Rust algorithms
  and independent checking formulas. A finite enumeration wrapped in a
  polynomial name is only an enumerative control and must be labeled so.
- **Exactness gate first:** on the smallest fixture, enumerate all signed
  base pairs independently. For every reachable target, compare the
  returned decomposition *set* (including duplicates and signs) with that
  enumeration. Every polynomial root must pass exact source or destination
  addition and subgroup/cofactor checks. Missing or false decompositions
  fail closed; preserve raw cases and stderr.
- **Paired solve gate:** use identical four targets, rounds, base size and
  trial cap across arms, and recover every available scalar via full
  relation collection, rank, linear algebra, target descent and separate
  `[k]G=Q` replay. Charge unsuccessful polynomial calls, root factoring,
  all map/correction work, setup, memory and final verification. Stop each
  arm at the inherited `10^6` query and `10^7` polynomial-operation caps;
  record exhaustion as censored, not zero yield.
- **Decision:** an exactness failure returns to formulas; equal relation
  outcomes with higher full cost rejects this concrete implementation;
  better relation yield earns a new complete-cost run, not a speed claim.
  Even a local full-cost win needs portable counted `S`, isolated
  interleaving and an independent-host replay before a frontier update.

## Gate B: does the full rational image change relation geometry?

**Question.** The [different-j map](different_j_20261015/reports/run-20261015T1713Z/REPORT.md)
has 4575 rational source points and 1525 image points, exactly three
source preimages per image. Its 61 target-subgroup images are distinct;
the paired projected relation controls had identical relation counts.

- **Frozen input:** keep `F_(67²)`, source `j=7`, destination `j=11`,
  degree-three Vélu kernel, public `G,Q`, order 61, round seeds and cap
  1220 in [V1](different_j_20261015/RUN_SPEC_V1.json). Freeze a new
  destination factor-base construction before looking at relation yield:
  select from the full 1525-point rational image by a deterministic
  invariant or line, with an independently enumerated census, and keep
  size nine equal to the projected control. Charge image enumeration,
  selection, rank and lookups once per cold target.
- **Controls:** source projected, isogeny-image projected, full-image
  selected, destination-direct with its own selection, and matched
  negation rho with A/A. Transport the same source target to every image
  arm. Confirm its 61 images and all map checks from the prior audit before
  relation collection.
- **Decision:** a larger relation count at equal trials but higher total
  cost remains a stage effect; a lower cold total on this fixed instance
  motivates the same design on increasing different-j pairs and an
  isolated counted-unit comparison. Equal support after the new selection
  narrows the coordinate mechanism. A bounded miss is not a theorem about
  all factor bases.

## Gate C: portable pairing cost and target-count scaling

**Question.** The [V6B run](pairing_batch_20261015/reports/run-20261015T1752Z/REPORT.md)
reused H and a finite-field BSGS table across 64 of 66 distinct targets.
Direct prime-field rho was faster in three post-test L0 rounds; pairing
was faster than the extension-field rho implementation. Establish how
these costs scale in a common unit.

- **Frozen comparison:** repeat the order-67 cell at prefixes
  `N=1,4,16,64,66`, with the same public target ordering and exact finite
  field construction, and measure a native counted conversion that includes
  extension multiplications, squares, inversions, base-field source/rho
  operations, H search failures, Miller/final exponentiation, BSGS setup,
  each multiplicative DLP, verification, memory and process overhead.
  Keep both direct `F_401` rho and extension arithmetic rho, but use the
  former as the applicable natural reference. Report cold and genuine
  multi-target amortized totals separately.
- **Isolation and replay:** interleave arms under an L2 or better reserved
  core on a second host, retain A/A noise, source/binary hashes, exact
  input lengths, stdout, stderr and RSS, and independently replay every
  scalar. Freeze a second prime subgroup order with at least 65 distinct
  targets and a certified pairing field before extending the size trend;
  no size is inferred from the original order-19 or order-61 groups.
- **Decision:** if pairing stays above direct rho in complete counted
  `S` across growing orders, record the measured limit of this design. If
  it falls below direct rho at a size, audit every primitive conversion,
  replay and A/A interval before calling it a crossover. A table-setup
  reuse benefit without a lower whole-method total remains an amortization
  diagnostic.

The concrete `F_(67²)` curve/Vélu bridge in
[the Lean proof map](different_j_20261015/PROOF_MAP.md) remains a separate
formalization obligation. The conditional order-61 injectivity theorem
does not itself prove the concrete map satisfies its kernel premise.

## 2026-10-15 V2 direct-root and V7B execution note

The native direct source `S3` quadratic-root and mapped quotient-root
controls passed [22/22 exhaustive target sets](chi_polynomial_20261015/reports/run-20261015T1827Z/REPORT.md).
Each then recovered the scalar in [12/12 paired relation rounds](chi_polynomial_20261015/reports/run-20261015T1828Z/REPORT.md)
at exactly the projected-pair control's relation counts, useful rank and
trials. The 11-arm independent checker replayed 123/132 scalar rows;
the nine other rows were raw-pair cap exhaustions. Direct source-root
paired CPU ratios to negation rho were 15.474, 6.014, 14.669 and 30.631
over the four cells. This executes Gate A's direct abscissa-root and
quotient portions only. A direct resultant in the χ coordinate, with
separate polynomial construction/factorization costs and exactness,
remains an unexecuted route.

The [post-suite V7B run](pairing_batch_20261015/reports/run-20261015T1822Z/REPORT.md)
repeated the exact V7 public targets and source/binary hashes after the
controlled release suite exited. At 64 distinct targets, direct source
BSGS, direct prime-field rho and pairing had median complete CPU
0.378, 0.938 and 1.219 ms; 594/594 scalar outputs replayed. Direct
source BSGS was lower at every prefix in every round. Gate C now needs
portable counted pricing, isolation, second-host replay and a second
larger-order cell against both direct BSGS and rho. The V7 and V7B
shared-host timing difference is not an isolated effect estimate.
