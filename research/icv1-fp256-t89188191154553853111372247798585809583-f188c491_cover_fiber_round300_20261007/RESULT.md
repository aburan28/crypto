# P-256 cover-fibre quotient screen, round 300: result

## Verdict

Cover-sheet multiplicity does not create additional P-256 relation equations
in the registered family.

The complete toy control gives the most favourable possible demonstration of
the issue.  The multiplication map `[8]` has eight rational sheets in the
cofactor-eight ambient group, so every signed three-term destination row has
`8^3=512` distinct lifted rows.  In the strongest cells the maximum raw
lifted-column rank rises from 6 to 48, an apparent gain of 42.  After
quotienting the exact 42-dimensional sheet-collapse kernel, the rank is still
6: precisely the rank of the collapsed destination rows.  Coordinate-defined
bases similarly rise from rank 5 to 47 and return to rank 5.  The maximum
quotient-rank gain over all 16 complete cells is **zero**.

This is not just a toy observation.  For the sheet-collapse map `C` and
`K=ker(C)`, linear algebra gives

```text
dim((span(R)+K)/K) = rank(C(R)).
```

Rows differing only by fibre choices differ inside `K`; algebraic degree and
raw lift count therefore cannot be booked as independent destination
relations.  Moreover, P-256 has cofactor one and prime rational group order:
`[k]` is a bijection on `E(F_p)` whenever `gcd(k,n)=1`, even though the
algebraic map has degree `k^2`.

This closes multiplication-map and pure deck-kernel fibre multiplicity within
the stated boundary.  It does not reject a cover or Jacobian algorithm that
makes genuinely distinct collapsed decompositions cheaper to find.  No such
P-256 construction, degree-at-most-five system, relation yield, inverse
recovery, or complete cost was supplied.  Promotion remains negative and no
unplanted P-256 relation was attempted.

## Exact quotient certificate

Let the destination factor base have columns `j=1..B`, and let each column
have rational lifts indexed by `s`.  Define

```text
C(e_(j,s)) = e_j.
```

The kernel is generated by `e_(j,s)-e_(j,0)` and has dimension
`B(r-1)` for a uniform `r`-sheet fibre.  The first isomorphism theorem for
vector spaces gives

```text
(span(R)+ker(C))/ker(C) ~= C(span(R)),
```

so its dimension is exactly `rank(C(R))`.  This holds over any coefficient
field.  Round 300 checks it independently over `F_149` for both coefficient
rows and same-target homogeneous differences in every nonempty bucket.

For a group pushforward `phi`, two lifted divisors or rows with the same
collapsed destination row differ by an element of the coefficient kernel;
their geometric difference pushes to zero.  Extra sheet coordinates may be
useful for solving a source-side problem, but they are not extra equations in
the destination factor-base logarithms.

The certificate does not say that every cover is useless.  A useful candidate
must improve the construction or search for **distinct collapsed rows**, then
provide the explicit fields, formulas, subgroup map, exceptional set, inverse
recovery, relation pullback, and paired complete cost.
