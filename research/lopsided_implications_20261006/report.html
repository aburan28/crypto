Method implications [proposed]

Proposal: identity claims ship a checker-replayable certificate beside the claim. Next action: follow-up PR writes the format into the study template.

  3. Tiny-exponent gains do not move our verdicts by design -- end-to-end S vs rho is the filter. Confirmatory.
  4. Watch the general regime (eps below 0.1204, paper Sec. 4). Kept open from round 2.
