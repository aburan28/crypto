# Verification diagram review

Both `generation.svg` and `final_verification.svg` were rendered with installed
Inkscape at 150 dpi and visually inspected after the timed campaign released
its reservation. Corresponding PNG copies are retained alongside their SVGs.
The final drawings have legible labels, no text outside their boxes, visible
arrow directions, exact curve/subgroup context and source/scope captions.
Initial long labels and connector arrow placement were corrected before saving
these final SVG/PNG copies.

The first diagram copies native generator/replay counts: 609 prior signed-orbit
keys, eight draws, zero rejections, three training and five holdout points.
The second copies native answer/provenance counts: 139 verified answers in
10/18/81/30 stages, zero invalid answers, nine exported-semantic pair matches,
2,439 hash comparisons, 455 distinct recorded-bound files and 1,036 final
snapshots. The diagrams compute no research results and make no speed claim.

The main report's PDF is the designated deliverable for generation/sampling and
verification coverage; the standalone Markdown reports and these diagrams are
its cited evidence. Its final rendering and review belong to the report handoff.
No additional verifier-only PDF is required by this handoff. Missing CairoSVG
was bypassed using the installed native Inkscape renderer. Its read-only profile
warnings did not affect the successful render or the inspected output.
