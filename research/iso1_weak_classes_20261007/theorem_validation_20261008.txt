Mathematical statements
1. Odd q = 1 (mod 4), odd n >= 3: norm-one lambda is a fourth power,
   its rational 2-isogeny target has full rational 4-torsion, and
   t = +/-(q^n+1) (mod 16). Ordinary implies 4 divides f_pi.
2. Any ordinary Q = 1 (mod 4): the trace congruence is equivalent to
   4 dividing the Frobenius-order conductor, by quadratic integrality.
3. Cubic q=p^2, p>3: absolute Frobenius and inversion need exactly
   (p^4+3p^2+8)/12 point counts with weights 2, 6, 12.
4. Exact p7 counterexample: t=+/-610, D_K=-19, f_pi=72, v2(f_pi)=3,
   zero norm-one weak representatives. An independent full-2 curve of
   trace 610 exists. The conductor converse is therefore false.
