The serial scheduler was stopped with SIGSTOP while its current child completed normally. No measured child was stopped. The baseline native collection ran alone on CPU 0. Artifact timestamps indicate a possible overlap of roughly 0.1 seconds between baseline startup and the preceding Rust wrapper verification/archive tail; the primary native collector had already exited. This is retained as a full-process timing deviation, not an isolated-host receipt. The scheduler resumes afterwards; the original four attempts remain part of the frozen crossover panel, without rerunning or dropping them.

These are one pair of production-size trials; they do not establish a population overhead estimate.
